**What was too slow and got replaced**

* **int64 NTT was the killer.** Each butterfly pass does ~8 array ops on n/2 elements, two of which are `%` on int64 (hardware div, ~10 ns/elem). For `(BW)^N`, N=2·10⁵ (K=N=2·10⁵, β=ω=0..K−1), the top-level convolution alone is n=2¹⁹ → 3 transforms × 19 passes × ~10 ns × 5·10⁵ ≈ 0.3 s, and *every* CDQ level has the same total transform length (≈1.5K), so ~11 levels ⇒ 3–5 s of NTT only. Replaced by **numpy `rfft`/`irfft` float convolution**:
  * `min(la,lb) ≤ 8192`: two 15-bit limbs, 4 forward + 3 inverse transforms. Worst-case coefficient ≤ `min(la,lb)·2³⁰ ≤ 2⁴³`, so absolute FFT error ≈ 2⁴³·2⁻⁵³·O(log n) ≪ 0.5 — rounding is exact; also ≪ 2⁵³ so `rint` is well defined.
