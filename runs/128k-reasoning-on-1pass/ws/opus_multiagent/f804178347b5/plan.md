Model the process as a complete ternary tree of depth N whose leaves are the characters of A and whose internal nodes hold the majority of their three children. For each node compute two things bottom-up: its current value `v`, and `cost[node]` = the minimum number of leaf flips needed to flip that node's value. A leaf costs 1. For an internal node with value `v`, look at the children equal to `v`: if all three equal `v`, the cost is the sum of the two smallest child costs; if exactly two equal `v`, the cost is the minimum of those two children's costs (the third child already opposes). The answer is `cost[root]`. Implement it iteratively level by level (no recursion) to handle 3^13 ≈ 1.6M leaves efficiently in Python.
