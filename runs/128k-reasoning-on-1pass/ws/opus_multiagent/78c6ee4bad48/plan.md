Model the state by the gap array `d_j = X_{j+1} - X_j` (j = 1..N-1). Reflecting pieces i+1, i+2 about the midpoint of pieces i and i+3 leaves the multiset of positions' gaps as (d_i, d_{i+1}, d_{i+2}) → (d_{i+2}, d_{i+1}, d_i), i.e. it swaps two gaps whose indices differ by 2; hence any permutation of odd-indexed gaps among themselves and of even-indexed gaps among themselves is reachable (and nothing else), while X_1 is invariant. Since sum of coordinates = N·X_1 + Σ_j (N − j)·d_j, we minimize by a rearrangement-inequality pairing: within each parity class, assign the largest gaps to the smallest weights (N − j). Implementation: split gaps by index parity, sort each ascending, pair with that class's weights taken in descending order, and sum. Everything is O(N log N) with Python big integers (values up to ~10^12 · 2·10^5, so use exact ints and fast I/O).
