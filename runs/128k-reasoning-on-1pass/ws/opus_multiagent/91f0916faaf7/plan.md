Work prime by prime: if $e_i=v_p(S_i)$ then $f(S_i/S_{i+1})$ has $p$-exponent $|e_i-e_{i+1}|$, so the condition is exactly $|e_i-e_{i+1}|=v_p(A_i)$ for every prime $p$, and $\gcd=1$ means $\min_i e_i=0$ for every $p$. Since primes are independent and the score is $\prod_p p^{\sum_i e_i}$, the answer is $\prod_p T_p$ where $T_p=\sum_{\text{valid }e} p^{\sum_i e_i}$ (primes dividing no $A_i$ give $T_p=1$). For a fixed $p$ with steps $a_i=v_p(A_i)$, a sequence is a sign choice $d_i=\pm a_i$; with prefix sums $c_j$ ($c_1=0$) we have $e_j=c_j-\min c$, so we DP over positions keeping the state $r=c_j-\min_{t\le j}c_t\ge 0$ and the accumulated weight $p^{W}$, $W=\sum_{t\le j}(c_t-\min_{t\le j}c_t)$; on a "+" or a non-record "–" step $W{+}{=}r'$, and when a new minimum is hit by $\delta$ at position $j$, $r'=0$ and $W{+}{=}j\delta$. State size is $\sum_i a_i+1$, and $\sum_p\sum_i v_p(A_i)\le 9\cdot(N-1)$, so the total work is small. Implement the per-step transition with numpy (or with a "twist" trick to skip runs of $a_i=0$ in O(1)) and multiply all $T_p$ modulo 998244353.
