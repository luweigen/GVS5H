Key insight: any alkane subgraph is determined by its set S of degree‑4 (internal) vertices, which must form a connected subtree of T with every vertex satisfying deg_T(v) ≥ 4 and having at most 4 chosen neighbors (deg_S(v) ≤ 4); the remaining vertices are leaves hung on S, giving total size |S| + Σ(4 − deg_S(v)) = 3|S| + 2. So the task reduces to: in the induced forest G on vertices with deg_T ≥ 4, find the maximum-size connected subtree in which every vertex has at most 4 subtree-neighbors; answer = 3·maxS + 2, or −1 if no vertex has degree ≥ 4. Solve with a rooted tree DP on each component of G: dp3[v] = 1 + (sum of 3 largest dp3 of children) (leaves room for the parent edge), and the candidate answer at v (topmost vertex of S) is 1 + (sum of 4 largest dp3 of children).
