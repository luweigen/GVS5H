The problem asks for the minimum cost to transform sequence $A$ into $B$ by flipping bits. Each flip operation incurs a cost equal to the current weighted sum of $A$. We must flip all indices where $A_i \neq B_i$ exactly once. Indices where $A_i = B_i$ should not be flipped as it only increases the total cost. Let $P$ be the set of indices where $A_i=1, B_i=0$ (flipping reduces the sum) and $Z$ be the set where $A_i=0, B_i=1$ (flipping increases the sum). To minimize the total cost, we should perform all operations in $P$ before operations in $Z$, because reducing the sum early lowers the cost of subsequent operations. Within $P$, we should flip indices with larger $C_i$ first to maximize the reduction in the sum for subsequent operations. Within $Z$, we should flip indices with smaller $C_i$ first to minimize the increase in the sum for subsequent operations. The total cost can be calculated using the initial sum and the sorted lists of costs.
