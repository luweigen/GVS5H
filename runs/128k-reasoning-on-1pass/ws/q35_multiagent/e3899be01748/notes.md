
## ideation
The core difficulty lies in efficiently checking the divisibility condition for all substrings. A naive $O(N^2)$ approach is infeasible for $N=10^5$. The key observation is that for any fixed divisor $d \in \{1..9\}$, the condition $V[i..j] \equiv 0 \pmod d$ can be transformed using prefix values $P[k]$ into $P[j+1] \equiv P[i] \cdot 10^{j-i+1} \pmod d$. Since $10^k \pmod d$ becomes periodic with a very small period (at most 6) for all $d \le 9$, we can group prefix indices by their position modulo 6. This allows us to maintain frequency counts of prefix remainders and answer queries in $O(1)$ amortized time. We explicitly handle substrings of length $<6$ to avoid period boundary edge cases, and use the grouped counts for lengths $\ge 6$. This yields an overall $O(N)$ time complexity with a small constant factor.
