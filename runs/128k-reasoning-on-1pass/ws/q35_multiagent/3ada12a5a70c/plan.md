Map each point on the square's boundary to its clockwise distance from `(0,0)` along the perimeter. This transforms the 2D problem into selecting `k` points on a circle of circumference `4 * side` to maximize the minimum circular distance. Use binary search on the candidate distance `D`. For each `D`, verify feasibility by greedily selecting points: iterate through each point as a potential starting point, then repeatedly pick the next point at least `D` distance away clockwise using binary search (`bisect`). If `k` points are successfully selected and the wrap-around gap back to the start is also `>= D`, then `D` is feasible. The maximum feasible `D` is the answer.
