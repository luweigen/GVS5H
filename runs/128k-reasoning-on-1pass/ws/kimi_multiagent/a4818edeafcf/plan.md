Compute prefix distinct counts `P[i]` and suffix distinct counts `S[j+1]` in linear time. The remaining challenge is maximizing `P[i] + distinct(A_{i+1..j})` over `i < j`; maintain this with a segment tree keyed by cut position `i`. When the right cut advances to `j`, only cuts `i` after the previous occurrence of `A_j` gain one new distinct middle value, so apply a range increment and query the best `i < j`. Combine with `S[j+1]` for every `j = 2..N-1` and take the maximum. This gives an `O(N log N)` solution suitable for `N ≤ 3e5`.
