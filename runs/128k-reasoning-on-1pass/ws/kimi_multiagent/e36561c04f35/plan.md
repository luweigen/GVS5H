Model a full strategy as choosing the final deletion order of the multiset: adjacent swaps pay the Kendall/inversion distance from A to that order, and deletions pay the number of maximal equal-value runs in it. Prove by exchange that same-value occurrences keep their relative order and that only local run-reducing swaps of the form x y x y -> x x y y need to be considered. Then compress A into run-length form and compute the maximum net reduction in runs with a stack/DP over runs, where four consecutive runs x,y,x,y with singleton middle runs can collapse. Answer is initial runs minus the maximum profitable collapses, implemented in total O(N log N) or O(N).
