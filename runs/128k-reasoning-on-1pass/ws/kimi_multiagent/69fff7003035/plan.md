Use linearity of concatenation: fix a value `v`; for every subset `R` of numbers placed to its right, there are exactly `(N-1)!` permutations with that right-set, and `v` is scaled by `10^{sum digits in R}`. Summing over all subsets gives `v * (N-1)! * ∏_{u≠v}(1+10^{digits(u)})`. Factor the full product `Q=∏_{u=1..N}(1+10^{digits(u)})`, so each `v` contributes `v*Q*inverse(1+10^{digits(v)})`. Since `N≤2e5`, digit lengths are only 1..6, so group values by digit length and compute counts/sums per length. The final answer is `(N-1)! * Q * Σ_len sumV[len] * inv(1+10^len) mod 998244353`.
