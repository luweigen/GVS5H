Model Takahashi as an expanding connected region: absorbing a cell adds that cell to the region and makes its unabsorbed neighbors part of the frontier. Because strength only increases, any absorbable frontier cell remains absorbable later, so repeatedly absorbing the weakest available frontier slime is optimal. Use a min-heap keyed by slime strength; if the heap minimum is not strictly below `cur/X`, no frontier slime is absorbable and the process stops. Use exact integer arithmetic, e.g. absorb iff `S <= (cur-1)//X`, to avoid floating error and overflow. The final answer is the accumulated strength after the closure terminates in `O(HW log HW)`.
