Treat each index `i` as the middle of the 5-subsequence and maintain frequency counters for the left and right sides as we sweep. For the middle value `v`, valid selections split into: exactly one extra `v` among the four side picks, requiring the three non-`v` picks to be pairwise distinct; or at least two extra `v` picks, where the remaining side elements are unrestricted. Precompute binomial coefficients up to `n`, and use aggregate sums (`count`, `sum of squares`) on each side to evaluate the distinct-non-`v` cases in O(1) per middle. Update counters and square sums while moving `i` from left to right, accumulating answers modulo `1e9+7`. This gives an O(n) sweep after O(n) precomputation, easily fast enough for `n <= 1000`.
