Factor N into prime powers p^e. For each odd p^e, use a=1+p modulo m=p^(e+1), whose multiplicative order is exactly p^e by LTE/binomial expansion; for p=2 use a=3 modulo 4 when e=1 and modulo 2^(e+2) when e≥2. Each local modulus is at most (p^e)^2, so the product M is at most N^2≤1e18. Combine the pairwise-coprime local congruences with CRT to get one A modulo M; then A^n≡1 mod M iff every local order divides n, so the least n is lcm(p^e)=N. Handle N=1 separately as (1,1).
