Model deletions as removing all occurrences of one value once they have been brought together at the front; adjacent swaps pay for inversions between elements whose deletion order differs from their original order. The total cost is therefore the number of distinct values (one deletion per value) plus the minimum inversion-like cost over an ordering of values. Derive a dynamic programming formulation based on each value’s first and last occurrence, then optimize it to meet the total \(N\) constraint. Validate the characterization against edge cases such as all equal, all distinct, and alternating values.
