Model each floor level as inducing connected components of blocks whose buildings reach that level; movement by walkways is free within such a component. For a query, the optimal route can be characterized by the highest floor at which the two relevant positions can be connected, together with unavoidable vertical movement from the endpoints. Build a maximum spanning tree over blocks, where adjacent blocks have edge weight equal to the minimum of their heights; the maximum feasible common walkway level between two blocks is the minimum edge weight on their tree path. Use binary lifting with minimum-edge aggregation to answer this bottleneck value per query, then derive the minimum stair count from it and the endpoint floors.
