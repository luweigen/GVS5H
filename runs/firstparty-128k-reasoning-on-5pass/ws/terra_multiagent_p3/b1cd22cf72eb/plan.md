Transform the goal into choosing final upper lengths \(u_i'\) and a common total height \(H\), where \(0 \le u_i' \le U_i\), \(0 \le H-u_i' \le D_i\), and adjacent upper lengths differ by at most \(X\). Since every retained tooth length saves one unit of grinding cost, maximize the total retained length \(N \cdot H\) subject to these constraints. For a fixed \(H\), each upper tooth has an allowed interval \([H-D_i, U_i]\), and feasibility with the adjacent difference condition can be checked greedily by propagating feasible intervals. Determine the largest feasible \(H\) by binary search, then compute the answer as the original total tooth length minus \(N \cdot H\).
