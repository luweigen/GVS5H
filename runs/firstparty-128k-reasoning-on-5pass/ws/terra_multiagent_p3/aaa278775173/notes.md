- **Clockwise mapping:** In row-down screen coordinates, `NW=(-1,-1) -> NE=(-1,1) -> SE=(1,1) -> SW=(1,-1) -> NW` is clockwise. Thus if `q` is the post-turn direction, the first-leg direction is `directions[(q_idx - 1) % 4]`. This agrees with sample 1: `SE -> SW`, and sample 2: `SW -> NW`.
- **First-leg DP:** `first[r][c]` stores the maximum valid segment ending at `(r,c)` in direction `d`, beginning with `1` and alternating thereafter as `2,0,2,...`. A predecessor length of odd parity requires the current cell to be `2`; even parity requires `0`.
- **Suffix DP:** `run0` and `run2` store maximum alternating suffix lengths moving in post-turn direction `q`, when the current cell is required to be `0` or `2`. The next cell must require the opposite value.
