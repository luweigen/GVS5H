Binary search the answer `x`, interpreted as a target minimum score that every index must reach. For a fixed `x`, each position `i` requires at least `ceil(x / points[i])` visits, and we can greedily simulate the minimum moves needed while traversing the line from left to right. Account carefully for the fact that revisiting a position generally costs two moves, while the final position may not require returning. The largest feasible target under `m` moves is the answer.
