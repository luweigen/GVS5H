Root the tree and use a bottom-up DFS to determine whether each subtree can be partitioned into complete length-\(K\) paths, except possibly for one unfinished chain that must connect upward through the parent. For every node, merge unfinished chains returned by its children together with the node itself; at most one chain may remain unfinished after forming complete paths. A chain becomes complete exactly when its vertex count reaches \(K\), and any invalid number or combination of unfinished chains causes failure. Handle \(K=1\) separately, since every vertex alone forms a valid path.
