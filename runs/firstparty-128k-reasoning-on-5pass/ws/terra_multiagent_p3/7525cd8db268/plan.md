Normalize each conflicting pair as `(min(a,b), max(a,b))`, since a subarray contains both exactly when its left boundary is at most the smaller endpoint and its right boundary reaches the larger endpoint. Sweep the right endpoint of subarrays from `1` to `n`, maintaining the largest forbidden left endpoint imposed by all pairs ending at or before the current right endpoint. This gives the baseline number of valid subarrays with all pairs present. To evaluate removing one pair efficiently, track how much each pair can reduce the active maximum left restriction during the sweep; the pair with the greatest accumulated improvement yields the answer.
