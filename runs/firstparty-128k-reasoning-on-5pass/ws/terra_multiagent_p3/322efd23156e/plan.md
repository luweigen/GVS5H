Exploit that foods of different vitamin types are independent except for the shared calorie budget. For each vitamin, compute a 0/1 knapsack DP where `dp[c]` is the maximum intake obtainable using exactly/within `c` calories. Then binary search the target minimum vitamin intake `m`: for each vitamin, find the minimum calories needed to reach at least `m`, and check whether the sum for all three vitamins is at most `X`. The answer is the greatest feasible `m`.
