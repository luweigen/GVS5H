Model each purchased unit as having an increasing marginal cost for its product: buying the \(k\)-th unit of product \(i\) costs \((2k-1)P_i\) yen additionally. The optimal strategy is to buy the globally cheapest marginal-cost units while their total cost stays within \(M\). Binary search a threshold marginal price \(x\), count how many units have marginal cost at most \(x\), and compute their total cost. Then use remaining budget to buy additional units whose next marginal cost equals the threshold boundary.
