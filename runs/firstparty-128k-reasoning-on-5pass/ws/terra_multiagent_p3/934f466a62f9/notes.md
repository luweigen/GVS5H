- **Reduction to parity assignment:** Charge every original pair to one coordinate attaining its maximum. A charged pair contributes the two cakes' values in that coordinate. Thus, equivalently, select exactly `2K` cakes, assign every selected cake one of three coordinate labels, require every label count to be even, and maximize the sum of assigned coordinate values. Cakes of each label can then be paired arbitrarily. This is exact in both directions.

- **Top `2K+2` fixed-candidate claim is false:** Restricting all selected cakes to the top `2K+2` cakes by `max(X,Y,Z)` is not valid. Example with `K=2`: two cakes `(H,0,0)`, `(0,H,0)`, four cakes `(0,0,Q)`, and outside cakes `(Q-1,0,0)`, `(0,Q-1,0)`, for very large `H` and `Q>1`. The top six are the first six cakes, but their best parity-feasible value is at most `max(4Q,2H)`. Selecting the two high cakes and the two outside cakes gives `2H+2Q-2`, which can be strictly larger. Therefore all cakes outside a top prefix must remain available.

- **Correct exchange bound:** Let `B` be the top `2K` cakes sorted by `M_i=max(X_i,Y_i,Z_i)`. There exists an optimum using at most two cakes outside `B`. Suppose an optimum uses `q>=3` outside cakes, hence omits `q` cakes of `B`. Match omitted baseline cakes with selected outside cakes arbitrarily. Replace an outside cake by its matched baseline cake assigned to a coordinate attaining its `M_i`; this never decreases value because every baseline `M_i` is at least every outside `M_i`. Each replacement changes label parity by `e_old XOR e_best`, which is either zero or one of the three nonzero even-weight masks. These masks form a group of size four. Among any three such masks, some nonempty subset has xor zero: either a mask repeats, a zero occurs, or the three distinct nonzero masks xor to zero. Perform the corresponding subset of replacements, preserving all-even parity and not decreasing value. Repeating yields `q<=2`.

- **DP:** Split cakes into baseline `B` of size `2K` and all remaining cakes. For baseline cakes, DP over `(omitted in 0..2, parity mask in 0..7)`; each cake is omitted or assigned one of three labels. For outside cakes, DP over `(taken in 0..2, parity mask)`, with skip or assignment transitions. Combine states with equal omitted/taken counts and equal masks, since xor of equal masks is zero. The DP itself is `O(N * 3 * 8 * 3)` time and constant-sized state; sorting to obtain `B` costs `O(N log N)`.

- **Parity mask:** Bit `d` is toggled whenever a cake is assigned coordinate `d`. Final xor zero means every coordinate label has even count.
