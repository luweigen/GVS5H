Track the cumulative wind displacement after each time step. Smoke generated at the origin at some earlier time remains at the current cumulative displacement minus the displacement at its generation time, so `(R,C)` contains smoke exactly when a corresponding shifted prefix-displacement has appeared before. Maintain a set of all cumulative displacements at times when smoke may originate, and test membership in O(1) per step. This yields O(N) time and O(N) memory.
