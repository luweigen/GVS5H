- **Perimeter reduction:** Map each boundary point to one clockwise coordinate in `[0, 4*side)`: bottom `x`, right `side+y`, top `3*side-x`, left `4*side-y`. Priority gives unique corner ownership: `(0,0)=0`, `(side,0)=side`, `(side,side)=2*side`, `(0,side)=3*side`.
- **Distance equivalence:** The answer is at most `side`, since among at least four cyclic perimeter gaps one is at most `side`, and the Manhattan distance of that consecutive pair is no larger. For tested `d <= side`, two boundary points have Manhattan distance `< d` exactly when their shortest cyclic perimeter separation is `< d`.
- **Circular spacing:** A selected set is valid iff every cyclic consecutive perimeter gap is at least `d`. If all selected consecutive gaps meet this condition, every route between two selected points contains at least one such gap, so no pair has cyclic separation below `d`.
- **Feasibility:** Duplicate sorted perimeter coordinates. For each index, compute `nxt[i]`, the earliest later coordinate at least `d` ahead. Fix every original point as first selection and greedily take `k-1` `nxt` jumps. Greedy produces the earliest possible final point, maximizing room for the closing gap. Accept when final position is at most `start + perimeter - d`.
- **Wraparound:** The final inequality enforces the last-to-first cyclic gap and prevents acceptance of selections that only work on the unwrapped line. A jump beyond the doubled array is rejected.
- **Complexity:** Each feasibility check is `O(n*k)` time and `O(n)` memory. Overall complexity is `O(n*k*log(side))`, with `n <= 15000` and `k <= 25`.
- **Test results:** Sample 1 (`side=2`, four corners, `k=4`) passes with result `2`. Sample 2 passes with result `1`. Sample 3 passes with result `1`.
- **Targeted tests:** Four corners with `side=1`, `k=4` gives `1` (pass). Four corners with `side=10`, `k=4` gives `10` (pass). Adding a midpoint `(5,0)` to the four `side=10` corners with `k=4` still gives `10` (pass; greedy can skip it). Eight perimeter points spaced every `5` units around a `side=10` square with `k=4` gives `10` (pass). These cover exact corner ownership, same-side points, skipping close points, and final wraparound spacing.
