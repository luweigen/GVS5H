Use the identity that selecting \(K\) elements is equivalent to excluding \(N-K\) elements: if `total_xor` is the XOR of all values, then XOR of selected elements is `total_xor ^ xor(excluded)`. Enumerate combinations of size `R = min(K, N-K)`, since \(\binom{N}{R}=\binom{N}{K}\le 10^6\). During DFS/backtracking, maintain the XOR of the currently chosen small subset incrementally and update the maximum at each complete combination. If `R == K`, the candidate is the subset XOR; otherwise it is `total_xor ^ subset_xor`.
