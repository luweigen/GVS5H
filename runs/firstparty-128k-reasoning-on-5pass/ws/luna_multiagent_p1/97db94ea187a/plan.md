Partition vertices into BFS distance layers from vertex 1, where the parity of a vertex’s distance is determined by its layer index. For a fixed sequence of nonempty layer sizes, edges may exist only within one layer or between consecutive layers; every vertex outside the root layer must have at least one edge to the preceding layer. Use dynamic programming over layers, total vertices, the number of vertices in even layers, and the edge-count generating polynomial. For a transition from a previous layer of size \(a\) to a new layer of size \(b\), multiply by \((1+x)^{ab}\) restricted so every new vertex has a preceding neighbor, and by \((1+x)^{\binom b2}\) for internal edges; collect coefficients with exactly \(N/2\) even-layer vertices.
