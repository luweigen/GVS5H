Express the concatenated number as the sum of each element multiplied by a power of 10 determined by the total digit lengths of elements appearing after it. For a fixed value \(x\), choosing the subset of elements after \(x\) gives a contribution weighted by \(a!b!\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are the numbers before and after \(x\). Group numbers by their decimal digit length; since lengths are at most 6, construct the generating polynomial \(G(z)=\prod_d(1+10^d z)^{c_d}\) in \(O(ND)\) time using its logarithmic-derivative recurrence, where \(D\le 6\). For each digit-length group, divide \(G\) by \(1+10^d z\), compute the weighted coefficient sum using factorials, and multiply by the sum of values in that group.
