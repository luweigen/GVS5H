Use a recursive dynamic programming approach on the ternary tree induced by the repeated majority operations. For each subtree, compute the minimum leaf changes needed to make its resulting value either 0 or 1. At a leaf, the cost is 0 for keeping its bit and 1 for changing it; at an internal node, combine the three child costs by considering all choices of child output values and enforcing the parent’s majority value. The answer is the minimum cost for forcing the root to differ from its original computed value.
