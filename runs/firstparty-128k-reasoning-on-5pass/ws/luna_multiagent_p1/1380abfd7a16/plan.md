Process insertions in reverse order: when considering value \(i\), its final position among the currently unassigned slots is \(P_i\)-th from the left. Use a Fenwick tree initialized with all \(N\) positions available, supporting selection of the \(k\)-th available position in \(O(\log N)\). Place each \(i\) into that position, remove the position from the tree, and output the constructed array.
