Let the positions of all `1`s be \(p_0,p_1,\dots,p_{k-1}\). If the final contiguous block starts at position \(x\), the minimum swaps is \(\sum_i |p_i-(x+i)|\), minimized when \(x\) is the median of the adjusted positions \(p_i-i\). Compute these adjusted positions and their median, then sum absolute deviations from it. This yields an \(O(N)\)-time and \(O(N)\)-space solution suitable for \(N\le 5\times10^5\).
