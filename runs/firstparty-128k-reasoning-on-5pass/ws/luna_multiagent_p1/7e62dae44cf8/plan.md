Use a min-heap to simulate the set of currently adjacent slimes, since absorbing the smallest eligible adjacent slime is always optimal. Starting from Takahashi’s cell, repeatedly inspect the heap minimum; if its strength is strictly less than `current_strength / X`, absorb it, add its strength, and insert all newly adjacent unvisited cells. Once the minimum adjacent slime is not absorbable, no other adjacent slime can be absorbed either, so the process terminates. Track visited cells to ensure each slime is added and processed at most once, giving an `O(HW log(HW))` solution.
