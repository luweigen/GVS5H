Let \(P_k\) be the sum of \(A_i+A_j\) over all \(i\le j\) such that \(A_i+A_j\) is divisible by \(2^k\). Then pairs whose sum has exact 2-adic valuation \(k\) contribute \((P_k-P_{k+1})/2^k\), so the answer is the sum of these quantities over all relevant \(k\). For each modulus \(2^k\), group values by residue and combine residue \(r\) with \((-r)\bmod 2^k\), carefully counting unordered pairs and diagonal pairs. Computing these groups for \(O(\log \max A_i)\) moduli gives an \(O(N\log \max A_i)\) solution.
