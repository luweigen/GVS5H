Model the black cells as a Young diagram: each row has a black-prefix length \(R_x\), and the column-prefix condition requires these lengths to be nonincreasing, with column heights determined by the conjugate partition. Each precolored cell imposes that the diagram boundary lies southeast or northwest of its coordinate depending on whether it is black or white. Develop a sparse-coordinate algorithm that propagates these inequalities through the \(O(M)\) relevant rows and columns, then checks whether all constraints are mutually consistent. Finally, verify edge cases such as duplicate constraints on one row/column, empty/full rows, and \(N=1\).
