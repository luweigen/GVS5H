Use square-root decomposition on the prefix length of \(A\). Partition \(A\) into blocks and precompute, for every block and every prefix of \(B\), the sum of \(|A_i-B_j|\) over all \(A_i\) in that block and \(B_j\) in the prefix. For a query \((X,Y)\), combine the precomputed contributions of all complete \(A\)-blocks before \(X\), then directly process the at most one partial block. Choose a block size around \(\sqrt N\), giving roughly \(O(N^2/\sqrt N+K\sqrt N)\) time and \(O(N\sqrt N)\) memory, with 64-bit integer answers.
