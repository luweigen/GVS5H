Because each food contains only one vitamin, optimize the three vitamin groups independently. For each vitamin, use a 0/1 knapsack DP over calorie amounts, where `dp[c]` stores the maximum intake achievable with exactly `c` calories. For a candidate target `T`, find the minimum calorie cost needed to obtain at least `T` units of each vitamin and check whether their sum is at most `X`. Binary-search the largest feasible `T`; the total complexity is `O(NX + X log S)`, where `S` is the total vitamin amount.
