Represent the final Snowflake Tree by its center, its selected intermediate vertices, and the retained leaves of each intermediate. For a fixed center \(c\) and positive \(y\), an adjacent vertex \(v\) can serve as an intermediate exactly when \(\deg(v)-1\ge y\), so selecting all such neighbors maximizes the remaining size to \(1+(y+1)\cdot k\). For every vertex, sort the values \(\deg(v)-1\) over its neighbors and scan possible \(y\) values to maximize this expression; the total sorting cost is \(O(N\log N)\). The answer is \(N\) minus the largest achievable Snowflake Tree size.
