For any chosen set containing \(A_i\), its GCD must be a divisor of \(A_i\), and a divisor \(d\) is feasible exactly when at least \(K\) array elements are divisible by \(d\). Compute the frequency of every value, then use a sieve over multiples to obtain the number of elements divisible by each \(d\). Process \(d\) in descending order; whenever its divisible count is at least \(K\), assign \(d\) as the answer for every present multiple of \(d\) that has not yet been assigned. The first assignment is therefore the maximum feasible divisor for each value, and outputs are retrieved by value.
