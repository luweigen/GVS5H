Express each pair price as the maximum over the three coordinate choices, then view every pair as choosing one of three “colors” (beauty, tastiness, popularity) that attains its contribution. For fixed counts of pairs assigned to each coordinate, the optimal total can be obtained by selecting cakes through a small-dimensional dynamic programming/greedy characterization based on coordinate rankings. Derive a reduction showing that only a constant number of extreme candidates per coordinate need special handling, while the remaining selection is solved by sorting and DP. Implement the resulting algorithm in \(O(N \log N)\) or similar per test case, respecting the total \(N\) bound.
