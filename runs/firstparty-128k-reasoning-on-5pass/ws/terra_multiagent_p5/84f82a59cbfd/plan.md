A 400 number has the form \(p^{2a}q^{2b}=(p^a q^b)^2\), where \(p\neq q\) are primes and \(a,b\ge1\). Precompute every such number up to \(10^{12}\), sort them, then answer each query with binary search for the largest precomputed value not exceeding \(A\). Generate prime powers and combine pairs of distinct primes whose squared-power products remain within the limit. This supports \(2\times10^5\) queries efficiently.
