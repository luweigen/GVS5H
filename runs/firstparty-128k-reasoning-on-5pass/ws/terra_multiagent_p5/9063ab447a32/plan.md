Model each additional unit of product \(i\) as having marginal cost \((2k-1)P_i\) for the \(k\)-th unit. The optimal purchase consists of taking the globally cheapest marginal costs, subject to the budget. Binary search a marginal-cost threshold \(x\), compute how many units have marginal cost at most \(x\) and their total cost, then use remaining budget to buy additional units at the next relevant marginal cost. Use 64-bit-safe arithmetic and cap intermediate values to avoid overflow.
