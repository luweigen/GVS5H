For a value \(x\), its answer is the largest divisor \(d\) of \(x\) such that at least \(K\) array elements are divisible by \(d\). Count the frequency of every value, then for every divisor \(d\), compute how many elements are multiples of \(d\) using a sieve-like loop. Process divisors in descending order and assign \(d\) as the answer for every multiple whose answer is still unassigned whenever its multiple count is at least \(K\). This yields \(O(M \log M)\)-style harmonic complexity, where \(M=\max A_i\le 10^6\), and supports the large input size.
