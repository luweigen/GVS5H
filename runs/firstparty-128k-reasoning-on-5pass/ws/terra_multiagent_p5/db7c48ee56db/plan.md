Exploit the guarantee that \(\binom{N}{K}\le 10^6\), so all valid choices can be enumerated directly. Generate every combination of \(K\) indices (or values), compute its XOR incrementally via DFS/backtracking, and track the maximum result. To reduce recursion depth when \(K\) is large, use the complement: choosing \(K\) elements is equivalent to excluding \(N-K\) elements, and XORs can be recovered using the XOR of all values. Enumerate the smaller of \(K\) and \(N-K\), ensuring at most \(10^6\) leaves.
