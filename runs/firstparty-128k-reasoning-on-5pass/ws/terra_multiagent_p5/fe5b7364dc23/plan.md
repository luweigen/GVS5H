Rewrite each subarray sum as a difference of prefix sums: \((S_r-S_{l-1})^K\). Expand with the binomial theorem, so for every right endpoint \(r\), the contribution can be computed from maintained sums of powers of earlier prefix sums. Precompute binomial coefficients up to \(K\), scan prefix sums from left to right, and update power-sum accumulators modulo \(998244353\). This yields \(O(NK)\) time and \(O(K)\) memory.
