Model the valid coloring as a Young-diagram-like shape: if a cell is black, every cell above it and to its left must also be black. Thus each black constraint imposes requirements on row/column boundaries, while each white constraint imposes upper bounds. Compress only the rows and columns appearing in constraints, then verify that no black requirement conflicts with any white requirement implied by monotonicity. Use sorting and prefix/suffix extrema to perform all checks in \(O(M\log M)\).
