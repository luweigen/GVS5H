Model the game as a race to make the first move on every index, while players may spend extra moves decreasing already selected indices to control turn parity. Sort the values and derive the known winning condition based on the smallest value, its uniqueness, and parity of the remaining total decrement opportunities. The final algorithm should run in \(O(N)\) or \(O(N\log N)\), suitable for \(N=2\times10^5\). Carefully validate the condition against cases with tied minimum values and minimum value equal to one.
