Treat the \((N-1)!\) parent sequences as a uniform random recursive tree, so the required sum is \((N-1)!\) times the expected distance. For an edge \(i\) (\(2 \le i \le N\)), it contributes \(A_i\) exactly when exactly one of \(u,v\) lies in the subtree rooted at \(i\). For \(u<v\), the separation probabilities are: \(i<u\): \(\frac{2(i-1)}{i(i+1)}\), \(i=u\): \(\frac{u-1}{u}\), \(u<i<v\): \(\frac{1}{i}\), \(i=v\): \(1\), and \(i>v\): \(0\). Precompute modular prefix sums of \(A_i\) multiplied by the coefficient families \(\frac{2(i-1)}{i(i+1)}\) and \(\frac{1}{i}\) using modular inverses. Each query is then answered in \(O(1)\) by combining the relevant prefix ranges, the point terms at \(u\) and \(v\), and multiplying by \((N-1)!\) modulo \(998244353\).
