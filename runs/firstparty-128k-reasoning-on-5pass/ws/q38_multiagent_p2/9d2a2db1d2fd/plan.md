Model a walkway between adjacent blocks as an edge whose capacity is the smaller of the two building heights. For any fixed cell path, the minimum stair count depends only on the minimum capacity m on that path: it is |Y-Z| plus 2*max(0,min(Y,Z)-m), because every level above m up to min(Y,Z) must be descended below and climbed back above. Thus each query reduces to finding the maximum possible m over all paths between the two cells, i.e. the maximum bottleneck path value. This value is the height at which the two cells become connected in the superlevel sets of buildings, and can be obtained from a Kruskal reconstruction tree built by activating cells in decreasing F and unioning active neighbors. Answer queries by taking the weight of the LCA of the two cell leaves and applying the formula.
