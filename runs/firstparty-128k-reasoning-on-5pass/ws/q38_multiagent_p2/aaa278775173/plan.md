Treat the segment as a path whose required values are fixed by its length: 1, then 2,0,2,0,... Use DP over cells, last diagonal direction, and whether the single clockwise turn has been used. First compute the best no-turn segment ending at each cell in each direction by scanning each diagonal in the direction's topological order. Then compute the best one-turn segment ending at each cell in each direction by continuing a turned state or turning from the no-turn state in the counterclockwise direction at the previous cell. The answer is the maximum over all states, with a lone 1 giving length 1 and no 1 giving 0. Keep arrays compact because the maximum length is at most 500, giving O(nm) time and low memory.
