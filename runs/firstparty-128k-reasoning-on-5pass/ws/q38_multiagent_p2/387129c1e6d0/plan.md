We simulate the final position by accumulating the axis-aligned movement deltas. We count visited houses by separating horizontal and vertical traversals: group houses by their y-coordinate and x-coordinate, and group move segments by their fixed coordinate. For each horizontal line, sweep sorted x-intervals against sorted houses to mark houses on horizontal segments and count them. For each vertical line, sweep sorted y-intervals against sorted houses to count vertical houses and subtract those already marked horizontal to avoid double counting. This yields O((N+M) log(N+M)) time and O(N+M) memory, with 64-bit integers for coordinates.
