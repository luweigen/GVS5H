Represent the configuration by the leftmost coordinate and the gaps between consecutive pieces. The operation on four consecutive pieces reverses the three gaps between them, equivalently swapping the two outer gaps while leaving the middle gap unchanged. Thus operations are exactly adjacent swaps within the odd-indexed gaps and within the even-indexed gaps, so each parity class of gaps can be permuted arbitrarily. The total coordinate sum is \(N X_1\) plus a weighted sum of gaps, where the weight of gap \(t\) is \(N-t\), decreasing as \(t\) increases. To minimize the sum, independently sort each parity class of gaps in nondecreasing order and place them from left to right, pairing smaller gaps with larger weights. This gives an \(O(N \log N)\) solution.
