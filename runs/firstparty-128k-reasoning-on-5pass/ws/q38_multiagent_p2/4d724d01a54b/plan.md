Because swap costs increase with index, an optimal sequence can be characterized by a potential that is decreased by at most the swap position, and the leftmost-inversion (insertion sort) sequence attains the bound. Equivalently, scan the original permutation left to right, keeping the prefix sorted and inserting each new element by swapping it left across previous larger elements. For the element at original position i, if L_i previous elements are smaller than it, its swaps occur at positions L_i+1 through i-1, contributing sum_{j=L_i+1}^{i-1} j. Compute L_i online with a Fenwick tree over values 1..N. Accumulate the closed-form sums in 64-bit/Python integer arithmetic for O(N log N) time.
