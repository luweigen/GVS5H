Treat Takahashi as a growing connected region starting at cell (P,Q), with strength equal to the sum of all absorbed cells. A neighboring unabsorbed cell can be absorbed exactly when X * its_strength < current_strength, and absorbing positive strength only makes future absorptions easier. Use a min-heap of frontier cells keyed by strength, repeatedly absorbing the smallest absorbable frontier cell. If the smallest frontier cell is not absorbable, then no frontier cell is absorbable, so the process stops. The final current_strength is the maximum possible strength, using arbitrary precision or 128-bit arithmetic for products and sums.
