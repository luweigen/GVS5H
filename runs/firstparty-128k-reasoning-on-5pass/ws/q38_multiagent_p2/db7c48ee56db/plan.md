Use the fact that choosing K elements is equivalent to either choosing K directly or choosing N-K elements to omit, and the XOR of chosen elements is total_xor XOR XOR(omitted). Let m = min(K, N-K); the guarantee C(N,K) <= 10^6 implies C(N,m) <= 10^6, so m is small. Enumerate all m-subsets of the array and compute their XOR. If K <= N-K, the answer is the maximum subset XOR; if K > N-K, the answer is the maximum total_xor XOR subset_xor over omitted subsets. This gives an O(C(N,m) * m) algorithm, easily fast enough.
