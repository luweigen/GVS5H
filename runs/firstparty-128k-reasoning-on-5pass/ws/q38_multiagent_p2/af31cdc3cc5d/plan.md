Model each removed adjacent pair as a noncrossing matching on the original indices. For any even-length segment, the maximum score is the sum of its largest half minus the sum of its smallest half, because an optimal matching can be uncrossed and the top half can be paired with the bottom half noncrossingly. If N is even, the whole array is one such segment, so the answer is that half-difference. If N is odd, the final remaining element at 0-index r must have even r, and no pair can cross it, so the score is H(prefix r)+H(suffix r+1) for some even r. Therefore compute H for all even-length prefixes and suffixes by scanning from both ends with two heaps maintaining the lower and upper halves. For odd N take the maximum over even r of pref[r]+suff[r+1], and for even N output pref[N].
