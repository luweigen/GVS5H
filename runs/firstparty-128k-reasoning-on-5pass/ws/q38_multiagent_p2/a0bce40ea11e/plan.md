Work over \(F_p\) and expand each entry of \(B^p\) as a sum of length-\(p\) walks. For a zero-position variable, its exponent must be \(0\) or \(p-1\) to survive the sum over \(F_p^*\), giving a global factor \((-1)^K\). For \(p>2\) this leaves only all-constant walks \((C^p)\) plus walks with \(p-1\) copies of a zero diagonal edge and one constant edge; for \(p=3\) off-diagonal zero edges also contribute via \(E_{u,v} C E_{u,v}\). Thus the answer is \((-1)^K(C^p+V) \bmod p\), with a trivial \(p=2\) shortcut. Compute \(C^p\) by fast modular matrix exponentiation and add the \(O(N^2)\) correction \(V\).
