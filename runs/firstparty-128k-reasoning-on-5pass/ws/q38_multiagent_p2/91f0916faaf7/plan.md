Work prime-by-prime using valuations. For each prime p, the condition f(S_i/S_{i+1})=A_i is equivalent to |v_p(S_i)-v_p(S_{i+1})|=v_p(A_i), and gcd(S_1,...,S_N)=1 is equivalent to min_i v_p(S_i)=0. The score factorizes over primes, so the desired sum is the product over p of the weighted sum of valid p-valuation sequences. For fixed p, let a_i=v_p(A_i) and H=sum a_i; valid sequences are nonnegative walks of length N with steps ±a_i (or 0 if a_i=0) that hit 0, and every such walk has all heights at most H. Compute each prime's contribution by a DP over position and height, weighting a height h by p^h, e.g. total nonnegative walks minus walks that stay strictly positive. Multiply the contributions modulo 998244353.
