If W > H, transpose the grid and map directions (L->U, R->D, U->L, D->R) so the number of columns is the smaller dimension, at most 447. Define P[h][w] and S[h][w] as the sums of products of cells on paths from (1,1) to (h,w) and from (h,w) to (H,W), excluding (h,w) itself; then the total answer is A[H-1][W-1]*P[H-1][W-1], and changing A[r][c] by delta changes the answer by delta*P[r][c]*S[r][c]. Maintain P and S rows lazily with two frontiers: P rows 0..p_front-1 are valid and S rows s_front..H-1 are valid. After an actual value change at row r, invalidate by p_front=min(p_front,r) and s_front=max(s_front,r+1); before using row r, recompute missing P rows from the top and missing S rows from the bottom, each in O(W). This yields O(HW + Q*min(H,W)) time and answers each query online.
