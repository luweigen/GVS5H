Treat the operations as reversible swaps of a block `0^X1^Y` with `1^Y0^X`, so the total numbers of `0`s and `1`s and the left-to-right order of each symbol are preserved. Label the zeros and ones in left-to-right order; in either operation each labeled zero moves by `0` or `±Y` positions and each labeled one moves by `0` or `±X` positions, so the position of the k-th zero modulo `Y` and the position of the k-th one modulo `X` are invariants. These invariants are also sufficient: orienting only `0^X1^Y -> 1^Y0^X` gives a terminating rewrite system with no self-overlaps, hence a unique normal form determined by the invariants. Thus `S` can be transformed to `T` exactly when the counts match and the corresponding ordered positions have the required congruences. An `O(N)` scan extracting positions and comparing residues solves the problem.
