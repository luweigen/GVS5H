Model each additional unit of product i as a marginal item with cost P_i(2k-1), where k is the unit number, so the sequences are increasing and precedence is automatically respected by taking cheapest marginals. For a threshold C, the number of units of product i with marginal cost at most C is t_i = floor((C/P_i + 1)/2), and their total cost is P_i t_i^2. Binary search the largest C such that sum_i P_i t_i^2 <= M, using a safe upper bound derived from the minimum P_i and M. After this C, all marginals <= C are bought; the next cheapest marginals must have cost C+1, so the remaining budget buys min(count_{C+1}, floor((M-spent)/(C+1))) additional units. This yields the maximum total units in O(U log B) time, where U is the number of distinct P_i.
