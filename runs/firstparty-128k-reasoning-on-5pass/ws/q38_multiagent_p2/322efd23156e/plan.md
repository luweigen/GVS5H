Group the foods by their vitamin type. For each vitamin type, compute a 0/1 knapsack DP over calorie capacity `X`, where `dp_v[c]` is the maximum amount of vitamin `v` obtainable using at most `c` calories from foods of that type. For a candidate answer `T`, feasibility means that each vitamin type can independently reach at least `T` units, and the sum of the minimum calories required for the three types is at most `X`. Binary search the largest feasible `T` between `0` and the minimum total vitamin amount among the three types. This gives an efficient solution with complexity about `O(NX + log(maxA) * X)`.
