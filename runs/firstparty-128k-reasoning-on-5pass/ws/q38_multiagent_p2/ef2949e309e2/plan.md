For each index as the middle of a length-5 subsequence, choose two indices before it and two after it. The middle value x is a unique mode iff it appears at least three times in the chosen 5 elements, or it appears exactly twice and the other three chosen values are pairwise distinct; appearing once is impossible. Sweep the middle index while maintaining prefix and suffix frequency arrays and equal-pair sums, so the number of distinct-value pairs on each side excluding x can be obtained in O(1). Count the automatically valid cases with at least two outer x's by combinations, and count the exactly-one-outer-x cases by iterating over the non-x value on the single side and subtracting opposite-side pairs that involve x or that value. Sum all contributions modulo 1e9+7; O(n^2) over distinct values is easily within n<=1000.
