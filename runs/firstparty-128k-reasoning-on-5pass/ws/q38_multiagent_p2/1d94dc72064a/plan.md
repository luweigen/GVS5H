Treat each move as either opening an untouched index or spending a token on an already touched index. The only relevant information is the parity of the total tokens in touched indices and the parity of each untouched A_i, because opening an odd A_i preserves that parity and opening an even A_i flips it. Reduce the game to a finite impartial game on counts of odd and even untouched piles plus a bit, and prove by induction that the outcome depends only on these counts and the bit. Solve the resulting recurrence to get a closed form: N=1 Fennec, N=2 Snuke, N=3 Fennec iff at least one A_i is odd, and for N>=4 Fennec iff the number of odd A_i is odd. Implement this O(N) check.
