Model the N repeated majority operations as a complete ternary tree whose leaves are the characters of A. For every node, compute two DP values: the minimum number of leaf flips in its subtree needed to make the node 0, and the minimum needed to make it 1. A leaf has costs (1-b, b); an internal node forcing 0 requires at least two of its three children to be 0, so take the minimum sum over the three choices of which child is 1, and forcing 1 is symmetric. Process these values bottom-up on the flat array, replacing each block of three child costs by one parent cost pair. The original final bit is the root value with cost 0, so the answer is the opposite root cost, giving O(3^N) time and O(3^N) memory.
