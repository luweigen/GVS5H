Group foods by vitamin and compute, for each group, the maximum vitamin intake achievable under every calorie limit from 0 to \(X\) using 0/1 knapsack DP. For a candidate target \(T\), find the minimum calorie cost needed for each vitamin to reach at least \(T\) units, and check whether their sum is at most \(X\). Since feasibility is monotonic in \(T\), binary search the largest feasible target. The precomputation takes \(O(NX)\), and each feasibility check takes \(O(\log X)\) per vitamin after the DP tables are made.
