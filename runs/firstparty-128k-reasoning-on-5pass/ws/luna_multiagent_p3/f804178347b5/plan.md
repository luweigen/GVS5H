Model the majority operation as a ternary tree whose leaves are the original bits and whose root is the final value. For each subtree, compute the minimum number of leaf changes needed to force its output to be 0 and to be 1. At an internal node, combine the three child costs: to force a target value, at least two children must be forced to that value, so select the two cheapest corresponding costs while allowing the third child to remain unchanged. Return the minimum cost to force the root to the opposite of its original computed value.
