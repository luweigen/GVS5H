For each pair of buildings \(j<i\), visibility of building \(i\) requires the slope from \((0,h)\) to its top to be strictly larger than the corresponding slope to building \(j\). This yields the condition \(h > (X_iH_j-X_jH_i)/(X_i-X_j)\), so the answer is the maximum value of this expression over all pairs, provided it is nonnegative; if the maximum is negative, height \(0\) already sees every building and the answer is \(-1\). Compute the maximum pairwise intercept efficiently by maintaining the lower convex hull of previously processed building points and querying the tangent/minimum secant slope for each new point. Use exact integer cross products for hull comparisons and floating-point division only for the final answer.
