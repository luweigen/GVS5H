Model each town with a potential \(x_v\), where road strengths are differences of adjacent potentials. A person from \(S\) to \(T\) requires \(x_S=x_T\) and \(x_v>x_S\) for every internal town when \(S<T\) (reversed inequalities when \(S>T\)); thus a selected set is feasible iff these equalities and strict inequalities contain no contradiction. For every query interval \([L,R]\), determine whether the induced constraint system is consistent, exploiting the fact that all constraints come from contiguous intervals on a line and processing queries offline in near-linear or \(O((M+Q)\log N)\) time. The implementation should maintain equality components and detect directed cycles/inequality contradictions while extending or removing people from the active range.
