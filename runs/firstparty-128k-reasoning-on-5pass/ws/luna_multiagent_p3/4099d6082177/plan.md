Root the tree arbitrarily and process vertices in postorder. For each subtree, determine whether it can be decomposed into complete K-vertex paths plus at most one unfinished path whose endpoint is the subtree root; its size is uniquely determined by the subtree structure. At a vertex, combine unfinished paths from its children: at most two may exist, and two can be joined through the vertex only when their total size plus one equals K. Propagate the resulting unfinished-path size upward, accepting exactly when the root has no unfinished path.
