Let the positions of all `1`s be \(p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_{k-1}\). If they are moved into a contiguous block starting at \(l\), the minimum swaps for that block is \(\sum_i |p_i-(l+i)|\). Transforming with \(q_i=p_i-i\), this becomes minimizing \(\sum_i |q_i-l|\), whose optimum is any median of the \(q_i\) values. Compute the median and sum absolute deviations in \(O(N)\) time and \(O(k)\) memory, then print the result.
