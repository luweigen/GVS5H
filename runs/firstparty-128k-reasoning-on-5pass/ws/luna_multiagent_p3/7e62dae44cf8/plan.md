Model the cells adjacent to Takahashi as a min-heap, initially containing his four neighboring cells. Repeatedly remove the smallest adjacent slime; absorb it exactly when its strength satisfies `X * slime_strength < current_strength`, adding newly exposed neighbors to the heap. If the smallest candidate is too strong, all other candidates are also too strong at that moment, so the process stops and the current strength is optimal. Use 64-bit integers, since the accumulated strength can exceed individual cell values.
