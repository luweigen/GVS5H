Track the cumulative wind displacement from the origin after each time step. A smoke particle generated at time \(k\) is displaced at time \(t\) by the difference between the prefix positions after \(t\) and \(k-1\). Thus, smoke is at \((R,C)\) at time \(t\) exactly when the prefix position at \(t\) minus \((R,C)\) has appeared as a previous prefix position, including the initial origin. Maintain a set of all prefix positions and build the answer in \(O(N)\) time.
