Express each number’s contribution by its decimal length and the set of elements placed after it: in any permutation, the digits of \(i\) are shifted by the total digit lengths of all later elements. By symmetry, for every pair of distinct values \(i,j\), exactly half of all permutations place \(j\) after \(i\), allowing the expected positional multiplier to be computed independently. Derive the resulting sum using factorial weights and modular powers of 10, then precompute digit lengths, factorials, and inverse factorials in \(O(N)\) time. Implement the formula modulo \(998244353\), reading \(N\) and printing the final result.
