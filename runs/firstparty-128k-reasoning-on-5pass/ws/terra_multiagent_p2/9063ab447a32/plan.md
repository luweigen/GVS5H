View each purchased unit as having a marginal cost: for product \(i\), the \(k\)-th unit costs \((2k-1)P_i\). The optimal strategy is to buy the globally cheapest marginal-cost units, since every product’s marginal costs are increasing. Binary search a threshold cost \(x\), count how many units have marginal cost at most \(x\), and compute their total cost efficiently. Then spend any remaining budget on additional units whose marginal cost equals the next possible threshold.
