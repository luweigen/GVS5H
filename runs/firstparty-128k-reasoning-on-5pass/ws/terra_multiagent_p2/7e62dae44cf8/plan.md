Model Takahashi’s occupied region as expanding from the starting cell. Maintain all currently adjacent unabsorbed slimes in a min-heap by strength; whenever the weakest adjacent slime satisfies `strength * X < current_strength`, absorb it, add its strength, and expose its unvisited neighbors. If the heap minimum cannot be absorbed, no other adjacent slime can be absorbed either, so the process must stop. Use 64-bit-safe arithmetic (Python integers are sufficient) because strengths can grow to roughly the sum of all cells.
