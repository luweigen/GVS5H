Exploit finite-field summation identities: summing a variable over \(1,\dots,p-1\) modulo \(p\) is zero except when its exponent is a positive multiple of \(p-1\). Expand each entry of \(B^p\) as a sum over length-\(p\) walks, and determine which walks survive after summing all replacements of zero entries. Derive a compact matrix expression for the surviving walks, avoiding dependence on \(p\) iterations since \(p\) can be large. Implement the resulting matrix operations modulo \(p\) in \(O(N^3\log p)\) or better.
