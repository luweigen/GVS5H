Model visibility of each building from observer height \(h\) using the slope of the line to a point on that building. A building is visible iff some point on it can be connected without crossing prior buildings, which can be characterized by comparing its attainable slope interval against the maximum blocking slope of earlier buildings. Derive the critical observer height at which every building becomes visible, then find the largest height where at least one remains blocked. Process buildings from left to right while maintaining the relevant upper envelope / maximum ratio condition in \(O(N)\) or \(O(N \log N)\).
