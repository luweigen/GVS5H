Model the parenthesis sequence as an ordered rooted forest/tree, where each matched pair is a node and concatenation gives siblings. Reversing a valid substring corresponds to reversing the order of a consecutive group of sibling subtrees, while recursively preserving their internal structures under the complemented reversal. Characterize all distinct reachable sequences via an interval DP over the tree/forest that counts distinct reorderings obtainable by these operations. Since \(N \le 5000\), derive an \(O(N^2)\) or \(O(N^3)\) dynamic program with modulo arithmetic and prove duplicate constructions are not overcounted.
