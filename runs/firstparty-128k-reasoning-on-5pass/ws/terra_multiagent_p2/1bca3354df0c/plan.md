Model every connected component as a bipartite graph, since the graph is always required to remain free of odd cycles. A legal move either adds a missing edge between opposite color classes within one component, or connects two different components while choosing their bipartition orientations freely. Analyze the game via an invariant that determines the total remaining move parity independent of move choices, then compute that invariant from component sizes and existing edges. Use BFS/DFS coloring to obtain the two partition sizes of each component efficiently.
