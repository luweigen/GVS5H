Model each edge orientation by a binary variable indicating which endpoint receives its contribution to indegree. The cycle edges determine the indegrees of vertices \(0\ldots N-1\) up to additions from the center vertex \(N\), while the center indegree records the number of selected spokes oriented toward it. Derive a compact DP/transfer-matrix representation that counts distinct resulting degree vectors rather than orientations, carefully handling collisions.
