Rewrite the equation as \(N=(x-y)(x^2+xy+y^2)\), and let \(d=x-y>0\). For each possible \(d\), derive a quadratic equation for \(y\): \(3y^2+3dy+d^2=N/d\). Since \(d^3 < N\), it is sufficient to enumerate \(d\) up to roughly \(\sqrt[3]{N}\), checking only divisors of \(N\). Use integer square root arithmetic to verify whether the quadratic discriminant is a perfect square and reconstruct positive \(x,y\).
