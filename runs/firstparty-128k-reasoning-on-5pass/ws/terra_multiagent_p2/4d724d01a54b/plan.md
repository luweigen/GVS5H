Model sorting as resolving inversions, where swapping adjacent positions \(i,i+1\) costs \(i\). Derive a greedy or invariant-based characterization of the minimum total weighted adjacent-swap cost, exploiting that each element must cross certain boundaries. Use an \(O(N \log N)\) data structure approach, likely Fenwick trees, to efficiently aggregate the necessary contributions. Prove optimality by showing every required crossing incurs an unavoidable boundary cost and that a construction achieves this lower bound.
