Model the effect of all column flips as a bitmask of length \(W\). Once a column-flip mask is fixed, each row can independently either stay unchanged or be flipped, so its contribution is the minimum of its number of ones and zeros after applying the mask. Enumerate all \(2^W\) masks, compute the total minimized row contribution for each, and take the minimum. Since \(W \leq 18\), this is feasible; optimize evaluation using frequencies of row bitmasks or direct precomputed popcounts.
