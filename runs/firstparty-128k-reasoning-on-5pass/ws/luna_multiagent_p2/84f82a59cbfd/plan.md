A 400 number is exactly the square of an integer having two distinct prime factors: if \(N=p^{2a}q^{2b}\), then \(N=(p^a q^b)^2\), and conversely. Precompute the smallest prime factor for every integer up to \(\sqrt{10^{12}}=10^6\), count distinct prime factors for each integer, and store the squares of those with exactly two. Sort the resulting list, then answer each query with binary search for the largest square not exceeding \(A\).
