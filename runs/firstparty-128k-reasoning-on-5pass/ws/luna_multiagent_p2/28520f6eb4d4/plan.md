For each building \(i\), determine the minimum height \(h\) such that its top can be seen over every earlier building; the critical value for an earlier building \(j\) is the \(y\)-intercept of the line through \((X_j,H_j)\) and \((X_i,H_i)\). Thus the global answer is the maximum over all pairs \(j<i\) of \((X_iH_j-X_jH_i)/(X_i-X_j)\), with negative results replaced by the special output \(-1\) when height \(0\) already suffices. Rewrite each per-building maximum as \(H_i-X_i\) times the minimum slope from \((X_i,H_i)\) to any previous point. Maintain the lower convex hull of previous buildings and query the minimum such slope using a monotone-pointer or binary-search tangent method, giving \(O(N)\) or \(O(N\log N)\) time.
