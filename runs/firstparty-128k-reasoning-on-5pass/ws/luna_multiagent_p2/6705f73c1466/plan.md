Let the positions of all `1`s be \(p_0,p_1,\dots,p_{k-1}\). If they are made contiguous while preserving their order, their final positions must be \(x,x+1,\dots,x+k-1\), and the required swaps equal \(\sum_i |p_i-(x+i)|\). Define \(a_i=p_i-i\); then choose the median of the \(a_i\) values to minimize the sum of absolute deviations. Compute this minimum in \(O(N)\) time and use 64-bit integers for the answer.
