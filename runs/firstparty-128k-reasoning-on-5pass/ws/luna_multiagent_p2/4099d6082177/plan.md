Root the tree arbitrarily and process vertices in postorder. For each subtree, maintain the length of the unique unfinished path whose endpoint is the current vertex, or report zero when the subtree is fully decomposed into complete paths. At a vertex, combine unfinished paths from its children: there may be at most two, and they must either extend through the vertex or form a complete path of exactly \(K\) vertices. The decomposition is possible exactly when the root finishes with no unfinished path; implement the traversal iteratively to support \(NK \le 2\cdot10^5\).
