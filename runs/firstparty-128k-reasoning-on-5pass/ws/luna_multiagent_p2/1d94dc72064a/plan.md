Model the game state by the multiset of unvisited indices and the total number of remaining moves available on already visited indices; such a move acts as a pass. Derive recursively that every state is either always winning for the player to move or alternates by the pass-count parity. Show that the state classification depends only on the number of odd-valued and even-valued \(A_i\). For the initial state, Fennec wins iff the resulting parity condition holds, allowing an \(O(N)\) solution without sorting or simulation.
