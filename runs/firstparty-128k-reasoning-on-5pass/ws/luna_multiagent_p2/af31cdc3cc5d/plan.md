Model each pairing process as a noncrossing matching of adjacent-removable elements; every matched pair contributes the absolute difference of its values. Derive a characterization of feasible matchings based on parity and interval structure, then simplify the optimization to a linear-time or \(O(N \log N)\) computation over the sequence. Verify the formula against odd/even lengths and boundary cases, including equal values. Implement the resulting algorithm with 64-bit integer arithmetic and fast input.
