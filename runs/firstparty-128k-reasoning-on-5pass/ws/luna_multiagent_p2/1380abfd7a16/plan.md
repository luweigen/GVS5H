Process insertions in reverse: when removing numbers from \(N\) down to \(1\), the position \(P_i\) identifies the position of \(i\) among the currently empty final slots. Use a Fenwick tree initialized with all \(N\) positions available, supporting order-statistic selection of the \(P_i\)-th available slot in \(O(\log N)\). Place \(i\) into that slot and mark it occupied. Finally, output the filled array from left to right, giving \(O(N\log N)\) time and \(O(N)\) memory.
