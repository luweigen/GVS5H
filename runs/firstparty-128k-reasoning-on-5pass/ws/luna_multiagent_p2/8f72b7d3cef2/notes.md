- **Cartesian tree:** Build a max Cartesian tree, popping while `A[stack[-1]] <= A[i]`. Thus equal-valued elements are separated by an ancestor/descendant boundary, and strict absorption is preserved.
- **Subtree sum:** `subtree[v]` is the sum of the complete Cartesian subtree of `v`. Once slime `v` itself is absorbed, the accumulated size is strictly greater than `A[v]`, so every remaining slime in `v`’s subtree, including equal-valued ones, can then be absorbed.
- **Initial basin:** Before slime `v` is absorbed, it can consume all connected descendants whose values are strictly smaller than `A[v]`. Equal-valued child subtrees are blockers and must be excluded. Define `basin[v] = A[v] + subtree[child]` over children with `A[child] < A[v]`.
- **Ancestor transition:** If `p = parent[v]`, then `v` can absorb `p` exactly when `basin[v] > A[p]`. After doing so, the obtainable mass becomes `subtree[p]`.
- **Later transitions:** Once the complete subtree of `v` has been obtained, moving to its parent `p` is possible iff `subtree[v] > A[p]`. These conditions are independent edge predicates on the Cartesian-tree ancestor path.
- **Binary lifting:** Store jumps consisting entirely of successful later transitions. After checking the first transition using `basin[v]`, binary lifting finds the highest reachable ancestor in `O(log N)`.
- **Correctness idea:** Any first slime that cannot be absorbed is a nearest Cartesian-tree ancestor with value at least the current mass. All lower descendants are absorbable and all equal descendants are blocked. Once an ancestor is absorbed, its current mass becomes larger than that ancestor’s value, making its entire Cartesian subtree absorbable.
- **Complexity:** Cartesian tree construction, subtree DP, and binary-lifting preprocessing take `O(N log N)` time and `O(N log N)` memory. Every answer is computed in `O(log N)`. Python integers safely handle all prefix/subtree sums.
