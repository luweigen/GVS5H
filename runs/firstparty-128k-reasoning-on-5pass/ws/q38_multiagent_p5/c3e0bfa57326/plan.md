Parse S into its rooted forest: each matched pair is a node, and nested pairs are children of the enclosing pair. For a valid substring, the operation is exactly the mirror of the corresponding sibling block: it reverses the block and mirrors every subtree inside it, so the unordered rooted forest shape is invariant. Conversely, mirroring a pair of adjacent siblings and then mirroring each resulting sibling back gives an adjacent swap that preserves internal embeddings, and operations inside a subtree can realize any embedding of its unordered type. Hence the reachable strings are precisely the plane forests whose underlying unordered rooted forest is the one represented by S.
