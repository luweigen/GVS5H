- **Model:** A snowflake is determined by a red vertex c, a set B of its original neighbors used as blue vertices, and a positive integer y. A blue vertex b needs at least y neighbors other than c, so y <= deg(b) - 1. For fixed c and d = y + 1, taking all neighbors of c with degree at least d is optimal; the kept size is 1 + d * count. The answer is N minus the maximum kept size.
- **Sweep:** Maintain cnt[c] = number of neighbors of c with degree at least the current threshold d. Process d from maximum degree down to 2. When a vertex v of degree d is activated, increment cnt[u] for every neighbor u. Since cnt values only increase, a single global max_cnt is enough. Evaluate only after the whole degree group is added, using candidate size 1 + d * max_cnt.
- **Correctness:** Any valid snowflake with k blue vertices and y gives d = y + 1. All its blue vertices have degree at least d, so k <= cnt_d[c] and its size is at most 1 + d * max_cnt_d. Conversely, for any c and d with k = cnt_d[c] > 0, choose those k neighbors as blue and choose d - 1 other neighbors of each as leaves. In a tree, these leaf sets are disjoint and introduce no extra edges, so this is a valid snowflake of size 1 + k*d. Thus the sweep maximum is exact.
- **Complexity:** Building adjacency is O(N). Each non-leaf vertex is activated once and its adjacency list is scanned once, total O(N). The degree loop is O(N) because max degree <= N - 1. Memory is O(N).
- **Edge cases:** N=3 path gives best 3. A star is kept whole by choosing a leaf as red and the center as the only blue. Paths of length at least 5 keep 5 vertices. The red center may be a leaf; this is handled because every neighbor of an activated vertex is incremented. Blue vertices may be high-degree in the original tree; their extra branches are deleted. d=1 is not considered because y must be positive.
- **Implementation:** Use 1-indexed adjacency; original degree is len(adj[v]). Read all tokens, build adjacency, then delete the token list. Bucket vertices by degree with head/nxt arrays to avoid sorting. Update max_cnt during increments and evaluate only non-empty degree groups. Sample checks: 1 -> 1, 2 -> 0, 3 -> 3.
