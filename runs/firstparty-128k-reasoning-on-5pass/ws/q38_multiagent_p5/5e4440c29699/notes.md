- **Model:** The town is the full rectangle [0,W]x[0,H] with the forbidden rectangle [L,R]x[D,U] removed. Paths are nonempty monotone sequences of right/up moves.
- **Full-rectangle counts:** For remaining moves (a,b), G(a,b)=C(a+b+2,a+1)-1. Its 2D prefix is S(A,B)=C(A+B+4,A+2)-A-B-4-(A+1)(B+1), with S=0 if A<0 or B<0.
- **Allowed starts:** total full paths S(W,H) minus the forbidden-start rectangle sum using a1=W-R, a2=W-L, b1=H-U, b2=H-D and 2D prefix inclusion-exclusion.
- **Bad paths:** A full path starting allowed and entering the hole has a unique first hole point. It can only be on the bottom edge (x,D), L<=x<=R, D>0, or left edge (L,y), D<=y<=U, L>0. Bottom term is G(x,D-1)*G(W-x,H-D); left term is G(L-1,y)*G(W-L,H-y). The corner is not double counted because the previous move differs.
- **Complement sums:** Sum over all x of the bottom term equals S(W,H)-S(W,D-1)-S(W,H-D); sum over all y of the left term equals S(W,H)-S(L-1,H)-S(W-L,H). If the needed interval is longer than its complement, sum the outside ranges and subtract from the full crossing sum. This bounds loops by about (W+1)/2+(H+1)/2.
- **Implementation:** Precompute fact/invfact up to W+H+4, which is below the mod. In loops compute each binomial as one product of three precomputed values with a single modulo, accumulate products without per-term modulo, then take final modulo.
- **Edge cases:** D=0 skips bottom entry, L=0 skips left entry; boundary holes work because remaining moves can be zero and outside ranges can be empty. Sample 1 was checked by hand: total_allowed=401, bad_below=151, bad_left=58, answer=192. Sample 3 has L=D=0, so no bad paths and only the allowed-start count is needed.
