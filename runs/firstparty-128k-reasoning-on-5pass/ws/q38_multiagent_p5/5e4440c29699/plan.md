Model the town as the full rectangle \(0\le x\le W,\ 0\le y\le H\) with the forbidden rectangle \(L\le x\le R,\ D\le y\le U\) removed. Count all monotone paths that start at an allowed point in the full rectangle, then subtract the paths that ever enter the forbidden rectangle. For a point with \(a\) right-moves and \(b\) up-moves remaining, the number of full-rectangle paths starting there (or ending there from any start) is \(G(a,b)=\binom{a+b+2}{a+1}-1\), and the 2D prefix sum of \(G\) has a closed form. A bad path has a unique first entry into the forbidden rectangle, either from below at \((x,D)\) for \(L\le x\le R\) or from left at \((L,y)\) for \(D\le y\le U\); the prefix to the adjacent outside point and the suffix from the entry are both unrestricted in the full rectangle. Sum these first-entry products over the two boundary edges in \(O(W+H)\) time and combine modulo \(998244353\).
