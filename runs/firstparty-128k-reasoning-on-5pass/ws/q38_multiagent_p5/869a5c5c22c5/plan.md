The problem is equivalent to finding a simple cycle with exactly R axis-unit edges (red moves) and B diagonal-unit edges (blue moves). Parity of \(r+c\) shows R must be even, and if R=0 the all-blue cycle must have even length B. These conditions are sufficient: for R>0 even and B>0, build a red 2-row snake from \((0,0)\) to \((1,0)\), choosing its side by B parity, then append a blue diagonal snake that starts orthogonally from the red end and ends diagonally at \((0,0)\). Use separate half-planes for the red and blue snakes so all vertices are distinct. Handle B=0 with an all-red snake cycle and R=0 with an all-blue snake in transformed \((u,v)\) coordinates, then shift all coordinates into the board.
