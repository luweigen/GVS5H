Represent a valid coloring by the number \(a_i\) of black cells in row \(i\). The row condition makes each row a black prefix, and the column condition is equivalent to \(a_1 \ge a_2 \ge \dots \ge a_N\). A fixed black cell \((x,y)\) imposes \(a_x \ge y\), while a fixed white cell imposes \(a_x \le y-1\). For each row aggregate \(L_x = \max\) required black column and \(U_x = \min\) allowed black length from whites, with defaults \(0\) and \(N\). A nonincreasing sequence within these bounds exists iff for every row \(x\), the maximum \(L_k\) over \(k \ge x\) is at most \(U_x\). Therefore sweep rows in descending order maintaining the suffix maximum of \(L\) and check it against each finite \(U\).
