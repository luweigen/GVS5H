Convert each conflicting pair into an interval [l, r] with l < r. For a fixed right endpoint R, a subarray ending at R is bad exactly when its start is at most the maximum l among intervals with r <= R, so the total bad count is the sum over R of this prefix maximum. Sweep R from 1 to n, adding intervals whose r equals R, and maintain the current largest active l, the second-largest active l, and how many active intervals have the largest l. When the largest l is unique, removing that interval would reduce the prefix maximum at R from largest to second-largest, so accumulate that difference into that interval's removal delta. The optimal removal is the interval with maximum delta, and the answer is total subarrays minus (base_bad - max_delta).
