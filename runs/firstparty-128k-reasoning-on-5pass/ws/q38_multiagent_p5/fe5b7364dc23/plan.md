Use prefix sums \(P_i\) so each subarray sum is \(P_r - P_{l-1}\). For each right endpoint \(r\), expand \((P_r - P_t)^K\) by the binomial theorem over all \(t < r\). Maintain the moments \(Q_m = \sum_{t=0}^{r-1} P_t^m\) for \(0 \le m \le K\), where \(Q_0\) is the count of previous prefixes. Then the contribution of endpoint \(r\) is \(\sum_{j=0}^K \binom{K}{j}(-1)^{K-j}P_r^j Q_{K-j}\). After processing \(r\), update the moments with \(P_r\). This gives an \(O(NK)\) algorithm, easily fast enough since \(K \le 10\).
