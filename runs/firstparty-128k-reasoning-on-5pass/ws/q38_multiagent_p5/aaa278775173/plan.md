Model a valid segment as a straight diagonal prefix starting at a 1, optionally followed by one straight diagonal suffix in the clockwise-next direction. Precompute, for every cell and diagonal direction, the longest straight run that starts there and matches the required periodic pattern (1 then 2/0, or 2/0 alternating). Also precompute, for every cell and direction, the longest straight prefix ending there that started with 1, kept separately by length parity. Then combine at each possible turn cell: an odd-length first leg (length >1) ends in 0 and can continue in the clockwise direction with a 0-starting alternating run; an even-length first leg ends in 2 and continues with a 2-starting run. The answer is the maximum of straight runs and these turn combinations. Use O(nm) time and memory-efficient flat arrays or per-direction passes because n,m up to 500.
