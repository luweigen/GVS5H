Model each additional unit as a marginal cost: the t-th unit of product i costs (2t-1)P_i, and an optimal purchase is the cheapest set of marginal units. For a threshold X, product i contributes c_i=(X//P_i+1)//2 units with total cost c_i^2 P_i. Binary search the largest X whose total marginal cost over all products is at most M, using a tight upper bound from the minimum P_i. At that X, let f be the total count, g the total cost, and C the smallest next marginal cost; the remaining budget can only buy units of cost C, so the answer is f + (M-g)//C.
