Treat each removed adjacent pair as an edge in a noncrossing matching; for odd N the single remaining element at 0-based index p must have even p and separates the problem into two independent even-length prefixes/suffixes. For any even-length segment, crossing pairs can be uncrossed without decreasing the total absolute difference, so the segment's optimum is the sum of its largest half minus the sum of its smallest half. Therefore even N is solved by sorting the whole array and taking that difference. For odd N, compute the even-prefix and even-suffix segment scores with two heaps maintaining the smallest and largest halves, then take the maximum prefix_score[p] + suffix_score[p+1] over even p.
