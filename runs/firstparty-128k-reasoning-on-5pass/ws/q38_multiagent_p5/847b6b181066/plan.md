Track the cumulative wind displacement P_t after t steps, with P_0 = (0,0). A smoke particle born at time b at (0,0) is at P_t - P_b at time t, and a new particle is born exactly when P_t is a prefix displacement not seen before, so birth times are first occurrences of prefix positions. Therefore target (R,C) is occupied at time t iff P_t - (R,C) has already appeared among P_0,...,P_t. Process S left to right, maintaining current prefix and a hash set of seen prefix coordinates; for each t test membership of the shifted coordinate, append 1/0, then insert the current prefix. This gives O(N) time and O(N) memory.
