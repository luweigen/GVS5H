Model each 1 by its current index and the target contiguous block of length k. Because adjacent swaps preserve the relative order of 1s, the i-th 1 must go to the i-th slot of the block. For a block starting at l, the cost is sum |pos_i - (l+i)| = sum |(pos_i - i) - l|. Thus the problem reduces to choosing l minimizing the sum of absolute deviations from the adjusted positions, which is achieved at their median. Since the adjusted positions are sorted and lie within feasible start positions, take the median and sum the distances.
