Root the tree arbitrarily and create two directed edges for every undirected edge. For a directed edge u->v, define dp[u->v] as the maximum size of a valid connected piece on v's side when uv is selected, with v's degree including u equal to 1 or 4, and big[u->v] as the same value when v has degree 4. Then big[u->v] is 1 plus the sum of the three largest dp[v->w] over w≠u (or -inf if fewer than three such neighbors), and dp[u->v]=max(1,big[u->v]). Compute all directed values by a postorder pass for parent-to-child directions and a preorder rerooting pass for child-to-parent directions. Finally, an optimal alkane can be rooted at a degree-4 vertex (choose four incident dp values) or at a leaf whose neighbor is degree-4 (choose one incident big value), so take the maximum over all vertices.
