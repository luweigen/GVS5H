Model the string as leaves of a full ternary tree of height N, where each internal node is the majority of its three children. For every node compute two values: the minimum number of leaf flips in its subtree needed to make the node 0, and the minimum needed to make it 1. A leaf has costs (0,1) or (1,0) depending on its bit. For an internal node, to force it to 0 choose the two cheapest children to be 0 and let the third be either value, taking the minimum over the three choices; the cost for 1 is symmetric. Process these recurrences bottom-up over the 3^N leaves, then output the cost for the value opposite the current root.
