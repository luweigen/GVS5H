Because each food contributes to only one vitamin, the three vitamin requirements can be optimized independently for a fixed target T. For each vitamin type, compute a 0/1 knapsack DP over calorie budgets up to X that stores the maximum amount of that vitamin obtainable with at most that many calories. A target T is feasible iff the sum of the minimum calories needed from the three DPs to reach at least T is at most X. This feasibility is monotone, so binary search the largest T. The DP costs O(NX) total and each check is O(3 log X), easily within limits.
