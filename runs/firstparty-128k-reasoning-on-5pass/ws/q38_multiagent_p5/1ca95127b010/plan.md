Treat Operation A as the rewrite rule 0^X 1^Y -> 1^Y 0^X, with Operation B as its inverse. This oriented rewrite is terminating and has no proper self-overlap, so it is confluent; therefore S and T are equivalent iff their A-normal forms are identical. An A-normal form is a binary string with no 0-run of length at least X immediately followed by a 1-run of length at least Y. Maintain this normal form online while scanning runs, using cells separated by small zero barriers (remainders modulo X); inside each cell, zero runs are full X-blocks and all full Y-blocks of ones are kept at the cell front. Appending a 1-run moves any full Y-blocks from the last 1-run to the current cell front in O(1), while appending a 0-run updates the trailing zero run and splits/merges barriers modulo X in O(1) amortized. Finally, compute the canonical normal forms for S and T and compare their merged run-length encodings.
