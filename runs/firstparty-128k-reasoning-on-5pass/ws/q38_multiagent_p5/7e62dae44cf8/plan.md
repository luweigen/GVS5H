Treat Takahashi as a connected absorbed region starting at (P,Q) with total strength T. A neighboring unabsorbed cell can be added exactly when X * S_cell < T, and adding it only increases T and the region. Because all strengths are positive, any currently absorbable boundary cell can be taken without making future absorptions harder, so repeatedly absorbing all possible boundary cells yields the optimal final strength. Maintain the boundary in a min-heap by S_cell, pop while X * S_cell < T, add its strength, and push its unabsorbed neighbors; stop when the minimum boundary cell fails the strict inequality. Use arbitrary-precision or 128-bit arithmetic for X * S_cell and the total sum.
