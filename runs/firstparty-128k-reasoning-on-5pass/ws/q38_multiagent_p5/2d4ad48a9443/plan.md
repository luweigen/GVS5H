Compute the minimum operations for a subarray as the sum of its running maxima minus the sum of its elements, because the optimal target is the prefix maximum sequence. Use a two-pointer over the right endpoint, since for a fixed right endpoint the cost is monotone as the left endpoint moves. Maintain the sum of running maxima S and the window element sum; when extending the right, increase S by the current window maximum. When moving the left, adjust S by removing the contribution of the leaving element and adding contributions of elements that were blocked by it, using precomputed previous greater-or-equal and next strictly greater positions. This yields an O(n) algorithm with monotonic stacks and a deque.
