Model the placement as a simple cycle whose edges are colored by the outgoing piece: red edges are orthogonal and blue edges are diagonal. A red edge flips checkerboard parity while a blue edge preserves it, so R must be even; if R=0, blue moves also flip row parity, so B must be even. These conditions are sufficient: for R>0 and even, choose endpoints according to the parity of B, draw a red U-shaped path of exactly R orthogonal edges between them, and draw a blue diagonal snake of exactly B edges on the opposite side, then traverse the red path followed by the reversed blue path. The all-red and all-blue cases are handled by analogous U-shaped cycles, with 2-cycles for length 2. Finally shift all coordinates by a large constant to fit the board and output the vertex sequence with colors determined by the outgoing edge.
