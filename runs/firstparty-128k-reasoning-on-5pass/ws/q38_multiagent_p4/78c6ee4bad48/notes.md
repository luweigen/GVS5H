- **Gap model:** Let the sorted coordinates be a_1 < a_2 < ... < a_N and define gaps d_k = a_{k+1} - a_k for k = 1..N-1. For an operation on positions i, i+1, i+2, i+3, the outer coordinates a_i and a_{i+3} are fixed, and the two middle coordinates are reflected about their midpoint. The four gaps transform as (d_i, d_{i+1}, d_{i+2}) -> (d_{i+2}, d_{i+1}, d_i). Thus the operation exactly swaps d_i and d_{i+2}.
- **Reachability:** The allowed swaps are (1,3), (3,5), ... for odd-indexed gaps and (2,4), (4,6), ... for even-indexed gaps. These are adjacent swaps inside the odd subsequence and inside the even subsequence. Adjacent swaps generate all permutations, so every permutation of odd gaps among odd positions and even gaps among even positions is reachable.
- **Invariant:** The leftmost coordinate a_1 is never moved, so it is invariant. All gap values remain the original positive gaps; only their positions within parity classes change.
- **Objective:** The sum of coordinates is N*a_1 + sum_{k=1}^{N-1} (N-k)*d_k. Gap k affects all coordinates to its right, so its weight is N-k. Weights strictly decrease from left to right.
- **Optimization:** Odd and even gap classes are independent. For each parity, the weights are decreasing. To minimize the weighted sum with positive gaps, assign smaller gaps to larger weights and larger gaps to smaller weights. Therefore sort each parity's gaps ascending and place them left to right.
- **Implementation:** Read all integers. Use data[1] as x0. For idx from 2 to N, compute gap data[idx] - data[idx-1]. Its 1-based gap index is idx-1, so odd gaps are appended when idx-1 is odd. Sort odd and even lists. Accumulate ans = N*x0, then add odd gaps with weights N-1, N-3, ... and even gaps with weights N-2, N-4, ...
- **Complexity:** Sorting two lists whose total length is N-1 gives O(N log N) time and O(N) memory. Python integers safely handle the maximum answer size.
- **Validation:** Sample 1: gaps [4,2,3], odd sorted [3,4], even [2], answer 4*1 + 3*3 + 4*1 + 2*2 = 21. Sample 2: gaps [1,5,4,4,2], odd sorted [1,2,4], even sorted [4,5], answer 1*5 + 2*3 + 4*1 + 4*4 + 5*2 = 41. N=4 equal gaps gives the original sum; maximum coordinate values are handled without floating point.
