Work with the gaps between consecutive sorted pieces instead of the coordinates themselves. The operation on four consecutive pieces leaves the outer two pieces fixed and swaps the first and third gaps, so it is exactly an adjacent swap of gaps whose indices have the same parity. Therefore the leftmost coordinate is invariant, and the only reachable changes are arbitrary permutations of odd-indexed gaps among odd positions and even-indexed gaps among even positions. The total coordinate sum is \(N x_1\) plus a weighted sum of the gaps, where the weight of gap \(k\) is \(N-k\), decreasing as \(k\) increases. To minimize the sum, assign the sorted gaps of each parity in increasing order to the increasing positions of that parity, equivalently putting larger gaps farther to the right where their weights are smaller.
