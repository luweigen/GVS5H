Fix the second cut j and precompute the distinct count of the suffix A_{j+1..N}. For each j, the remaining task is to maximize over i<j the sum of the prefix distinct count L[i] and the distinct count of the middle segment A_{i+1..j}. As j increases, maintain the value V_i = L[i] + distinct(A_{i+1..j}) for all active i. When A_j is added, V_i increases by 1 exactly for i at or after the previous occurrence of A_j, so perform a range add over those i and insert the new i=j-1 with value L[j-1]+1. A lazy segment tree gives the maximum V_i for each j in O(log N). Finally take the maximum over j=2..N-1 of that maximum plus the precomputed suffix distinct count.
