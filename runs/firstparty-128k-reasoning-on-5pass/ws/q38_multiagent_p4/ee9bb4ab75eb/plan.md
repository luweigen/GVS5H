Build a trie of all words, incrementing subtree counts, and perform a DFS that assigns each word occurrence a leaf position and records each node's depth and leaf interval. A prefix length d is feasible after removing index i iff some trie node at depth d contains at least k remaining strings, i.e., its count is at least k+1, or its count is exactly k and i lies outside that node's interval. Compute global_best as the maximum depth of nodes with count at least k+1, which are valid for every removal. For nodes with count exactly k, treat their intervals as weighted intervals and compute, for each leaf position, the maximum depth of an interval not covering it using prefix maxima over right endpoints and suffix maxima over left endpoints. Finally, if n-1 < k return all zeros; otherwise answer[i] is the maximum of global_best and the best non-covering exact-k interval depth at the position of words[i].
