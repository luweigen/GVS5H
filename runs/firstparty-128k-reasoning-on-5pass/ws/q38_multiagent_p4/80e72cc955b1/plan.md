Each number x needs a fixed number t(x) of selections, where t(x)=k for 4^{k-1} <= x < 4^k. For a query [l,r], let S be the sum of t(x) over the range and M be the maximum t(x), which is t(r). One operation can contribute at most one selection to two elements, so the answer is at least ceil(S/2) and at least M; these lower bounds are simultaneously achievable by scheduling the required selections as unit tasks over that many rounds. Compute S with a prefix function over the power-of-4 intervals, since r <= 1e9 gives at most 15 intervals. Then return the sum of max(M, (S+1)//2) over all queries.
