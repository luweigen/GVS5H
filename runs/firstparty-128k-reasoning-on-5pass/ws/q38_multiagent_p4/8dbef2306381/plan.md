Compress the huge row to O(MB) critical good positions: 1, N, and all good positions within B of either end of a bad interval. Any move that crosses a bad interval must have both endpoints critical, while moves that stay inside one good segment can be summarized without enumerating intermediate squares. Inside a good segment, a later position v is reachable from an earlier reachable position u iff there exists k with kA ≤ v-u ≤ kB, an O(1) integer test. Sweep the critical positions left to right, maintaining reachable critical positions per good segment, and propagate reachability by this internal test plus direct jumps of length A..B to critical positions. Finally, report Yes iff N is marked reachable; the total work is O(MB²) with B≤20.
