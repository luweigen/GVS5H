Simulate the M moves to obtain the final coordinates, and record each move as an inclusive axis-aligned interval on its fixed coordinate. Group horizontal intervals by y and vertical intervals by x, sort and merge overlapping/touching intervals in each group so that every traversed line segment is represented without duplicates. For each house, check whether its x-coordinate lies in the merged horizontal intervals for its y or its y-coordinate lies in the merged vertical intervals for its x. Count houses satisfying either condition, which automatically handles houses visited by multiple segments or by both orientations. This yields O((N+M) log(N+M)) time and O(N+M) memory, with 64-bit integers for coordinates.
