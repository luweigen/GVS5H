Model a deletion of value `x` by marking all indices with `nums[i] = x` as inactive zeros and keeping all other indices active; the best subarray after deletion is the maximum sum of a contiguous segment containing at least one active index. Maintain this with a segment tree that stores total sum and the best prefix, suffix, and subarray that contain at least one active index, supporting point toggles. Build the tree with all indices active to get the no-operation answer. For each value with frequency less than `n` (only negative values need be tried for an improvement), toggle its indices inactive, take the root's best, then toggle them back. This gives `O(n log n)` time and `O(n)` memory.
