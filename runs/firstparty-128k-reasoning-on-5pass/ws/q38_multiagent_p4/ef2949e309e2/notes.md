- **Anchor:** Every length-5 subsequence has exactly one third chosen index k. Counting by k with two chosen indices before and two after partitions the answer, so there is no double counting.
- **Sweep state:** Compress values to integer ids. Maintain left and right count arrays. At step k, first remove nums[k] from right, so left holds indices < k and right holds indices > k. Process only 2 <= k <= n-3, then add nums[k] to left.
- **Notation:** For x = nums[k], cx_l and cx_r are counts of x on the two sides; ln and rn are counts of non-x indices on the two sides. C2(a) = a*(a-1)//2.
- **Middle value frequency at least 3:** If x appears 3, 4, or 5 times in the chosen five, it is automatically the unique mode. t_ge3 counts side choices with at least two extra x's: total C2(k)*C2(n-1-k) minus no extra x C2(ln)*C2(rn) minus exactly one extra x cx_l*ln*C2(rn) + C2(ln)*cx_r*rn.
- **Middle value frequency exactly 2:** Exactly one extra x is chosen. The remaining three side elements are non-x and must be pairwise distinct; otherwise another value ties or beats x.
- **Distinct pair aggregates:** Over non-x values, P_L = (ln^2 - sum l^2)/2 and P_R = (rn^2 - sum r^2)/2 count unordered index pairs with distinct values on the left and right. Also maintain cross1 = sum l*r, cross2 = sum l*r^2, cross3 = sum l^2*r.
- **t2 formula:** If the extra x is on the left, choose a left x and a left non-x value u, then a right distinct pair avoiding u; summing over u gives cx_l*(P_R*ln - rn*cross1 + cross2). If the extra x is on the right, symmetrically cx_r*(P_L*rn - ln*cross1 + cross3). Add both parts.
- **Complexity:** For each k, one loop over m distinct values computes the aggregates, giving O(n*m) time and O(m) memory. With n <= 1000 this is at most about one million iterations.
- **Edge cases:** m = 1 makes all cross sums zero and t2 zero; ln or rn zero makes pair counts zero; n = 5 processes only k = 2; negative values are handled by compression; all-same arrays give C(n,5); all-distinct arrays give 0.
- **Validation:** The included brute checker enumerates all 5-index combinations and checks that the middle value is the sole maximum frequency. Self-tests cover the three examples, all-same n=5..10, all-distinct, hand cases, random small arrays, duplicate-heavy arrays, and negative-value arrays.
- **Pitfalls avoided:** The middle index is excluded from both sides; x is excluded from non-x aggregates; opposite-side pairs containing the single non-x value are subtracted; index pairs, not value pairs, are counted; modulo is applied after exact per-k arithmetic.
