Count each valid 5-subsequence by its middle original index k, which must have exactly two chosen indices before and two after. For x = nums[k], let a and b be the numbers of chosen x's from the left and right, so the total frequency of x is 1+a+b. When this total is 3, 4, or 5, the middle value is automatically the unique mode, and the count is a sum of small binomial products. When the total is 2, the three non-x chosen indices must have pairwise distinct values; maintain left/right frequency aggregates while sweeping k to compute the distinct 1-left/2-right and 2-left/1-right triple counts in O(1). Sum the four case contributions over k=2..n-3 modulo 10^9+7.
