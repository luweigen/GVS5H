Pair price is the maximum of three coordinate sums, so label each pair by a coordinate attaining that maximum; the total price is then the sum of the labeled coordinate values of the 2K used cakes. Conversely, any 2K cakes labeled X/Y/Z with each label count even can be paired inside each label and gives at least that sum, so the problem is to choose exactly M=2K labeled cakes with even label counts maximizing the sum. For a penalty λ per chosen cake, each cake has four choices (unused, X, Y, Z) with adjusted values, and a 4-state parity DP gives the best adjusted value and, among ties, the maximum chosen count. The chosen count is nonincreasing in λ, so binary search the largest λ with count at least M (WQS/Aliens trick) and recover the exact answer as dp_value + λ*M. This runs in O(N log 1e9 * 16) over all test cases.
