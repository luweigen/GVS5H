Use the symmetry of XOR and combinations: choosing K elements is equivalent either to enumerating the K chosen elements or, when K is large, enumerating the N-K omitted elements and XORing them out of the total XOR. Since C(N,K)=C(N,N-K)≤10^6, the smaller of K and N-K is at most 11, so direct enumeration of all combinations of that size is feasible. Compute the XOR of all A once. If K≤N-K, maximize the XOR over all K-tuples; otherwise maximize total_xor XOR (XOR of an (N-K)-tuple). Handle the edge case N-K=0 by returning total_xor. The resulting algorithm runs in O(C(N,K)·min(K,N-K)) time and O(N) memory.
