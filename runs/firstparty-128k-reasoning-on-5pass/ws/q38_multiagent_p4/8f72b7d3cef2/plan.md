Use the fact that a slime can only be blocked by a strictly larger slime, so each slime belongs to the maximal interval bounded by the nearest strictly larger slimes; equal maxima in such an interval form one node. Build a laminar tree of these intervals: a node's parent is the interval of the smaller of its two bounding larger slimes (or the only one). A slime can absorb its entire node iff it has an adjacent smaller slime inside the node; otherwise it is stuck at its own size. If it can absorb its node, its size becomes the node's total, and it can move to the parent exactly when that total is strictly larger than the parent's maximum. Precompute for each node the final total by climbing parents while total > parent max, then answer each slime from its node and the adjacent-smaller flag.
