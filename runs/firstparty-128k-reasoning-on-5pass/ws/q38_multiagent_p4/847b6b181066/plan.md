Model each smoke particle by its birth time and use prefix wind displacements. Let P_t be the coordinate reached by the initial smoke after t winds, with P_0=(0,0). A new particle is born at time g>0 exactly when P_g is a coordinate not seen among P_0,...,P_{g-1}, because then (0,0) is empty after the wind. At time t, target T=(R,C) is occupied iff some born particle satisfies P_t-P_g=T, i.e. P_g=P_t-T. Since the first occurrence of any prefix coordinate is a birth time, this is equivalent to P_t-T having appeared in the prefix positions up to time t-1. Therefore scan S once, maintain current P_t and a hash set of seen prefix coordinates, output 1 iff (P_t-T) is in the set, then insert P_t.
