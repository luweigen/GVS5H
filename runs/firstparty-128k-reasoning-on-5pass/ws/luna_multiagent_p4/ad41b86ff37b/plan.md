Characterize a Snowflake Tree by its center: for a chosen center \(c\), each selected neighbor is a second-layer vertex, and it must have at least \(y\) other neighbors that can serve as leaves. For fixed \(c\) and \(y\), the maximum number of second-layer vertices is the number of neighbors \(v\) with \(\deg(v)-1\ge y\), giving a retained size of \(1+(y+1)\cdot\text{count}\). For every vertex, sort the values \(\deg(v)-1\) of its neighbors and test all relevant thresholds \(y\), maximizing the retained size in \(O(N\log N)\) total time. The answer is \(N\) minus the largest retained Snowflake size.
