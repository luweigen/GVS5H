A 400 number is exactly the square of an integer whose prime factorization contains two distinct primes, because every prime exponent in the original number must be positive and even. Precompute all integers \(x \le 10^6\) with exactly two distinct prime factors, store \(x^2\), and sort the resulting list. For each query \(A\), binary-search the largest stored square not exceeding \(A\).
