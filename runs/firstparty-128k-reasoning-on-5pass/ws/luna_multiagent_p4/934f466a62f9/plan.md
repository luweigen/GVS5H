Reformulate each selected pair by assigning it the coordinate (beauty, tastiness, or popularity) that attains its price; then its contribution becomes the sum of that coordinate over its two cakes. This turns the problem into assigning exactly \(2K\) distinct cakes to three coordinate groups, with every group having even size, maximizing the sum of assigned coordinate values. Develop an efficient method for this three-way assignment with parity constraints, prove its equivalence to the original matching problem, and implement it within the total \(N\le 10^5\) limit. Validate the method on edge cases such as duplicate cakes, zero values, and groups of size zero.
