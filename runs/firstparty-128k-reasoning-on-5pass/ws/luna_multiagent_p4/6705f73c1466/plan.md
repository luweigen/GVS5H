Let the positions of all `1`s be \(p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_{k-1}\). If the final contiguous block starts at position \(l\), the \(j\)-th `1` must move to \(l+j\), costing \(|p_j-(l+j)|\) adjacent swaps. Define \(q_j=p_j-j\); minimizing the total cost over \(l\) is equivalent to choosing the median of the \(q_j\) values. Compute the sum of distances to the median in \(O(N)\) time and use Python integers for the result.
