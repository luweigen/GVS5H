Derive a greedy characterization of the maximum matching for any sorted subarray, using the fact that an optimal solution pairs smaller mochi with larger mochi in order. Develop an offline or persistent data structure that answers the greedy matching count for all arbitrary intervals \([L,R]\) in \(O(\log N)\) or \(O(\log^2 N)\) per query after \(O(N\log N)\) preprocessing. Prove both the greedy optimality and the correctness of the range-query structure, including duplicate sizes and boundary cases. Implement the chosen method with 64-bit-safe comparisons such as \(2A_i\le A_j\).
