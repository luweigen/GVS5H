- **BFS layering:** Every connected graph has a unique shortest-distance layering \(L_0,L_1,\ldots\) from vertex \(1\), with \(L_0=\{1\}\). For layer sizes \(1,s_1,\ldots,s_d\), edges between nonconsecutive layers are absent, edges inside layers are arbitrary, and every vertex in \(L_i\) must have a nonempty neighborhood in \(L_{i-1}\).
- **Generating function:** Introduce \(y=1+x\), where \(x\) marks edges. A layer of size \(b\) contributes \(y^{\binom b2}\), while the edges from a preceding layer of size \(a\) contribute \((y^a-1)^b\). Expanding the latter gives \(\sum_{j=0}^b(-1)^{b-j}\binom bj y^{aj}\), so every transition is sparse in the \(y\)-basis.
- **Label weights:** For a fixed ordered sequence of layer sizes, the number of assignments of labels other than vertex \(1\) is \((N-1)!/\prod_i s_i!\). The factor \(1/s_i!\) is included during each transition, and the final factor \((N-1)!\) is applied afterward.
- **Dynamic programming state:** At total assigned size \(t\), retain the number \(q\) of vertices in even-indexed layers, the current layer size \(a\), and the parity of the current layer index. The polynomial stores coefficients by exponent of \(y\). States violating \(q\le N/2\) or \(t-q\le N/2\) are discarded.
- **Final conversion:** If the aggregate polynomial is \(\sum_e c_e y^e\), then its \(x^m\) coefficient is \(\sum_{e\ge m}c_e\binom em\). The required outputs are coefficients for \(m=N-1,\ldots,\binom N2\).
- **Complexity:** The degree is at most \(\binom N2\le435\). Each layer transition has at most \(b+1\le31\) terms. The resulting compressed DP is practical for \(N\le30\); modular reduction is deferred between DP levels to reduce Python overhead.
