Use the identity \(f(x)=x\left(1-\sum_{k\ge1}2^{-k}[2^k\mid x]\right)\), where only powers of two up to \(2\max(A_i)\) matter. The unrestricted sum of \(A_i+A_j\) over \(i\le j\) is computed directly. For every power \(2^k\), compute the sum of \(A_i+A_j\) over pairs whose sum is divisible by \(2^k\) by grouping values by residue modulo \(2^k\) and matching complementary residues. Combine these values with coefficient \(-2^{-k}\), using exact integer arithmetic by multiplying the entire formula by a sufficiently large power of two. The resulting algorithm runs in \(O(N\log \max A_i)\) time and \(O(N)\) memory.
