Model every sequence of adjacent removals as a noncrossing matching of indices, with at most one unmatched index when \(N\) is odd. Derive a compact characterization of the maximum-weight noncrossing matching for weights \(|A_i-A_j|\), avoiding the infeasible interval DP. Prove the characterization and reduce it to a linear or \(O(N\log N)\) scan using prefix/suffix extrema or equivalent dynamic states. Implement the resulting algorithm with 64-bit integer arithmetic and verify it on edge cases, including equal values, odd lengths, and strictly increasing or decreasing sequences.
