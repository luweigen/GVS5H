View each product’s purchases as a sequence of marginal costs \(P_i(1), P_i(3), P_i(5),\ldots\), since the \(k\)-th unit increases the total cost by \(P_i(2k-1)\). For a marginal-cost threshold \(T\), compute how many increments from each product have cost at most \(T\), and thus the total cost of taking all such increments. Binary search the largest feasible \(T\) whose accumulated cost does not exceed \(M\). Finally, use the remaining budget to buy as many units as possible at the smallest next marginal cost.
