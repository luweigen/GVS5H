Maintain, for each divisor \(d \in [1,9]\), counts of residues of all substrings ending at the previous position modulo \(d\). When appending a digit \(x\), transform every previous residue \(r\) into \((10r+x)\bmod d\), and add the one-digit substring separately. If the current digit is nonzero, add the count of newly formed substrings whose residue modulo that digit is zero. This gives \(O(45n)\) time and \(O(45)\) space, suitable for \(n \le 10^5\).
