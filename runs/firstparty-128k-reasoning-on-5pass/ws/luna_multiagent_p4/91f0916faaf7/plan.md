Factor every \(A_i\) into prime powers; for each prime \(p\), the reduced numerator/denominator of \(S_i/S_{i+1}\) must assign the entire power \(p^{e_i}\) to exactly one endpoint, giving an exponent difference of either \(+e_i\) or \(-e_i\). Choices for distinct primes are independent, and the global gcd condition is enforced by subtracting the minimum exponent over all positions for each prime, so the total answer factors into a product of per-prime contributions. For each prime, use dynamic programming over the current exponent height after normalization to minimum zero; when a transition goes below zero, shift all previous heights upward and multiply the accumulated score by the corresponding power of \(p\). The maximum height is at most the sum of exponents for that prime, making an \(O(N\sum e_i)\) DP per prime feasible.
