Group foods by vitamin and independently compute, for every calorie capacity up to \(X\), the maximum intake obtainable for that vitamin using 0/1 knapsack DP. For a candidate target \(T\), find the minimum calories required for each vitamin to reach at least \(T\), and check whether their sum is at most \(X\). Since feasibility is monotone in \(T\), binary search the largest feasible target. The total complexity is \(O(NX + X\log M)\), where \(M\) is the maximum possible vitamin intake.
