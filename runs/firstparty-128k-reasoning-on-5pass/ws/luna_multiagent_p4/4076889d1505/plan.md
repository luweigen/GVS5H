Use the construction \(A=N+1\) and \(M=N^2\). By the binomial theorem, \((N+1)^k \equiv 1+kN \pmod{N^2}\), so \(M\) divides \(A^k-1\) exactly when \(N\) divides \(k\); therefore the smallest valid exponent is \(N\).
