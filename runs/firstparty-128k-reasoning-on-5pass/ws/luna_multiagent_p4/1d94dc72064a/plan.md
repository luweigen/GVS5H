Model the game by separating unvisited indices from already visited indices: moves on visited indices act as expendable “pass” moves, while visiting a new index reduces the number of unvisited indices and creates \(A_i-1\) future pass moves. Derive the winning/losing-state recurrence, then simplify it to a characterization depending only on the relevant parity/count information or another compact invariant. Prove that characterization by induction and implement the resulting \(O(N\log N)\) or better algorithm for \(N\le 2\times10^5\).
