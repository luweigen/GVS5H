Model the required flips as two groups: positions changing from 0 to 1 and positions changing from 1 to 0. Any unnecessary extra flips can be removed, so each mismatching position is flipped exactly once. The cost of every flip is the current weighted sum of active 1-bits, so an optimal order can be determined by pairwise exchange arguments. Derive the ordering rule between flip types and sort the relevant positions by their weights to accumulate the minimum cost efficiently in \(O(N \log N)\).
