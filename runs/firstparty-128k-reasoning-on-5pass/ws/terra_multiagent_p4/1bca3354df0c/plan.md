Model each connected component as a bipartite graph with two color classes. A legal move either adds a missing edge between opposite color classes within one component, or connects two different components in one of two valid bipartite orientations. The eventual graph must become a complete bipartite graph over all vertices, and the total number of remaining moves is invariant: it equals \(\lfloor N^2/4\rfloor-M\). Therefore the winner depends only on the parity of this value.
