Model sorting as resolving inversions, where swapping adjacent positions \(i,i+1\) costs \(i\). Derive a characterization of the unavoidable cost contributed by elements crossing each boundary, and show an optimal sequence attains this lower bound. Use an efficient data structure or combinatorial counting method to sum the needed contributions over all boundaries in \(O(N \log N)\) or better. Carefully validate the formula against permutations where elements move both left and right.
