Interpret every valid coloring as a down-left closed set: if a cell is black, then every cell above it and to its left must also be black. Therefore, impossibility occurs exactly when a prescribed black cell is weakly southeast of a prescribed white cell, since the black cell would force that white cell to be black. Sort the prescribed cells by row and sweep through black cells, maintaining the minimum column among white cells seen so far; if this minimum is at most the black cell’s column, answer `No`, otherwise answer `Yes`. This runs in \(O(M\log M)\) time and \(O(M)\) memory.
