Derive a structural characterization of an optimal matching by exploiting that each pair’s value is the maximum of three coordinate-wise sums. Reduce the problem to selecting a small set of candidate cakes or edges, then solve the resulting matching/selection problem efficiently for all \(K\) pairs. Prove the reduction and its handling of ties and duplicate cake values. Implement the algorithm in \(O(N\log N)\) or comparable time per test case, using 64-bit integers and respecting the summed-\(N\) constraint.
