Use a priority queue to simulate absorption in increasing order of slime strength, starting from Takahashi’s cell. Maintain the current strength and a visited grid; whenever a cell is absorbed, add all unvisited neighboring cells to the priority queue. Repeatedly absorb the minimum-strength adjacent slime only if its strength is strictly less than `current_strength / X`, equivalently `strength * X < current_strength`; otherwise no queued cell can be absorbed and the process stops. Since each cell is inserted and removed at most once, the algorithm runs in `O(HW log(HW))`.
