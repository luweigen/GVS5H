Model the process as shortest-path search over ordered pairs of distinct vertices, where a transition moves exactly one piece along an edge while preserving distinctness. Since the full state space can be quadratic, derive and prove a graph-theoretic characterization that reduces the answer to a small number of ordinary shortest-path computations, exploiting the fact that each piece must travel from one terminal to the other. Handle separately the impossible cases, especially graphs where the pieces cannot pass each other, and verify the formula against the samples and edge cases. Implement the resulting linear or near-linear algorithm with BFS and reconstruct only the distances needed.
