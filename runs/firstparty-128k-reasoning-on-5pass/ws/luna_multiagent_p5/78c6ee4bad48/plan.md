Represent the configuration by consecutive gaps \(g_i=X_{i+1}-X_i\). An operation on four consecutive pieces reverses the corresponding three gaps, effectively swapping \(g_i\) and \(g_{i+2}\) while leaving \(g_{i+1}\) unchanged. Therefore, gaps with odd indices can be permuted arbitrarily among themselves, and gaps with even indices can likewise be permuted independently. The total coordinate sum is \(N X_1+\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}(N-i)g_i\), so assign the smallest gaps to the largest weights separately within each parity class.
