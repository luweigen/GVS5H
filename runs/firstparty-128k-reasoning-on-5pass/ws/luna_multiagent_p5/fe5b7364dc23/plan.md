Represent each subarray sum as a difference of prefix sums, so the target becomes \(\sum_{0\leq x<y\leq N}(P_y-P_x)^K\). Expand by the binomial theorem and maintain, while scanning prefix sums, the sums of powers \(P_x^j\) for \(0\leq j\leq K\). Each new prefix \(P_y\) contributes its expanded difference against all previous prefixes in \(O(K)\), giving total \(O(NK)\) time and \(O(K)\) memory modulo 998244353.
