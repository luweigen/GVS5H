Let \(H\) be the common final sum of each upper/lower tooth pair; the total grinding cost is \(\sum_i(U_i+D_i)-NH\), so we must maximize feasible \(H\). For a fixed \(H\), the final upper tooth \(A_i\) must lie in \([\max(0,H-D_i),\min(U_i,H)]\), while consecutive \(A_i\) values must differ by at most \(X\). Check feasibility by propagating the reachable interval of \(A_i\) from left to right, then binary-search the largest feasible \(H\), using 64-bit integers for the answer.
