Use square-root decomposition on the prefix length \(X\). Partition \(A\) into blocks and precompute, for every block, the prefix sums over \(B\) of \(\sum_{i\in\text{block}} |A_i-B_j|\). For a query \((X,Y)\), sum the precomputed contributions of all complete \(A\)-blocks before \(X\), then directly process the at most one remaining partial block; each direct contribution can be computed from a sorted/prefix-sum structure for \(B_1,\ldots,B_Y\). Choose a block size around \(\sqrt N\), giving roughly \(O((N^2/S)+KS\log N)\) time and \(O(NS)\) preprocessing/storage, with 64-bit answers.
