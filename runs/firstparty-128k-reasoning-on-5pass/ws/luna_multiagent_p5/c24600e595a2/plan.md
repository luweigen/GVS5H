Model each operation as moving between bitmask states, where the cost of a state is the weighted sum of its 1-bits. First characterize the structure of an optimal path, including whether temporarily flipping bits that already match can ever help and how flips should be ordered by their weights. Reduce the resulting choice of temporary flips and ordering to an efficient dynamic program or greedy/sorting procedure, then implement it in \(O(N\log N)\) or better with 64-bit integer arithmetic. Validate the recurrence and edge cases such as \(A=B\), all-zero/all-one states, and large costs.
