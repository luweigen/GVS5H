Let the positions of all `1`s be \(p_0,p_1,\dots,p_{K-1}\). If the final contiguous block starts at position \(x\), the minimum swaps required is \(\sum_i |p_i-(x+i)|\), since each `1` must move to one of the consecutive target positions. Transforming with \(q_i=p_i-i\), this becomes minimizing \(\sum_i |q_i-x|\), achieved when \(x\) is a median of the \(q_i\). Compute the transformed positions, choose the median, and sum the absolute deviations in \(O(N)\) time.
