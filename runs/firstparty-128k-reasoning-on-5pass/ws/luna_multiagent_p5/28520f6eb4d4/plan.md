For each building \(i\), determine the smallest observer height threshold at which its top can be seen without being blocked by any earlier building \(j<i\). The blocking threshold for a pair is the \(y\)-intercept of the line through \((X_j,H_j)\) and \((X_i,H_i)\), namely \((X_iH_j-X_jH_i)/(X_i-X_j)\). The answer is the maximum such threshold over all pairs, with negative results converted to \(-1\); equality remains impossible because touching another building counts as intersection. Compute this maximum in \(O(N\log N)\) using a convex hull of prior buildings and tangent/binary-search queries.
