Model the sequence of removals as a noncrossing matching of indices, with at most one unmatched element when \(N\) is odd. Derive a structural simplification for maximizing the sum of absolute differences, avoiding the usual \(O(N^2)\) interval DP. Prove the resulting formula and implement it in \(O(N)\) time with 64-bit integer arithmetic. Validate the reasoning against parity, equal-value, and sample-like cases.
