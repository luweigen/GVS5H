Simulate Santa's moves to obtain the final position and record every horizontal/vertical segment as an interval on its fixed coordinate. Group horizontal intervals by y and vertical intervals by x, then sort and merge intervals in each group to form disjoint covered ranges. For each house, test whether its x lies in the merged horizontal range for its y or its y lies in the merged vertical range for its x. Count houses satisfying either condition once, using binary search over merged intervals. This runs in O((N+M) log M) and avoids overflow by using 64-bit/Python integers.
