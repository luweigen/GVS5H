When a parent selects an edge to child v, the best contribution from v is max(dp0[v], dp3[v]); if the highest vertex has only one selected child, that child must provide a finite dp3 state so that at least one degree-4 vertex exists. For every u, use the largest one and four child contributions to form final candidates whose highest vertex is u: one child with finite dp3, or four children with their best contributions.
