Convert the problem from coordinates to the N-1 positive gaps between consecutive pieces. Show that the operation on four consecutive pieces with gaps u,v,w replaces them by w,v,u, i.e. it swaps the two outer gaps g_i and g_{i+2}. Thus reachable configurations are exactly all permutations of the initial gaps that preserve gap-index parity, since distance-2 swaps generate adjacent swaps within each parity class. The total coordinate sum equals N*X_1 plus the weighted gap sum Σ (N-k)g_k, where weights strictly decrease with k. For each parity independently, minimize by the rearrangement inequality: sort that parity's gaps ascending and pair them with its weights descending, then add N*X_1.
