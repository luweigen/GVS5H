Normalize each conflicting pair to an interval [a, b] with a < b and sort the intervals by b. For a fixed right endpoint r, a subarray ending at r is invalid exactly when its left endpoint is at most the maximum a among intervals with b <= r, so total invalid subarrays is the sum of this prefix maximum over r. Sweep b in increasing order, maintaining the largest and second-largest active a values, the count of the largest value, and the id of the unique largest interval. For each constant segment of r between consecutive b values, add max1 * length to the total invalid count and, if the largest value occurs once, add (max1 - max2) * length to that interval's removal delta. The best removal increases the valid count by the maximum delta, so return total_subarrays - total_invalid + max_delta.
