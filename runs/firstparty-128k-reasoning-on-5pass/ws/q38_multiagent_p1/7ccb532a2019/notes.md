- **Fixed target cost:** For fixed final counts, the independent delete/insert cost is `sum |c[i] - f[i]|`. A one-step forward change from `i` to `i+1` saves exactly 1 when it replaces a deletion at `i` and an insertion at `i+1`. Useful adjacent edges cannot be adjacent to each other because a letter cannot be both surplus and deficit relative to its fixed final count. Therefore the maximum saving is `sum_i min(max(0, c[i] - f[i]), max(0, f[i+1] - c[i+1]))`. Longer forward moves cost at least delete+insert and give no strict saving.

The DP tracks `(previous choice, has_chosen)` using three reachable states: `(0,0)`, `(0,1)`, `(1,1)`. Transition adds the current unary cost and subtracts the adjacent saving when the current letter is chosen.
