Treat the k-th unit of product i as a marginal cost P_i(2k-1); these marginal costs are increasing for each product, so an optimal purchase is a prefix of the globally sorted marginal costs. For a threshold T, product i contributes c_i=(floor(T/P_i)+1)//2 units with marginal cost at most T, and their total cost is P_i c_i^2. Binary search the largest integer T such that S(T)=sum_i P_i c_i^2 is at most M, using a safe upper bound and capping the sum at M+1 for early exit. At this T, all increments with cost <=T are affordable, and the remaining budget buys floor((M-S(T))/(T+1)) increments of the next marginal value T+1. This runs in O(N log U) time, where U is about 10^18 or tighter, easily within limits.
