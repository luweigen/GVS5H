Reframe a 400 number as a perfect square \(m^2\) where \(m\) has exactly two distinct prime factors. Since \(A \le 10^{12}\), we only need \(m \le 10^6\). Precompute all integers up to \(10^6\) with exactly two distinct prime factors using a smallest-prime-factor sieve. For each query, take \(r = \lfloor \sqrt{A} \rfloor\), binary search for the largest valid \(m \le r\), and output \(m^2\). This gives fast preprocessing and \(O(\log M)\) per query.
