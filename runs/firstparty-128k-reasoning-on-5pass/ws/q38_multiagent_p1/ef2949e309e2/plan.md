Iterate over every index that can serve as the third element of a 5-subsequence, i.e., with at least two indices before and after. For the middle value x, split the remaining choices into two left indices and two right indices and count valid pairs using frequencies on each side. A 5-subsequence is valid iff x appears at least 3 times total, or x appears exactly 2 times and the other three chosen values are pairwise distinct. Count the first case with simple combinations of left/right pairs by their number of x's, and count the second case with distinct-pair counts plus cross-frequency sums so each middle index is processed efficiently. Accumulate all counts modulo 10^9+7, yielding an O(n^2) solution for n<=1000.
