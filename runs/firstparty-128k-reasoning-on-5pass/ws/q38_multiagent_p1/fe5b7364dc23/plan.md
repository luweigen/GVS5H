Let S_i be prefix sums modulo 998244353. Each subarray sum is S_r - S_{l-1}, so the answer is sum over 0 <= i < j <= N of (S_j - S_i)^K. Expand (S_j - S_i)^K by the binomial theorem and separate terms into S_j^t times a sum of previous S_i^{K-t}. Maintain running sums of powers of all previously seen prefix sums, then each new prefix contributes in O(K). This yields O(NK) time and O(K) memory, easily within constraints.
