Convert each person to an interval [l,r] of prefix-sum indices (l=min(S,T)-1, r=max(S,T)-1) and a sign (+ if S<T, - if S>T). The stamina requirements become endpoint equalities and strict inequalities on prefix sums, and for this interval structure a set is feasible iff it contains no conflicting pair. Two persons conflict exactly when their intervals share a left or right endpoint, or when they cross (l_i<l_j<r_i<r_j or symmetric) and have the same sign. Thus a query [L,R] is Yes iff no conflicting pair (i,j) with L<=i<j<=R exists. Compute for each i the smallest conflicting j>i, take suffix minima, and answer queries in O(1); crossing conflicts are found by two offline sweeps with segment trees and lazy heaps.
