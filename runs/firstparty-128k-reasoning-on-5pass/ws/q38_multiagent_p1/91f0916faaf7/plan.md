For each prime p, let e_i = v_p(A_i). A good sequence corresponds independently for each p to a nonnegative integer height sequence c_i with c_i - c_{i+1} = ±e_i (or 0) and min c_i = 0; the p-part of the score is p^{sum c_i}. Because choices for different primes are independent, the total answer is the product over primes of the sum of p^{sum c_i} over all such height sequences. For a fixed p, total step weight W = sum e_i bounds all valid heights, so we can DP left to right over height 0..W, tracking whether a zero has already occurred and multiplying by p^{new height} at each step. Sum over all primes appearing in A_i; primes with W = 0 contribute 1, and the total complexity is O(N * sum_i Ω(A_i)), which is small under the constraints.
