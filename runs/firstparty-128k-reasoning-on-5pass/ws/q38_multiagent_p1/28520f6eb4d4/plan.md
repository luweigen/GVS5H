Model each building as a vertical segment and note that, for a fixed target building, aiming at its top maximizes the line height over every earlier building. For a pair (j,i), the critical observer height at which the line from (0,y) to the top of i just touches the top of j is the y-intercept of the line through (X_j,H_j) and (X_i,H_i). Thus the desired height is the maximum of these intercepts over all pairs, or -1 if that maximum is negative. For any three x-sorted points, if the middle point lies below the line through the endpoints the left pair has a larger intercept, and if it lies above the right pair has a larger intercept, so the global maximum is always attained by adjacent buildings. Therefore scan adjacent pairs, keep the maximum rational intercept exactly, and output -1 for a negative maximum or the decimal value otherwise.
