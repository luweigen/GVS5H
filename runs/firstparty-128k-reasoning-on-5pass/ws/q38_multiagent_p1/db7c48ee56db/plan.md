Use the symmetry between choosing K elements and omitting N-K elements. Let m = min(K, N-K); since C(N,K) = C(N,N-K) <= 10^6, enumerating all subsets of size m is feasible. If K <= N-K, enumerate all K chosen elements and compute their XOR directly. If K > N-K, enumerate all N-K omitted elements, compute their XOR, and derive the chosen XOR as total_xor ^ omitted_xor.
