Valid grids are exactly Ferrers diagrams anchored at the top-left, so they can be represented by row prefix lengths R_x that are nonincreasing with x. A fixed black cell (x,y) forces R_x >= y, and a fixed white cell forces R_x <= y-1; combine all constraints per row into lower and upper bounds. Since N is huge, keep only rows that have at least one fixed cell; unconstrained rows can always be filled between neighboring constrained values. Check feasibility of a nonincreasing sequence over the sorted constrained rows by propagating the minimal required value from bottom to top. If at any row the required value exceeds its upper bound, answer No; otherwise Yes.
