Use dynamic programming on diagonals, tracking only length parity because the required next value after a segment of length L is 2 when L is odd and 0 when L is even. For every cell and each of the four diagonal travel directions, compute the longest valid straight segment ending there for odd and even lengths by scanning each diagonal in the direction of travel. Also compute, for every cell, direction, and expected next value (2 or 0), the longest alternating continuation starting just after that cell. A V is formed at a turn cell by pairing a straight-ending segment in direction d with the continuation in clockwise(d), choosing the continuation's first value from the ending segment's parity. The answer is the maximum over straight segments, one-turn combinations, and single-cell starts, giving O(nm) time with compact O(nm) storage and returning 0 if no 1 exists.
