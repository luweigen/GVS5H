Model Takahashi as a growing connected region starting from cell (P,Q), where his strength is the sum of the strengths of all cells in the region. A neighboring cell can be absorbed exactly when X * S_cell < current total, and because all strengths are positive, absorbing any eligible cell only increases the threshold and never makes future absorptions harder. Maintain the boundary of the region in a min-heap ordered by cell strength, so the easiest-to-absorb boundary slime is checked first. Repeatedly pop the smallest unabsorbed boundary cell; if it satisfies the strict inequality, absorb it, add its strength to the total, and push its unvisited neighbors; otherwise no boundary cell can be absorbed and the process stops. This greedy expansion yields the maximum possible final strength in O(HW log(HW)) time.
