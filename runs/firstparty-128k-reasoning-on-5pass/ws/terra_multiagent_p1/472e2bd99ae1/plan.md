Sort all three sequences in descending order. Since every value is positive, the expression \(ab+bc+ca\) is monotone non-increasing when any sorted-array index is increased. Use a max-heap initialized with index triple \((0,0,0)\); repeatedly extract the current largest value and push its three neighboring triples obtained by increasing one index, while deduplicating triples with a visited set. The K-th extracted value is the answer, and at most \(3K\) states are generated.
