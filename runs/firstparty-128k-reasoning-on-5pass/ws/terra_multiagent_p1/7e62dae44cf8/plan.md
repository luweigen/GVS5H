Maintain all currently adjacent unabsorbed slimes in a min-heap by strength; whenever the smallest one satisfies `strength * X < current_strength`, it is always safe and beneficial to absorb it. After absorption, add its unvisited grid neighbors to the heap, and continue until the minimum boundary slime cannot be absorbed.
