Express each concatenated permutation as the sum of each value multiplied by a power of 10 determined by the total digit lengths of elements after it. By symmetry, fix a value \(x\): among all permutations, every subset of the other values can appear after \(x\), with factorial multiplicities for their internal orders. Use a DP over total digit length to sum the required powers of 10 across all possible suffix subsets, then multiply each contribution by the appropriate factorial factors. Since values \(1\) through \(N\) have only \(O(\log N)\) digit-length groups, optimize the subset DP using counts per digit length rather than individual values.
