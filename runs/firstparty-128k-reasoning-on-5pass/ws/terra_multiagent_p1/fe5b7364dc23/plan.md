Rewrite each subarray sum as a difference of prefix sums: \((S_r-S_{l-1})^K\). Expand this with the binomial theorem, so for every right endpoint \(r\), its contribution can be computed from maintained sums of powers of earlier prefix sums. Since \(K\le 10\), maintain \(\sum_{j<r} S_j^t\) for every \(0\le t\le K\), updating each in \(O(K)\). This yields \(O(NK)\) time and \(O(K)\) extra space modulo \(998244353\).
