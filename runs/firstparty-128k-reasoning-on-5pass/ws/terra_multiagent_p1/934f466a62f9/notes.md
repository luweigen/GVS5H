- **Reduction to labels:** For every pair, choose a coordinate attaining its maximum price. Label both endpoints by that coordinate. The total pair price then equals the sum of the labeled coordinate values. Conversely, any selection of cakes labeled by coordinates with an even number of cakes per coordinate can be paired within each label class, and each pair's actual price is at least its labeled-coordinate sum. Thus the original optimum equals the maximum total labeled value when exactly `2K` cakes are selected and every label count is even.
- **Baseline:** Let `M = 2K`. Select the `M` cakes with greatest `v_i = max(X_i,Y_i,Z_i)`, and give each its fixed arbitrary maximizing label. This is an unconstrained optimal assignment.
- **Repair actions:** Relative to this baseline, an omitted baseline cake plus an inserted outside cake is an exchange action. A retained baseline cake changing from its baseline maximizing label to another label is a relabel action. Each action changes label parity by one of the three nonzero vectors in the two-dimensional even-parity vector space. Its loss is nonnegative: relabel loss is `v_i - coordinate_i`, and exchange loss is `v_removed - coordinate_inserted`, nonnegative because every baseline maximum is at least every outside maximum.
- **At most two actions:** If there are more than two nonzero-parity actions, their parity vectors are linearly dependent over XOR. A nonempty subset has XOR zero and can be deleted while preserving both cardinality and final parity, without decreasing value. Repeating yields an optimal solution using at most two actions total; in particular, at most two exchanges.
- **Outside candidate bound:** An optimal solution uses at most two outside cakes. For each assigned coordinate, only the best two outside cakes by that coordinate need be retained. If an outside cake assigned coordinate `d` is not among these two, one of the two is unused by the other possible outside selection and can replace it without hurting feasibility or value. Their union has at most six cakes.
- **DP:** Process all `M` baseline cakes, tracking only excluded count `q <= 2` and a 3-bit parity mask. Each cake is either excluded or selected under one of three labels. Separately process the at-most-six retained outside candidates, tracking selected count `r <= 2` and parity. Combine states with `q = r` and equal masks, making total parity zero.
- **Complexity:** Sorting costs `O(N log N)`. The baseline DP is `O(M * 3 * 8 * 3)`, and the outside DP is constant size. Total memory is `O(1)` besides input storage.
