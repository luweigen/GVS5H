- **Prime decomposition:** Let \(e_i=v_p(S_i)\) and \(a_i=v_p(A_i)\). Since the exponent of \(p\) in \(f(S_i/S_{i+1})\) is \(|e_i-e_{i+1}|\), the constraints for each prime are independent: \(|e_i-e_{i+1}|=a_i\).
- **Gcd normalization:** The condition \(\gcd(S_1,\ldots,S_N)=1\) is equivalent to \(\min_i e_i=0\) for every prime \(p\). Primes not occurring in any \(A_i\) necessarily have all exponents zero, so contribute one.
- **Per-prime score:** For a fixed prime, a valid exponent sequence contributes \(p^{\sum_i e_i}\) to the score. The global answer is the product over occurring primes of the sum of these weights.
- **Height bound:** Put \(T=\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}a_i\). Any nonnegative walk whose minimum is zero has every height at most \(T\): from a zero-height position, reaching any position requires changes whose total absolute amount is at most \(T\).
- **DP states:** `dp0[h]` is the weighted sum of partial walks ending at height `h` that have not visited zero; `dp1[h]` is the corresponding sum for walks that have visited zero. Every time a vertex of height `h` is included, multiply by \(p^h\).
- **Initialization:** Choose \(e_1=h\) for every \(0\le h\le T\). Its weight is \(p^h\). Height zero starts in `dp1`, while positive heights start in `dp0`.
- **Transitions:** For edge difference `d>0`, transition from `h` to `h-d` and `h+d` when within `[0,T]`, multiplying by \(p^{\text{new height}}\). A transition reaching zero moves either flag state into `dp1`. For `d=0`, there is exactly one equality transition `h -> h`, preserving the flag and multiplying by \(p^h\).
- **Result and correctness:** After all edges, only `dp1` paths have minimum exactly zero, so the fixed-prime contribution is `sum(dp1)`. The DP enumerates every allowed exponent walk once, with exactly its required weight.
- **Complexity:** For prime \(p\), time is \(O(NT)\) and memory is \(O(T)\). Since \(\sum_p T_p=\sum_i\sum_p v_p(A_i)\) is small for \(A_i\le1000\), total work is practical.
