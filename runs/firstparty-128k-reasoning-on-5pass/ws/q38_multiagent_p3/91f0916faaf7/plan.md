Factor every A_i and process primes independently, because for each prime the condition f(S_i/S_{i+1})=A_i only fixes the absolute difference of p-adic valuations, and both gcd=1 and the score factorize over primes. For a fixed prime p with exponent sequence a_i=v_p(A_i), the problem becomes summing p^{sum x_i} over nonnegative integer walks x_1..x_N with |x_i-x_{i+1}|=a_i and min_i x_i=0. Such walks are finite once min=0, with all x_i bounded by B=sum a_i, so use a DP over current value and a flag for whether 0 has been visited, multiplying by p^{new value} on each transition. The total work over all primes is O(N * sum_p B_p) = O(N * total prime exponents in A), at most about 9N^2 for N=1000, easily feasible.
