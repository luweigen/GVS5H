View the configuration by its consecutive gaps \(d_k = p_{k+1}-p_k\). For four consecutive pieces with gaps \(a,b,c\), the operation leaves the outer pieces fixed and changes the three gaps to \(c,b,a\), i.e. it swaps \(d_i\) and \(d_{i+2}\). Hence the leftmost coordinate and the multiset of gaps at odd indices and at even indices are invariant, while any permutation within each parity class is reachable. The total coordinate sum is \(N p_1 + \sum_{k=1}^{N-1} (N-k)d_k\), so the problem reduces to assigning the invariant odd/even gap multisets to their fixed parity positions to minimize this weighted sum. By the rearrangement inequality, in each parity class the largest gaps should be placed at the largest indices, where the weights \(N-k\) are smallest. Compute this optimal assignment separately for odd and even gaps and add \(N X_1\).
