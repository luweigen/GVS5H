Each number `x` needs `d(x) = floor(log_4(x)) + 1` divisions by 4 to become zero. One operation can perform one such division on two elements, so the minimum operations for a query depend on the total required divisions and the largest individual requirement. For an interval `[l, r]`, if `S` is the sum of `d(x)` over all `x` in the interval and `M` is the maximum `d(x)` in the interval, the answer is `max(ceil(S / 2), M)`. Since `d(x)` is nondecreasing and piecewise constant on blocks `[4^(k-1), 4^k - 1]`, `M` is simply `d(r)`, and `S` can be computed with a prefix-sum over these base-4 blocks. Precompute powers of 4 up to `1e9` and use them to answer each query quickly, then sum the answers over all queries.
