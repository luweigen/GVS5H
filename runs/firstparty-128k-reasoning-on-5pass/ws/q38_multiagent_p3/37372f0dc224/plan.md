The shortest palindrome with S as a prefix is formed by appending the reverse of the shortest prefix that must be mirrored, which is the part before the longest palindromic suffix of S. If the longest palindromic suffix has length L, append reverse(S[:n-L]) to S. To find L in linear time, use the KMP prefix function on reversed(S) + '#' + S, because a palindromic suffix of S of length L equals the prefix of reversed(S) of length L. This runs in O(n) time and memory, suitable for n up to 500000. Finally, construct and print the resulting palindrome.
