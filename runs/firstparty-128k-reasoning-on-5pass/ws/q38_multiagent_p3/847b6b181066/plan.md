Model the smoke as particles born at the origin whenever the origin is empty after a wind step. A particle born at time b is at position P_t - P_b at time t, where P_t is the cumulative wind displacement after t steps. The origin is empty after step t exactly when P_t has not appeared as a previous prefix position, so existing particles correspond to first occurrences of distinct prefix positions. Therefore, target (R,C) is occupied at time t+0.5 iff P_t - (R,C) has already appeared among P_0,...,P_t. Maintain a hash set of seen prefix coordinates while scanning S once, test the shifted coordinate, and output the resulting bits.
