- **Core decomposition:** For a permutation B, f(B)=sum_i B_i 10^{digits after i}. For a fixed value x of digit length d, if T is the set of values after x and |T|=s, there are s!(N-s-1)! permutations with that T. The sum over T of 10^{digit_sum(T)} is the coefficient of z^s in P(z)/(1+10^d z), where P(z)=prod_d (1+10^d z)^{cnt_d}. Summing x in the same digit group gives val_d c_{s,d}. Hence answer = sum_{s=0}^{N-1} s!(N-s-1)! sum_d val_d c_{s,d}.
- **Digit groups:** d=1..len(str(N)), at most 6. cnt_d = count of numbers with d digits, val_d = sum of those numbers, a_d=10^d mod 998244353. Only groups with cnt>0 are kept.
- **Coefficients p_s:** p_s=[z^s]P(z). Let M(z)=prod_d(1+a_d z) and E(z)=sum_d cnt_d a_d M(z)/(1+a_d z). Since P'/P=sum_d cnt_d a_d/(1+a_d z), we have M P'=E P. If M=sum m_i z^i and E=sum e_j z^j, coefficient z^s gives (s+1)p_{s+1}=sum_{j=0}^{min(K-1,s)} e_j p_{s-j} - sum_{i=1}^{min(K,s+1)} m_i (s+1-i) p_{s+1-i}. p_0=1. This is O(KN).
- **Coefficients c_{s,d}:** c_{s,d}=[z^s]P/(1+a_d z). From (1+a_d z)C_d=P, c_{s,d}=p_s-a_d c_{s-1,d}, c_{-1,d}=0. Maintain one array c[d] while sweeping s.
- **Polynomial M and E:** K<=6, build M by multiplying linear factors. For each d, divide M by (1+a_d z) with synthetic division q_i=m_i-a_d q_{i-1}, q_0=1, and add cnt_d a_d q_i to e_i. All modulo 998244353.
- **Factorials and inverses:** Precompute fact[0..N] and modular inverses inv[1..N] linearly (mod is prime and N<mod). The recurrence divides by s+1.
- **Loop order:** For s=0..N-1, p_s is known. First update c[d] to c_{s,d} and form term=sum val_d c_{s,d}. Add fact[s]fact[N-s-1]term to ans. Then if s<N-1 compute p_{s+1} by the recurrence. This avoids a separate q_{s-1} and matches the direct fixed-x formula.
- **Edge cases:** N=1 gives one group, p_0=1, c_0=1, answer 1. K=1 recurrence reduces to p_{s+1}=a(cnt-s)/(s+1)p_s. The term for s=N-1 uses fact[0]. Groups with cnt=0 are skipped, so K is the number of digit lengths actually present.
- **Complexity:** O(NK) time with K<=6, O(N) memory for p, fact, inv. Python is safe for N=2e5.
- **Checks:** N=1 ->1, N=2 ->33, N=3 ->1332. The direct formula also equals sum_s s!(N-s-1)!(A p_s - q_{s-1}) with A=sum values and q_{s-1}=sum_d val_d a_d c_{s-1,d}, but the c_s form is simpler.
