Fix each possible middle index i and count ways to choose two earlier and two later indices. For x=nums[i], valid subsequences are those where x appears at least twice among the five; split into (a) at least two x's among the four side positions, and (b) exactly one x among the side positions with the three non-x side values pairwise distinct. Maintain prefix and suffix frequency maps while scanning i, and use the counts of x plus non-x frequencies to compute both cases with O(number of distinct values) arithmetic per i. In case (b), count distinct-value pairs on the opposite side and subtract pairs that collide with the single non-x value from the side that contains one x. Sum over all i modulo 1e9+7, yielding O(n^2) for n<=1000.
