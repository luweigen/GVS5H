Represent the set as a binary array f over the value range, with f[x]=1 iff x∈S. A fine triplet (A,B,C) is determined by its center B and satisfies A+C=2B, so the number of valid triplets centered at B is ((f*f)[2B]-1)/2, where the -1 removes the self-pair (B,B) and division by 2 accounts for the two ordered pairs (A,C) and (C,A). Compute the convolution f*f in O(M log M) using FFT or NTT, padding to a power of two at least 2·max(S)+1. Sum this centered count over all B∈S and output the result.
