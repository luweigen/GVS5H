Treat the pieces as ordered labeled points; operations only shrink gaps between consecutive pieces and may shift the whole configuration. For a fixed assignment of the initial pieces to the required B positions, the minimum number of operations can be computed in O(1) from the total span reduction, the first/last gap reductions, and a parity choice. The assignment is a partition of A's 1s into blocks matching B's 1s, and each B gap must be matched to an A gap of at least that length, with an extra parity restriction when the lengths are equal. For each of the two possible parities, greedily match B gaps to A gaps to determine which first/last boundary cases are achievable, then take the minimum O(1) cost. Handle the degenerate cases M=1 and M=2 separately, and output -1 when no valid matching exists.
