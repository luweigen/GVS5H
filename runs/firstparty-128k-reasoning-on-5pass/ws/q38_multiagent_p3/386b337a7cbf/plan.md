Process nums in order and build subsequences by either skipping or appending the current value. A state needs length parity (which determines the next sign), alternating sum, and product; because the sum range is at most 1800, store reachable sums as bitsets. For each product 0..limit keep two sum-bitsets (even/odd length), and also keep two product-ignoring sum-bitsets for all non-empty subsequences so that a later zero can make the product 0 even if the prefix product exceeded limit. Positive products are pruned once they exceed limit, while product 0 is tracked explicitly and stays 0; zeros are handled by moving all old product-ignoring states into product 0. At the end, return the largest product whose bitset contains k in either parity, or -1.
