- **Gap intervals:** For each value, consecutive occurrences at positions x and y create a gap of cut positions [x, y-1]. A cut inside that gap separates the value into two sides. For two cuts i < j, the value contributes 1 plus the number of its gaps containing i or j. Summing over values gives total_distinct plus the number of gap intervals hit by at least one of the two cuts.
- **Two-cut coverage:** Let cover(c) be the number of gap intervals containing cut c. For cuts i < j, the number of intervals hit by at least one cut is cover(i) + cover(j) - common(i, j), where common(i, j) is the number of intervals with left endpoint <= i and right endpoint >= j.
- **Sweep over first cut:** Maintain V[j] = cover(j) - common(i, j) for the current first cut i. Initially V = cover. When the sweep reaches an interval start l, that interval starts contributing to common(i, j) for all j in [l+1, r], so apply range add -1 to V on half-open [l+1, next_pos[l]). Then the best second cut is the suffix maximum of V over j >= i+1. Candidate answer is total_distinct + cover(i) + suffix_max(i+1).
- **Computing cover and next_pos:** One backward pass computes next_pos, total_distinct, and a difference array for cover. Each gap [l, next_pos[l]) adds +1 at l and -1 at next_pos[l]. Prefixing diff gives cover(c). cover(i) is maintained on the fly during the sweep.
- **Segment tree:** Iterative lazy segment tree over cut positions 0..N-2, padded to a power of two with a large negative sentinel. It supports range add -1 and suffix maximum. Invariant: d[p] = lazy[p] + max(d[2p], d[2p+1]). Range add applies directly to O(log N) canonical nodes and then pulls boundary paths; no push is needed. Suffix max climbs from root to leaf l while carrying the sum of ancestor lazy values; whenever the path goes left, the right sibling is fully included and is evaluated with that carry.
- **Complexity:** O(N log N) time and O(N) memory, suitable for N up to 3e5.
- **Edge cases:** N=3, all equal, all distinct, consecutive duplicates producing empty update ranges, and padded leaves are handled correctly. Samples and small brute-force cases are consistent.
