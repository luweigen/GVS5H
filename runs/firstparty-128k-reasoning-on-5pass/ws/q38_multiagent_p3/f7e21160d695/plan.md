Use the threshold characterization: f(x, y) is the smallest T such that x and y are connected in the subgraph of edges with weight at most T. For any threshold T, pairs with f < T must be matched inside the same connected component, so the unavoidable number of pairs with f ≥ T is half the sum of absolute (#A−#B) imbalances over the current components. Process edges in increasing weight with a DSU, maintaining the sum of absolute imbalances of components. For each weight w, the decrease in this unavoidable count when adding all edges of weight w is exactly the number of pairs whose optimal cost is w, so add w times that decrease. Summing over all weights gives the minimum permuted sum.
