Process the insertions in reverse order, from i = N down to 1. In the final array, after removing all elements greater than i, the remaining empty positions are exactly the final positions of elements 1 through i, and element i is the P_i-th among them. Maintain a Fenwick tree over the N final positions, initially marking every position as empty. For each i, find the P_i-th empty position, assign i to that position, and mark it occupied. This gives the final array in O(N log N) time and O(N) memory, which is efficient for N up to 5×10^5.
