Characterize strong connectivity by prefix cuts: every prefix must have a white-to-black edge from the suffix, equivalently the number of prefix blacks matched to suffix whites is positive; zeros can only occur after white vertices. Index whites by i=1..N, let q_i be #blacks before the i-th white; inclusion-exclusion over zero cuts after white vertices gives F_0=1, F_i=0 if i<q_i, else F_i=-(∑_{t<i} F_t fact[i-q_t])/fact[i-q_i], and the answer is -F_N (0 if S_1!=B or S_N!=W). Group white indices by q_i; for a fixed q-block, the same-q contributions are handled in O(length) by maintaining P with P_{i+1}=-(i-q_i+1)E_i, where E_i is the cross-q sum. Compute all cross-q sums E_i=∑_{b<q_i} T_b fact[i-b] (T_b=∑_{q_i=b}F_i) by CDQ divide-and-conquer over q-values, using NTT convolutions with local index shifts to add left-half T_b to right-half white indices. Precompute factorials/inv factorials modulo 998244353; total complexity O(N log^2 N) and O(N) memory.
