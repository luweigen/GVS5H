Model the removal process as a noncrossing matching of original positions, with at most one unmatched element; the final survivor must have odd 1-indexed position. For any even segment, a total score can be written as sum c_i A_i where each pair contributes one + and one -, and every balanced sign sequence (equal + and -) is realizable by a noncrossing matching. Therefore the best score for an even segment is obtained by assigning + to its largest half and - to its smallest half, i.e. f(segment)=sum(largest half)-sum(smallest half). For even N the answer is f(whole array); for odd N the answer is max over odd positions m of f(left of m)+f(right of m). Compute these prefix and suffix f values in O(N log N) using two heaps (or an order-statistic structure) and take the maximum.
