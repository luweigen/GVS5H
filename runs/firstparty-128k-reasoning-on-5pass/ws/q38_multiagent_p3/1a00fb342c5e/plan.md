Treat the constraints as XOR equations on an undirected graph with vertices 1..N. Assign each vertex a potential relative to its connected component root by DFS/BFS, setting the root to 0 and propagating `potential[v] = potential[u] XOR Z`; if any edge violates this, no good sequence exists. In a consistent component, every valid assignment is the computed potential XOR a single component-wide mask `C`. The total sum is additive over bits, so for each bit independently choose `C`'s bit to make the majority of vertices have 0 in that bit. Apply the chosen mask to all vertices in the component; isolated vertices remain 0. This yields a minimum-sum good sequence.
