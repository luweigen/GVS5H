Expand each entry of B^p as a sum over length-p walks and sum the resulting monomials over all replacements of zeros. Over F_p, a zero entry contributes -1 when its walk count is 0 or a multiple of p-1 and 0 otherwise, so every valid walk gets a common factor (-1)^K. For odd p, a zero edge can therefore appear only 0 or p-1 times; this leaves the all-fixed walks F^p plus O(N^2) corrections from zero self-loops, and for p=3 also zero directed 2-cycles. The p=2 case collapses to the all-ones matrix, giving N mod 2 in every entry. Compute F^p by fast matrix exponentiation modulo p, add the corrections, apply the sign, and output.
