Compute BFS distances from S and T, let d = dist(S,T), and fix one shortest S-T path P. Any swap needs 2d moves plus the cost of a detour that lets the pieces pass; model a detour as a walk from a vertex of P to another vertex of P whose internal vertices are outside P, with loops allowed only at internal P vertices. Give each directed edge u->v weight 1-(distS[v]-distS[u]) (0,1,2), so the weight of a detour equals the extra moves over 2d. Run two multi-source Dijkstra passes on the subgraph of vertices not on P: one allowing any P source and internal P targets, and one allowing all P sources except T with target T. The minimum found weight is the required extra; if none exists, output -1, else 2d+extra.
