Simulate the M axis-aligned moves using 64-bit integers to obtain the final point. While simulating, record each traversed segment as a closed interval on its fixed coordinate: horizontal segments grouped by y with x-interval, vertical segments grouped by x with y-interval. For each coordinate, sort and merge its intervals into a disjoint union of covered ranges. Then for every house, test whether its x lies in the merged horizontal coverage for its y or its y lies in the merged vertical coverage for its x, using binary search. Count houses satisfying at least one condition, which automatically avoids double counting houses hit by both orientations or multiple overlapping segments. This yields O((N+M) log M) time and O(N+M) memory.
