Let the positions of all `1`s be \(p_0,p_1,\dots,p_{k-1}\). If the final contiguous block starts at \(x\), the minimum swaps equals \(\sum_i |p_i-(x+i)|\), which is minimized by choosing \(x\) based on the median of \(p_i-i\). Compute these adjusted positions and evaluate the cost around their median in \(O(k)\) time. Use 64-bit integer arithmetic because the answer can exceed 32-bit range.
