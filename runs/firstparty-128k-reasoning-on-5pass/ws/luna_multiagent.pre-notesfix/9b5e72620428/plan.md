Model the final common sum as an integer \(S\). For every index with known \(B_i\), the assigned \(A\)-value must be \(S-B_i\), while known \(A\)-values can be matched only to compatible \(B_i\)-values; unknown entries can be chosen freely but must remain non-negative. Since \(A\) may be arbitrarily rearranged, determine whether the multiset of fixed \(A\)-values can be injected into the available positions for a candidate \(S\), then verify that all remaining requirements can be filled. Develop an efficient characterization or monotonic search over feasible \(S\), targeting \(O(N\log N)\) or \(O(N\log^2 N)\).
