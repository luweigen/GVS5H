Use the identity \(f(s)=s-\sum_{k\ge1,\;2^k\mid s}s/2^k\), so the answer becomes the total sum of all \(A_i+A_j\) minus a contribution \(T_k/2^k\) for every power of two \(2^k\), where \(T_k\) is the sum of \(A_i+A_j\) over pairs satisfying \(A_i+A_j\equiv0\pmod{2^k}\). For each \(k\), group values by their residue modulo \(2^k\); residue \(r\) pairs only with \((-r)\bmod 2^k\). Maintain each group’s count and sum, carefully handling self-complementary residue classes and the \(i\le j\) restriction. Iterate only through powers of two up to the maximum possible pair sum, yielding \(O(N\log A_{\max})\) time and \(O(N)\) auxiliary memory.
