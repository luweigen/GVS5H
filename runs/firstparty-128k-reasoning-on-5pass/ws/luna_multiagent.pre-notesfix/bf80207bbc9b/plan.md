Choose a subset of columns to flip; for any fixed column-flip mask, each row can then be independently flipped to minimize its number of ones. The row’s contribution is therefore the minimum Hamming weight between its bitmask XOR the column mask and its complement. Sum these contributions over all rows and minimize over all \(2^W\) column masks. Since \(W\le 18\), enumerate all masks and compute contributions efficiently using a frequency array of row masks and subset/complement relations.
