Use a best-first search over the grid, starting from Takahashi’s cell and maintaining the current strength. At each step, among all adjacent unabsorbed slimes, absorb any slime whose strength satisfies \(S < \text{strength}/X\); a min-heap allows the smallest candidate to be processed first. Whenever the strength increases, newly reachable neighboring cells are added to the heap, and stale or no-longer-eligible heap entries are skipped. Since all strengths are positive, the process is maximized by repeatedly absorbing every currently eligible reachable slime, and the answer fits in 64-bit integer arithmetic.
