For a candidate divisor \(d\), an element \(A_i\) can belong to a chosen \(K\)-element subset whose GCD is divisible by \(d\) exactly when \(A_i\) is divisible by \(d\) and at least \(K\) array elements are divisible by \(d\). Compute, using a sieve over values up to \(\max A_i\), how many input elements are multiples of every \(d\). Then iterate through all valid divisors \(d\), updating the answer for every present value that is a multiple of \(d\); the largest such divisor is the required maximum GCD. The total harmonic-series complexity is \(O(M\log M)\), where \(M=\max A_i\), with \(O(M)\) memory.
