Use monotonic stacks to determine, for every index, the maximal left and right ranges in which `nums[i]` is the unique representative maximum or minimum, applying consistent tie-breaking for equal values. For each index, count the pairs of left/right endpoints whose subarray length is at most `k`; this can be computed in O(1) from the two range sizes using a helper for bounded pairs. Sum each element’s maximum contribution and minimum contribution separately, then return their total. The overall complexity is O(n) time and O(n) space.
