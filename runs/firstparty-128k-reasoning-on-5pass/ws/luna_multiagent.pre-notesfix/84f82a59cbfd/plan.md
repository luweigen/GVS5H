Observe that every 400 number has the form \(N=(p^a q^b)^2\), where \(p\) and \(q\) are distinct primes and \(a,b\ge1\); conversely, every such square is valid. Precompute all integers \(x\le10^6\) having exactly two distinct prime factors, store \(x^2\), and sort the resulting list. For each query \(A\), binary-search the largest precomputed square not exceeding \(A\). Use a smallest-prime-factor sieve to classify every \(x\) efficiently.
