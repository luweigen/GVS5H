Track the cumulative displacement of the wind after each time step, including the initial position `(0,0)`. A smoke particle generated when the cumulative position first reaches a particular coordinate remains represented by that coordinate, and at time `t` it is located at `prefix[t] - prefix[k]` for some previously visited prefix `prefix[k]`. Therefore, smoke exists at `(R,C)` exactly when `prefix[t] - (R,C)` has appeared among the visited prefix coordinates. Maintain these coordinates in a set while scanning the string once, yielding an O(N) solution.
