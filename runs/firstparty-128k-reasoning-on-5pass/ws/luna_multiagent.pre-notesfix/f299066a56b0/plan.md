Use the sorted order of mochi sizes and observe that an optimal solution pairs smaller mochi from the first part with larger mochi from the second part. Binary search the maximum number \(K\) of pairs, checking feasibility by matching the \(K\) smallest mochi as tops with \(K\) mochi beginning at index \(N-K\) as bottoms. Since both groups are sorted, feasibility is verified with a linear two-pointer scan, or equivalently by comparing corresponding elements. The overall complexity is \(O(N \log N)\), with \(O(1)\) extra space beyond the input.
