Group foods by vitamin type and independently compute a 0/1 knapsack DP for each group, where `dp[c]` is the maximum vitamin amount obtainable with at most `c` calories. Since the three vitamin groups are disjoint, a target value `T` is feasible exactly when the minimum calories needed to obtain at least `T` units of each vitamin sum to at most `X`. Precompute these DPs in `O(NX)` time, then binary-search the largest feasible `T`. The final algorithm uses `O(X)` additional memory per vitamin group.
