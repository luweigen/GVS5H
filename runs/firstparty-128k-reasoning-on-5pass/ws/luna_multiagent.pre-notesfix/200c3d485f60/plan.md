Count arrays by choosing the \(k\) adjacent positions that remain equal; the other \(n-1-k\) positions must change. For any selected pattern, choose the first value in \(m\) ways, and each change has \(m-1\) choices while equal positions are forced. Thus the answer is \(\binom{n-1}{k} \cdot m \cdot (m-1)^{n-1-k}\) modulo \(10^9+7\). Precompute factorials and inverse factorials up to \(n\) to evaluate the binomial coefficient efficiently in \(O(n)\) time and \(O(n)\) space.
