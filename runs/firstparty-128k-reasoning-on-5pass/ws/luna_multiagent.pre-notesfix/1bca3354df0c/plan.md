Model the graph as a bipartite graph, since the initial graph has no odd cycle and every legal added edge must preserve a valid two-coloring. Characterize legal moves both within connected components and between distinct components, then determine whether the total number of possible moves—and therefore its parity—is invariant under the order of play. Prove the resulting winner criterion, taking special care with isolated vertices and with how component color orientations may be chosen when components are merged. Implement the characterization using linear-time bipartite traversal and 64-bit arithmetic.
