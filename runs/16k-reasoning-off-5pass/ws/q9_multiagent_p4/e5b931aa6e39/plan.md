The problem asks us to find two positive integers $x$ and $y$ such that $x^3 - y^3 = N$, where $N$ can be as large as $10^{18}$. Since $x^3 - y^3 = (x-y)(x^2 + xy + y^2)$, and $x$ must be slightly larger than $y$, we can estimate that $x \approx y + \delta$ where $\delta$ is small. Specifically, for large $N$, $x$ will be approximately $(N/3)^{1/3}$. We can iterate through possible values of $y$ starting from 1 up to an estimated upper bound (roughly $N^{1/3}$) and check if $x^3 - y^3 = N$ for the corresponding $x$. Given the constraint $N \le 10^{18}$, $x$ will be at most around $10^6$, making a linear scan feasible within typical time limits.
