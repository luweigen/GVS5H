The problem requires finding the maximum subarray sum after removing all occurrences of at most one integer $x$. Since the array length is up to $10^5$, an $O(N^2)$ solution checking every possible $x$ is too slow. Instead, we can precompute prefix and suffix maximum subarray sums for the original array. For each unique number $x$ in the array, the optimal result after removing $x$ will be the maximum of: (1) the best subarray entirely in the prefix before the first occurrence of $x$, (2) the best subarray entirely in the suffix after the last occurrence of $x$, or (3) a subarray crossing the gap formed by removing $x$ (which is simply the sum of the suffix of the prefix part ending at the removal point plus the prefix of the suffix part starting after the removal point). We can efficiently calculate these values by iterating through the array once to build prefix/suffix arrays and then iterating through unique elements to compute the answer.
