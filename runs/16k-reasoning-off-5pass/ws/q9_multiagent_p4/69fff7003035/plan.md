The problem asks for the sum of numbers formed by concatenating permutations of $1$ to $N$. We can calculate the contribution of each digit $d$ (from $1$ to $N$) to the total sum by determining its frequency at each decimal position (units, tens, hundreds, etc.). For a specific number $d$, let $L(d)$ be the number of digits in its decimal representation. In any permutation, $d$ appears in the $k$-th position of the concatenated string (where $k$ ranges from $1$ to $N \times \max(L)$) with a specific probability. We will iterate through each number $i$ from $1$ to $N$, calculate its digit length, and then sum its contributions across all possible positions it can occupy in the final string, multiplying by the number of permutations where it lands there.
