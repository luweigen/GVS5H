The strategy involves iterating through every vertex in the tree to assume it is the central root of the potential Snowflake Tree. For each candidate root, we calculate the cost to form a valid structure by checking if its neighbors can serve as the "x" intermediate nodes (each having exactly `y` leaves attached). We determine the optimal `y` for each root by analyzing the degree distribution of its neighbors, specifically looking for a `y` that maximizes the number of valid intermediate nodes. The global minimum deletion count is the answer.
