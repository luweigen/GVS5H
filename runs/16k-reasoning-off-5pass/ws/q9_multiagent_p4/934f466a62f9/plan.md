The problem asks us to maximize the sum of $\max(X_{a_i}+X_{b_i}, Y_{a_i}+Y_{b_i}, Z_{a_i}+Z_{b_i})$ over $K$ disjoint pairs. Since the value of a pair is determined by the maximum of three sums, we can iterate over which attribute (beauty, tastiness, or popularity) determines the maximum for each pair. For a fixed attribute (say beauty), the contribution of a pair is simply the sum of the beauty values of the two cakes. To maximize the total sum when beauty is the deciding factor, we should greedily pair the cakes with the largest beauty values together. We calculate the maximum possible total score for each of the three attributes independently by sorting the cakes based on that attribute and pairing adjacent elements in the sorted list (taking the top $2K$ elements). The final answer is the maximum of these three scenarios.
