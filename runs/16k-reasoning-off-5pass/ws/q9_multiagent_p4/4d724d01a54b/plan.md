The minimum cost to sort the permutation is given by the sum over all $i$ from 1 to $N-1$ of $i \times c_i$, where $c_i$ is the number of elements in the prefix $P[1 \dots i]$ that are greater than $i$. This counts the number of inversions that must cross the boundary between $i$ and $i+1$. We will iterate through the array, maintaining a count of elements greater than the current index.
