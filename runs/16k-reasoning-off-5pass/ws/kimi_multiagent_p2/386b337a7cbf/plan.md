This is a DP problem over three dimensions: position in the array, current alternating sum, and current product. Since nums[i] ≤ 12 and limit ≤ 5000, we cap products at limit+1 (treating "exceeds limit" as a single absorbing state) to keep the product dimension bounded at ~5001. The alternating sum range is bounded by sum of all elements (≤ 1800), so we offset it for indexing. We track, for each (alternating sum, product) pair, whether it's achievable, processing elements one at a time with parity-aware transitions (add at even positions, subtract at odd). Finally, scan all achievable states with alternating sum == k and product ≤ limit, and return the maximum product, or -1 if none exist.
