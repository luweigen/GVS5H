Takahashi occupies a growing connected region; he can absorb any boundary slime whose strength is strictly less than his current strength divided by X. Since absorbing only increases his strength, absorbing any currently-absorbable slime never hurts — the greedy choice is to always absorb the cheapest available boundary slime. This maps to a Dijkstra/Prim-style expansion: maintain a min-heap of boundary slimes keyed by strength, pop the minimum, and if it satisfies the threshold absorb it (adding its neighbors); otherwise stop, since the smallest boundary slime failing means all others fail too. The threshold check must use exact integer arithmetic (s * X < current_strength) to avoid floating-point issues with values up to ~10^17. The algorithm is O(HW log(HW)), fine for 500×500.
