Model the smoke as a set of cells, but track it implicitly: each smoke particle is born at (0,0) at some integer time (whenever (0,0) is empty after the wind step), and thereafter moves deterministically with the wind. A particle born at time b is at position equal to the sum of wind vectors from step b+1 through t at time t+0.5. So smoke exists at (R,C) at time t+0.5 iff there is a birth time b (0 ≤ b ≤ t, where b=0 is the initial smoke) such that the prefix displacement from b+1 to t equals (R,C), and (0,0) was empty at time b+0.5 (which determines births). Simulate forward, maintaining the set of "active birth times" whose current position is (0,0) to decide generation, and answer queries via prefix-sum lookups.
