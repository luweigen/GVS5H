Split p at the two '*' into three literal parts: A (prefix), B (middle), C (suffix). A substring s[i..j] matches iff it starts with A, ends with C, and contains B somewhere between the end of A and the start of C (parts may overlap only within the same literal, not across the star boundaries). For each occurrence of A starting at index i, the optimal right endpoint is determined by the first occurrence of B starting at or after i+len(A), followed by an occurrence of C starting at or after the end of that B; the candidate length is (start of C + len(C)) − i, and we take the minimum over all A-occurrences. To find the "next occurrence at or after position x" for B and C in O(1) per query, precompute next-occurrence arrays by scanning right to left. All literal occurrences are found with KMP (or Z-algorithm) for O(n+m) total time. Edge cases: empty A, B, or C are handled naturally (empty literal "occurs" at every position); answer 0 arises when A=B=C="" (p == "**").
