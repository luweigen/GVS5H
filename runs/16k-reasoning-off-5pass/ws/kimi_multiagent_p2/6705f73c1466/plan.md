Adjacent swaps only reorder by moving characters past each other; since all 1s are identical, the minimum swaps to gather them equals the minimum total distance to move each 1 onto a contiguous target block of length K (K = number of 1s). If the 1-positions are p[0..K-1] (0-indexed), the optimal target block placement is anchored at the median: define q[i] = p[i] - i, then the answer is sum |q[i] - median(q)|. This is the classic "minimum adjacent swaps to group ones" reduction. Compute positions in one pass, take the median of q, and sum absolute deviations — O(N) time, O(K) space.
