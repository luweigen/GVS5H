We need max over i<j of L[i] + M(i,j) + R[j+1], where L[i] is distinct count of prefix ending at i and R is distinct count of suffix. Precompute prefix distinct counts L[i] and suffix distinct counts R[j+1] in O(N). The middle distinct count for subarray (i+1..j) is the hard part: for each right endpoint j, we want max over i<j of (distinct count of A[i+1..j] + L[i]). Maintain this with a segment tree / lazy propagation: as we extend j, the distinct count of every suffix-start i increases by 1 only for starts i+1 greater than the previous occurrence of A[j]. So we add 1 to a range of candidate i values, and query max of (middle distinct + L[i]) over i in [1, j-1], then combine with R[j+1]. Overall O(N log N).
