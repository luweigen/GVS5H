Adjacent swaps only reorder by moving characters past each other, and since all 1s are identical, the problem reduces to choosing which final contiguous block positions the 1s occupy. If the 1s are at positions p[0..k-1] (1-indexed) and we gather them into a contiguous block, the minimum adjacent swaps equals the number of inversions between 1s and 0s crossed, which for a chosen block equals sum |p[j] - (block_start + j)|. The optimal block is centered at the median: transform q[j] = p[j] - j, then the answer is the sum of absolute deviations of q[j] from its median. This is computable in O(k) after reading positions.
