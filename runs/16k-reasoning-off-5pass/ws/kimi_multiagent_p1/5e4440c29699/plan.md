The grid is a rectangle [0,W]×[0,H] with a rectangular hole [L,R]×[D,U] removed; moves only go right/up. A path is any monotone chain of blocks, so the answer equals the total number of monotone paths summed over all ordered start/end pairs of blocks (each chain counted by its endpoints), i.e. sum over blocks s,t with s≤t coordinatewise of (number of monotone paths from s to t). Equivalently, for each block p, let f(p)=number of monotone paths from any block to p (DP over increasing x+y), and the answer is the sum of f over all blocks. Compute f via DP with combinatorial closed forms: f(x,y) = C(x+y,x) minus contributions from the hole, using inclusion-exclusion / subtraction of paths passing through the forbidden rectangle. Since the hole is a single rectangle, f on the region above/right of the hole can be computed by subtracting paths that enter the hole region, using boundary-strip DP with binomial coefficients and prefix sums in O(W+H) after O((W+H)) factorial precomputation.
