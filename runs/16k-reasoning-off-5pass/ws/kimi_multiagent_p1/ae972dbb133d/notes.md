The solution is the GF(2) corner-coloring reduction developed across the team notes:

1. **Model.** A placement chooses 2 of 4 edge-midpoints per cell; the no-dead-end condition ⇔ neighboring cells agree on shared edges ⇔ the chosen edges form a spanning 2-regular subgraph (2-factor) of the H×W toroidal grid graph (vertices = cell centers), with a "turn" at A-cells (adjacent edges) and "straight" at B-cells (opposite edges). Each valid edge set = exactly one placement.

2. **Boundary / corner coloring.** On the torus, an even-degree edge set is the boundary of a 2-coloring of the corner lattice iff its winding parities are even; all 4 winding sectors are covered by 4 twist boundary conditions (p, q) ∈ {0,1}² (anti/periodic). Each sector's colorings map 2-to-1 (global complement, which acts freely) onto edge sets, so answer = (Σ_{p,q} N_{p,q})/2, the division being exact.

3. **Local linear conditions** on the 4 corners a=x(i,j), b=x(i,j+1), c=x(i+1,j), d=x(i+1,j+1):
   - A-cell ⇔ 3-1 split ⇔ odd parity: a⊕b⊕c⊕d = 1.
   - B-cell ⇔ adjacent-block 2-2 split ⇔ a⊕d = 1 AND b⊕c = 1 (this automatically excludes 4-0 and checkerboard patterns).

4. **Propagation.** Take corner row 0 and column 0 as free initials (n0 = H+W−1). Sweeping rows, each cell determines the opposite corner d (A: a⊕b⊕c⊕1; B: a⊕1); B-cells add the check b⊕c=1. Horizontal wraps (x(i+1,W)=x(i+1,0)⊕q) and vertical wraps (x(H,j)=x(0,j)⊕p) close the torus. The map initials → full coloring is a bijection between constraint solutions and valid colorings, so N_{p,q} = 2^{n0−rank} or 0.

5. **Implementation.** Every corner value is an affine GF(2) form in the initials, stored as a big-int bitset mask plus a constant bit. Masks are sector-independent, so they are propagated once and cached; each of the 4 sectors only recomputes constants (plain ints) and runs Gaussian elimination with a pivot dictionary on the constraints (B-checks + wraps, ≤ b+H+W+1 of them), with early exit on contradiction.
