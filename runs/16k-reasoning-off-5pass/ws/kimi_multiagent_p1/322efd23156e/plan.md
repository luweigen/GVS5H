Binary search the answer T (max feasible minimum vitamin intake). For a candidate T, each vitamin group becomes a 0/1 knapsack: compute the minimum calories needed to reach at least T units of that vitamin, capping values at T (since exceeding T is useless). Feasibility is simply whether the sum of the three minimum costs is ≤ X.
