For each right endpoint r (1..n), a subarray ending at r is valid iff its left endpoint l is greater than every "left partner" of a conflicting pair whose other endpoint lies in [l..r]. So for each value x, maintain the largest and second-largest partners y<x that conflict with x; the running maximum of largest partners gives the tightest left bound, and the count of valid subarrays equals sum over r of (r - maxLeft). Removing one pair (a,b) only affects the constraint at max(a,b) with partner min(a,b): if that partner is the unique largest, the bound relaxes to the second-largest, gaining (largest - second) subarrays for every r >= max(a,b). Compute the baseline total and per-pair gains in one pass, then answer = baseline + max gain.
