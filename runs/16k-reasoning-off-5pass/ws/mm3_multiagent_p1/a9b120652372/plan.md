Model the operation as all pieces moving one step toward a chosen index i. This is equivalent to taking the multiset of piece positions and, after each operation, shifting each position one step toward the target index. The key observation is that the order of pieces from left to right is preserved, and any piece can only travel left or right depending on the chosen i. The final positions must match the set of indices where B_i = 1. The problem reduces to checking if there is a monotone matching between initial piece positions and final required positions, and if so, minimizing the number of operations (each operation reduces the total Manhattan distance by exactly the number of pieces moving). Compute the sum of absolute differences between matched positions, divide by number of pieces (since each step moves every piece by 1), giving the answer.
