The operation on four consecutive pieces swaps the gaps (differences) between the first and second pieces and between the third and fourth pieces, leaving the middle gap unchanged. This means we can independently permute the gaps at odd indices and even indices. The total sum of coordinates is a weighted sum of gaps with decreasing weights. To minimize it, we sort the odd-indexed gaps in non-decreasing order and assign them to odd positions in increasing index order, and similarly for even-indexed gaps. Then reconstruct the final positions and compute the sum.
