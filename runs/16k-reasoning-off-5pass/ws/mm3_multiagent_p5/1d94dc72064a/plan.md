The game ends when every index 1..N has been chosen at least once, and the last move to do so wins. Let `U = sum(A_i)` be the total number of decrements performed overall. The game lasts exactly U moves; the player who makes move U (0-indexed) wins. Since Fennec moves on turns 0,2,4,... and Snuke on 1,3,5,..., the winner is determined by the parity of U: Fennec wins if U is odd, Snuke wins if U is even. The moves themselves don't matter because the game is guaranteed to last U turns and the termination condition is only checked after the last move (move U triggers it). So just compute the total sum of A_i modulo 2.
