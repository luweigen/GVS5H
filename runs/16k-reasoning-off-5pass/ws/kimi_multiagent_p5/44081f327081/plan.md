The answer for index i is the maximum d dividing A_i such that at least K elements of A are divisible by d (we can then pick A_i plus K−1 others, or K others if A_i is among them—counting handles this since A_i itself is divisible by d). So we need, for every value d, the count cnt[d] of array elements divisible by d; then for each element A_i, the answer is the largest divisor d of A_i with cnt[d] ≥ K. Compute cnt via a divisor-count sieve: for each d from 1 to max(A), add freq[d] to all multiples of d, which is O(M log M) with M = 10^6. Then for each A_i, enumerate its divisors (or precompute best[d] = largest multiple-based answer) and take the max divisor with sufficient count. A faster alternative: precompute ans_for_value[v] for each distinct value v by scanning divisors, or compute best[d] = largest multiple m of d with cnt[m] ≥ K via a reverse sieve, making each query O(number of divisors) or even O(1) after a best-divisor precomputation.
