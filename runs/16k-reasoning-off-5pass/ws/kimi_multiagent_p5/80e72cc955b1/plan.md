The key insight is to analyze how many times each number must be divided by 4 (with floor) to reach zero, which is essentially its base-4 digit length. Since each operation can reduce two numbers simultaneously, the total operations needed for a range [l, r] is the ceiling of half the sum of "division steps" for all numbers in the range. We can precompute the sum of division steps for ranges using prefix sums over base-4 intervals, then process each query in O(log r) time.
