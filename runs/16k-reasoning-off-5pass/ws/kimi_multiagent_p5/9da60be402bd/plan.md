We need shortest palindrome-labeled walk between every ordered pair. Model states as unordered endpoint pairs (u,v) meaning "we have matched a prefix from u forward and a suffix ending at v backward"; the goal is to reach a state where the remaining middle is a palindrome of length 0 (u==v) or 1 (edge u->v). BFS from all "center" states (u,u) with distance 0 and (u,v) with an edge u->v with distance 1 over the multigraph of unordered pairs: from state (u,v), for any edge x->u labeled c and edge v->y labeled c, transition to (x,y) with +2. The first time BFS reaches (i,j) gives the shortest palindrome length for pair (i,j). N≤100 gives at most ~5050 states and transitions bounded by incoming/outgoing edges per letter, easily fast enough.
