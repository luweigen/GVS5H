For each permutation, f(P) is the concatenation of the decimal strings of the numbers in order. The contribution of a value x at position i equals x times 10^{total digits of all elements after position i}. By symmetry, each value x appears in each position exactly (N-1)! times, and for a fixed position, the multiset of "remaining elements" is uniform over all (N-1)-subsets. So the total answer is (N-1)! * sum over all x of x * (sum over all (N-1)-subsets R of 10^{digitsum(R)}), where the inner sum is the same for every position. The subset sum of 10^{digits} factors as a product: sum over subsets = (1/(1+10^{d_x})) * prod_{v=1}^{N} (1 + 10^{d_v}), computable with a modular inverse per distinct digit-length group. Group numbers by digit count (at most ~6 groups for N ≤ 2e5), compute the product via prefix/suffix products over groups, then combine.
