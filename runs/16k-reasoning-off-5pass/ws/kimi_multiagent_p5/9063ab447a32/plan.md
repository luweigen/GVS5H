The cost of buying k units of product i is k²·P_i, which is convex in k, so the marginal cost of the j-th unit of product i is (2j−1)·P_i and increases with j. The optimal strategy is therefore to buy units in increasing order of marginal cost across all products. We binary search on a threshold T: buy every unit whose marginal cost is ≤ T, i.e. for product i take k_i = floor((T/P_i + 1)/2) units. The count function is monotone in T, so we find the largest T with total count ≤ answer candidate, then compute the exact answer by counting units with marginal cost below the final threshold and filling the remainder with units at exactly the threshold cost, checking affordability with 128-bit-safe arithmetic (Python ints are unbounded).
