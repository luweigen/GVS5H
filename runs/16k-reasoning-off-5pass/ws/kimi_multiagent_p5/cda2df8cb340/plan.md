f(x) is x with all factors of 2 removed, i.e., the odd part of x. For a sum s = A_i + A_j, write s = 2^k * m with m odd; then f(s) = m = s / 2^{v2(s)}. Key observation: v2(a+b) depends on the 2-adic valuations of a and b — if v2(a) ≠ v2(b), then v2(a+b) = min(v2(a), v2(b)); if equal, v2(a+b) > that common value. This suggests grouping numbers by their v2 value and processing parity classes recursively: split numbers by parity, handle odd+odd (sum even, recurse on halves) and even+even similarly, while odd+even sums are odd so f = the sum itself. We compute the total sum of (A_i+A_j) over i≤j per parity class, then divide by appropriate powers of 2 via recursion on halved values.
