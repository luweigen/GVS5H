Adjacent swaps mean the minimum cost to gather all 1s into a contiguous block equals the minimum total distance moved by the 1s. If the positions of the 1s are p[0..k-1], moving them to consecutive positions starting at x costs sum |p[i] - (x+i)|. Substituting q[i] = p[i] - i, the cost becomes sum |q[i] - x|, minimized when x is the median of q. Compute the median and sum absolute deviations in O(k).
