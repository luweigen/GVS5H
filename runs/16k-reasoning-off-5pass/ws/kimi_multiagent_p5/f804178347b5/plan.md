Model the process as a full ternary tree of height N: leaves are the input bits, and each internal node takes the majority of its three children. For each node, compute the minimum number of leaf flips in its subtree needed to make that node evaluate to 0 and to 1. For a leaf, cost is 0 for its own value and 1 for the opposite. For an internal node, the cost to achieve value v is the sum of the two smallest child costs for value v (since majority needs at least two of three children to be v). The answer is the root's cost for the opposite of its current value.
