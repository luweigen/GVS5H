The operation picks four consecutive pieces in sorted order (positions i..i+3) and reflects the middle two about the midpoint of the outer two; the outer two never move, and the multiset of gaps transforms in a structured way. Key observation: the operation preserves the alternating sum structure — equivalently, if we look at gaps d_k = x_{k+1} - x_k, the operation on indices i..i+3 swaps/transforms gaps so that the parity classes of gaps evolve independently. The sum of coordinates can be expressed as a linear combination of gaps, and the reachable configurations correspond to permuting gaps within parity classes (odd-indexed gaps and even-indexed gaps can each be arbitrarily rearranged). To minimize the total sum, we assign larger gaps to positions with smaller weights (weights decrease toward the right end), sorting each parity class of gaps appropriately. We then compute the minimal sum via prefix accumulation of sorted gap classes.
