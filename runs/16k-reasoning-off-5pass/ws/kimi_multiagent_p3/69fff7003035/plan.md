For each permutation, f(P) is the concatenation of the decimal strings of 1..N in permuted order. The contribution of a value v depends on its position: if elements with total digit-length L come after v, then v is multiplied by 10^L. For a fixed v, summing over all permutations, each other element u appears after v in exactly half the permutations, giving a factor (N-1)! * 10^{len(u)} per u, plus (N-1)! for v's own digits. So the total answer is (N-1)! * sum over v of v * (10^{len(v)} + sum_{u≠v} 10^{len(u)}). Group numbers by digit length (at most 6 groups for N ≤ 2·10^5) to compute the aggregate sums in O(number of digit groups), using fast modular exponentiation.
