Model the array as a sequence of runs: choose the first element (m ways), then each of the n-1 adjacent positions is either "same" (1 way) or "different" (m-1 ways). We need exactly k "same" positions among n-1 slots, giving answer = m * C(n-1, k) * (m-1)^(n-1-k) mod (1e9+7). Precompute factorials and inverse factorials up to n-1 for O(1) binomial queries, and use fast modular exponentiation. Handle edge cases: k=0 works naturally, and m=1 forces k=n-1 (formula yields 1 only then since 0^positive=0 and 0^0=1).
