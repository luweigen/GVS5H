Adjacent swaps mean the minimum operations to gather all 1s equals the number of inversions between the sequence of 1-positions and their target contiguous block. If the 1s are at positions p_1 < p_2 < ... < p_k, moving them to a contiguous block starting at position t costs sum |p_j - (t + j - 1)|. Define q_j = p_j - j; then the cost is sum |q_j - (t-1)|, minimized when t-1 is the median of the q values. So compute positions of 1s, transform to q_j, take the median, and sum absolute deviations — an O(N) or O(N log N) solution.
