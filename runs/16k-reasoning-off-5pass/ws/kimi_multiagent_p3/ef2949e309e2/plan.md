For each index i as the middle of the subsequence, we need to choose 2 elements from the left (indices < i) and 2 from the right (indices > i) such that nums[i] is the unique mode. Total ways to pick 2 left and 2 right is C(L,2)*C(R,2); subtract the "bad" selections where nums[i] is not the unique mode. Bad cases are limited: either some other value reaches frequency ≥2 (tie or beat), which can be counted via frequency maps of left/right values combined with combinatorics over counts (pairs of same value on each side, or one occurrence on each side). Use per-index left/right frequency maps (updated incrementally) and combinatorial identities to compute bad counts in O(distinct values) or better per index, giving roughly O(n * d) or O(n^2) total with n ≤ 1000. All arithmetic mod 1e9+7.
