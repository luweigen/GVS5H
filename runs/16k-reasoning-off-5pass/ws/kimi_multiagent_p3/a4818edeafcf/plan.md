We need max over i<j of D(prefix i) + D(middle i+1..j) + D(suffix j+1..N). Prefix distinct counts are trivially precomputed; suffix distinct counts likewise. The middle segment's distinct count as a function of (i,j) is the hard part. Key idea: fix the right cut j (or left cut i) and maintain a structure over the other cut. A cleaner approach: for each value, only its first/last occurrences matter per segment; use the classic trick of sweeping j from N-1 down while maintaining, over all i, the value D(prefix i) + D(middle i..j) via a segment tree with lazy propagation: when extending the middle leftward past an occurrence of value v, only the leftmost occurrence of v inside the middle contributes +1, so we add +1 to the range of i where this occurrence is the leftmost in the middle. Then answer = max over j of (suffix distinct after j) + max over i of seg[i]. Coordinate handling is O(N log N) overall.
