We need sum over all subarrays of length ≤ k of (min + max). Split into two independent problems: sum of maximums over all subarrays with length ≤ k, plus sum of minimums over all subarrays with length ≤ k. For each element nums[i], compute its contribution as a maximum: count of subarrays (length ≤ k) where it is the maximum, using monotonic stack to find previous greater-or-equal and next greater elements, then cap the left/right extension lengths so total window length ≤ k. The count of valid subarrays where element at i is the chosen max with left reach L and right reach R (bounded by k-1 total span) can be computed with a combinatorial formula summing over allowed left choices. Do the symmetric computation for minimums with a monotonic stack on reversed comparisons. Total is O(n) time and O(n) space, fitting n ≤ 80000.
