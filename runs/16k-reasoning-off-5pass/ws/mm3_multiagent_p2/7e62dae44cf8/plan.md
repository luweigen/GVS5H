We need the maximum total strength Takahashi can accumulate by repeatedly absorbing adjacent slimes whose strength is strictly less than his current strength divided by X. Since absorption only adds to his strength and never removes options, the optimal strategy is to absorb any reachable slime that satisfies the condition as soon as possible. This is equivalent to a BFS/DFS expansion where we can enter a cell only if its slime strength is < current_strength / X at the moment of absorption. Because strengths only increase, once a slime becomes absorbable it stays absorbable. We can simulate a multi-source BFS: start with Takahashi's initial strength, push his cell into a priority queue (max-heap) keyed by slime strength, and repeatedly try to absorb the strongest reachable slime that is less than current_strength / X. When we absorb it, we add its strength to current_strength and push its four neighbors (if not visited) into the heap. This greedy approach works because absorbing a stronger available slime first yields a larger current_strength sooner, unlocking more cells. Complexity is O(HW log(HW)).
