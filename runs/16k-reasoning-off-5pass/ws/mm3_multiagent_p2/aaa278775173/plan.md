We need to find the longest V-shaped diagonal segment that starts with 1, then alternates 2,0,2,0,... along a diagonal direction, optionally making one clockwise 90° turn to another diagonal direction while preserving the alternating sequence. Since the sequence is deterministic after the starting 1, we can precompute for every cell and every diagonal direction the maximum length of a valid segment that starts at that cell and proceeds in that direction without turning. Then for each cell we can consider the four possible clockwise turn pairs (e.g., SE→SW, SW→NW, NW→NE, NE→SE) and combine the precomputed lengths of the two legs (minus 1 for the shared turning cell) to get the total length of a V-shape. The answer is the maximum over all cells and all turn configurations, also considering straight segments (no turn). Complexity is O(n·m) per direction, overall O(n·m).
