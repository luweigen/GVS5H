The constraints (up to 10^9) mean we cannot iterate through all numbers. We must use digit dynamic programming (digit DP). The key state for our DP needs to track the current position, whether we are tight to the upper bound, the current sum of digits, and the current product of digits. However, the product of digits can become very large, making it an impractical state. Instead, we observe that for the product to be divisible by the sum, the sum of digits must be a divisor of the product. Since the maximum sum of digits for a 9-digit number is 9*9=81, we can iterate over all possible sums S (from 1 to 81). For each fixed sum S, we count how many numbers have digit sum S and digit product divisible by S.
