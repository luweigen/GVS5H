The problem asks for the maximum XOR sum of exactly K distinct elements from a sequence A. The critical constraint is that the number of ways to choose K elements, $\binom{N}{K}$, is at most $10^6$. This small search space suggests that we can iterate through all possible combinations of K elements. We will generate all combinations of indices of length K, calculate the XOR sum for each combination, and track the maximum value found. Given the constraints and the nature of XOR operations, this brute-force approach over combinations is feasible and optimal under the given condition.
