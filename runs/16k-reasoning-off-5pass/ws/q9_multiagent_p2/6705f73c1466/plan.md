The problem asks for the minimum number of swaps to make all '1's contiguous. This is equivalent to finding a target position for the block of '1's such that the total distance each '1' needs to move to reach its spot in that block is minimized. Since one swap can reduce the total distance by at most 1 (by moving a '1' one step closer to its target), the minimum number of swaps equals the sum of distances. We can iterate through all possible starting positions for the contiguous block of '1's (from index 0 to N-count_ones), calculate the cost for each, and take the minimum.
