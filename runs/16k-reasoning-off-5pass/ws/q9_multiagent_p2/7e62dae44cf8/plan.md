The problem asks for the maximum strength Takahashi can achieve by absorbing adjacent slimes that are strictly less than $S/X$ of his current strength. Since absorbing a slime increases Takahashi's strength, it might enable him to absorb larger slimes later. This suggests a greedy approach: always absorb the smallest available valid neighbor to maximize the potential for future absorptions. We can simulate this process using a priority queue to efficiently retrieve the smallest valid neighbor at each step. The simulation continues until no valid moves remain.
