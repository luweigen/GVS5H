The core strategy involves counting the frequency of each character in the string. We then collect all unique frequencies present and sort them. For each unique frequency, we calculate the cost to either delete all characters with that frequency (cost = count of chars * freq) or change them to match another target frequency (cost = count of chars * abs(freq - target)). We also consider the option of deleting all characters with a specific frequency entirely. The optimal solution is the minimum cost across all possible target frequencies (including 0, which means deleting everything).
