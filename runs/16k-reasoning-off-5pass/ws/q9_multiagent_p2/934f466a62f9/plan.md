The problem asks us to select $K$ disjoint pairs of cakes to maximize the sum of their prices, where the price of a pair is the maximum of the sums of their respective beauty, tastiness, and popularity values. Since the price of a pair is determined by exactly one of the three attributes (beauty, tastiness, or popularity), we can iterate through each attribute separately. For a fixed attribute (say beauty), we want to pair up cakes such that the sum of the beauty values of the pairs is maximized. This is equivalent to pairing the largest available beauty values together. We calculate the maximum possible total price for each of the three attributes independently and take the maximum of these three results as the final answer.
