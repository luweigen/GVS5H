The problem asks for the minimum number of vertices to delete to leave a "Snowflake Tree". A Snowflake Tree consists of a central root, $x$ children connected directly to it, and each of those $x$ children having exactly $y$ leaf nodes attached. The strategy is to iterate through every vertex in the original tree, treating it as the potential central root of the Snowflake Tree. For each candidate root, we calculate the maximum number of vertices we can keep by checking if the tree structure around it matches the Snowflake pattern (specifically, the degrees of neighbors and the sizes of their subtrees). The answer will be $N$ minus the maximum kept vertices found across all candidates.
