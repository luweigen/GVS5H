We need to flip exactly the set S of positions where A_i != B_i, in some order; each operation's cost is dot(A_after_flip, C). Total cost = sum over flips of (base + contributions toggled). Rewrite: cost of flipping i when current state is x equals sum_k x_k C_k after toggling bit i. Total = sum over each flip of (current dot C) ± C_i. Summing, each position k contributes C_k times the number of operations performed while A_k = 1 (after that operation's flip). This becomes: we choose an ordering of S; total = sum over steps of dot(state, C). Equivalent formulation: total = sum_{i in S} [dot(initial A, C) adjustments] — better to think of it as: each flip of i changes the "running dot" by ±C_i, and we pay the running dot after each flip. So total = sum over flips of running dot after flip = m * D0 + sum over flips of (sum of deltas up to and including that flip), where D0 = initial dot, m = |S|, delta = +C_i if flipping 0->1 else -C_i. Minimizing over order: total = m*D0 + sum_{j=1..m} (m - j + 1) * delta_{(j)}. To minimize, sort deltas ascending (most negative first) — by rearrangement inequality, pair largest weight (m) with smallest delta. Then compute answer directly.
