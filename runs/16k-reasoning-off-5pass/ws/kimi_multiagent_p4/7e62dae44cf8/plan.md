This is a classic "expanding frontier" problem: Takahashi occupies a growing connected region, and any slime adjacent to the region with strength strictly less than current_strength / X can be absorbed, increasing his strength. Since absorbing only ever increases strength (never hurts), the optimal strategy is greedy: always absorb any absorbable adjacent slime. Use a max-heap keyed on slime strength (or a min-heap on the threshold) over the frontier; repeatedly absorb the strongest absorbable slime (equivalently, all slimes with S < cur/X, i.e., S*X < cur). To avoid overflow with X up to 1e9 and S up to 1e12, compare using the condition S * X < cur carefully with Python big ints (fine) or cur // X > S style logic. BFS/heap expansion over at most 250k cells is easily fast enough.
