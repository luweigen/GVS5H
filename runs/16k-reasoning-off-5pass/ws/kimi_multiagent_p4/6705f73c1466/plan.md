Adjacent swaps only reorder by moving characters past each other; since all 1s are identical, the problem reduces to gathering the k ones into a contiguous block with minimum total movement. If the one-positions are p[0..k-1], the minimum adjacent swaps to make them consecutive equals the minimum over target blocks of sum |p[i] - (t+i)|, which is minimized at the median of q[i] = p[i] - i. Compute q, take its median, and sum absolute deviations. This is O(k log k) (or O(k) with nth_element-style selection), fine for N up to 5e5.
