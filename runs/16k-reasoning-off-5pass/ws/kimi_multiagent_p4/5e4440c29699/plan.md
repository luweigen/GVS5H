The blocks form a rectangle [0,W]×[0,H] with a rectangular hole (L..R)×(D..U) removed. Since moves are only +x or +y, paths are monotone, so the number of paths ending at each point satisfies a simple DP: f(p)=1+sum of f over predecessors, and the answer is the sum of f over all blocks. The hole forces us to compute this DP over up to four rectangular regions (below the hole, left of it, right of it, above it), where each region's boundary values come from previously computed regions. Using the identity that summing binomial-weighted boundary values over a rectangle yields closed forms with binomial coefficients, we can compute each region's contribution in O(1)–O(n) with precomputed factorials up to W+H+2.
