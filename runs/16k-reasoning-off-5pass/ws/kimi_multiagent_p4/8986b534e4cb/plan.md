Model each person as an interval constraint on prefix sums: let P[0..N-1] be the prefix sums of road weights; a person from S to T requires P[S-1]=P[T-1]=0 and all intermediate prefix sums strictly positive (if S<T) or strictly negative (if S>T). This becomes a system of difference constraints (equalities and strict inequalities) on the P values; feasibility is checkable via detecting contradictions (positive cycles) in a constraint graph. For range queries, use offline processing (e.g., Mo's algorithm or segment-tree divide and conquer) maintaining a DSU with parity/potentials plus a check that no strict-inequality edge forces a cycle, since constraints are of the form P[v] >= P[u] + 1 or equality. Key insight: feasibility reduces to no equality-connected component containing a strict lower-bound cycle, which can be maintained incrementally with rollback DSU over a segment tree of time intervals.
