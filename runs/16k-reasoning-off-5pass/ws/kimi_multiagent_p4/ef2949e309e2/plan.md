For each index i as the middle element, count pairs (a,b) from the left side and (c,d) from the right side such that nums[i] is the unique mode of the 5-element subsequence. The count of nums[i] in the subsequence is 1 + (number of chosen left/right elements equal to nums[i]), which can be 1, 2, or 3; for each case, count ways to pick the remaining elements so no other value reaches that frequency. Use per-index left/right frequency maps and combinatorial precomputations (total pairs, pairs sharing a value, pairs equal to a specific value) to compute each case in O(1)–O(k) per index, giving overall O(n²) or better. Sum over all middle indices modulo 1e9+7.
