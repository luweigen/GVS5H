We need to simulate Santa's axis-aligned moves, tracking his final position and counting distinct houses visited. Since each house lies at integer coordinates, a house is visited if it lies on the line segment of a move (including endpoints). For a horizontal move (L/R) at fixed y, we need all houses with that y and x in the swept range; for a vertical move (U/D) at fixed x, we need all houses with that x and y in the swept range.

To answer efficiently, we group houses by their y-coordinate (for horizontal queries) and by their x-coordinate (for vertical queries). For each group we keep a sorted list of (coordinate, house_id) pairs. During a move, we binary search the relevant group to find the slice of houses within the interval, then process each house in that slice. To avoid double counting and repeated scans, we remove the house from both its x-group and y-group the first time it is encountered. Each house is removed at most once, so total work over all moves is O((N+M) log N).
