We sort the pieces (they are already sorted). The operation on indices i..i+3 reflects the two middle pieces across the midpoint of the outer two. This is exactly the "fold" operation that preserves the multiset of distances from the outer pair: after the operation, the four pieces occupy positions symmetric around M, and the sum of the four coordinates equals the sum of the outer two (since the two reflected pieces cancel out). Therefore the total sum of all N pieces is invariant under any operation. The minimum sum is simply the initial sum.
