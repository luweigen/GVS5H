We model the graph where moving along an edge costs 1 and reversing the entire graph costs X. The state alternates between original graph and reversed graph; we can think of two layered BFS/Dijkstra: layer 0 = original orientation, layer 1 = reversed orientation. Edges: from (v,0) to (u,1) with cost 1 (original edge v→u becomes u→v after reversal), from (v,1) to (u,0) with cost 1, and toggle (v,0)→(v,1) and (v,1)→(v,0) with cost X. Since costs are 1 or X, we can use 0-1 BFS or Dijkstra. N up to 2e5, M up to 2e5, so we use deque-based 0-1 BFS by splitting into two weight classes: weight 1 pushed to back of appropriate deque, weight X pushed to front if X<1? Actually we use Dijkstra with priority queue for safety with large X.
