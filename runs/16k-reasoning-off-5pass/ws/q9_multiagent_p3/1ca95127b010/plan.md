The operations allow swapping a block of $X$ zeros followed by $Y$ ones with a block of $Y$ zeros followed by $X$ ones, effectively moving the boundary between 0s and 1s. Since the total number of 1s is invariant, the first step is to check if $S$ and $T$ have the same count of 1s. If they do, we can simulate the process by greedily matching the first differing position from left to right. If $S[i] \neq T[i]$, we attempt to apply the necessary operation (A or B) at this position to flip the required segment. If the required pattern doesn't exist or we reach the end without matching, the answer is "No".
