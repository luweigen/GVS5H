The problem requires transforming the frequency distribution of characters in string `s` such that all present characters have the same frequency. Since we can delete, insert, or change characters (cost 1 per operation), the optimal strategy involves identifying a target frequency `k` that minimizes the total operations. For each possible target frequency `k` (from 0 up to the maximum frequency in `s`), we calculate the cost: for characters with frequency `f > k`, we must delete `f - k` characters; for characters with frequency `0 < f < k`, we must change `k - f` characters to reach frequency `k`. Characters with frequency 0 do not need to be changed unless we are increasing their count, but since we only care about existing characters having equal frequency, we effectively choose a `k` and adjust existing counts to `k` (deleting excess or changing deficits). The answer is the minimum cost over all valid `k`.
