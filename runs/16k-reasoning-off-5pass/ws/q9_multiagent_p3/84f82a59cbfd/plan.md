The problem requires finding the largest integer $\le A$ that has exactly two distinct prime factors, where each prime factor appears with an even exponent. This structure implies the number must be of the form $p^{2k} \cdot q^{2m} = (p^k \cdot q^m)^2$, meaning the number is a perfect square of a composite number with exactly two distinct prime factors. We can precompute all such "base" numbers (numbers with exactly two distinct prime factors) up to $\sqrt{10^{12}} = 10^6$, square them, and store the results in a sorted list. For each query $A$, we use binary search (specifically `bisect_right`) on this precomputed list to find the largest value $\le A$.
