The problem asks to minimize the sum of coordinates by repeatedly applying an operation that reflects the middle two pieces of a 4-piece window across the midpoint of the outer two. This operation reduces the sum if the sum of the outer pieces is less than the sum of the inner pieces. We can model this as a greedy process where we always perform the operation that yields the largest reduction in the total sum. We use a priority queue to efficiently select the best operation at each step. After each operation, the array of coordinates changes, so we must re-sort the array to maintain the correct indexing for subsequent operations. We also need to handle "stale" operations in the priority queue by re-evaluating the reduction amount before applying an operation. The process continues until no more operations can reduce the sum.
