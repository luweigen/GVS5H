The problem asks for the maximum strength Takahashi can achieve by absorbing adjacent slimes that are strictly less than $S/X$ of his current strength. Since absorbing a slime increases Takahashi's strength, this creates a dependency where stronger slimes become absorbable only after gaining enough strength from weaker ones. This suggests a greedy approach: repeatedly find the smallest available adjacent slime that satisfies the condition, absorb it, and update the grid and adjacency. To efficiently manage the set of available slimes and their current adjacency status, we can use a priority queue to store candidates and a visited array to prevent re-processing. We simulate the process until no more moves are possible.
