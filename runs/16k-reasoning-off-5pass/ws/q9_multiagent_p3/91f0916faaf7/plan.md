The problem asks for the sum of scores (product of elements) of all "good sequences" $S$ where adjacent ratios $S_i/S_{i+1}$ have a specific numerator-denominator product $A_i$. We can model this using dynamic programming where the state tracks the prime factorization of the current term $S_i$. Since $A_i$ are small ($\le 1000$), the prime factors involved are limited to primes $\le 1000$. The key insight is that the contribution of each prime factor to the total sum can be calculated independently. For each prime $p$, we determine the possible exponents of $p$ in $S_i$ based on the constraints imposed by $A_i$, and sum the geometric series contributions. Finally, we multiply the results for all primes modulo 998244353.
