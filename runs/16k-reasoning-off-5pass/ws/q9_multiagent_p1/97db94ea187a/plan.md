The problem asks for the count of connected graphs where the bipartition sizes induced by BFS layers from vertex 1 are equal. Since $N$ is small ($N \le 30$), we can iterate over all possible bipartitions $(S, \bar{S})$ of the vertices where $|S| = | \bar{S} | = N/2$. For a fixed bipartition, we count graphs where all edges go between $S$ and $\bar{S}$ (making it bipartite) and are connected. Then, we must subtract cases where the BFS layering from vertex 1 does not match the fixed bipartition (i.e., the graph is bipartite but the specific "even/odd" distance sets differ from $S/\bar{S}$). This requires careful inclusion-exclusion or direct counting based on the structure of bipartite connected graphs and their specific BFS layerings. We will precompute binomial coefficients and powers modulo $P$, then sum the valid configurations for each edge count $M$.
