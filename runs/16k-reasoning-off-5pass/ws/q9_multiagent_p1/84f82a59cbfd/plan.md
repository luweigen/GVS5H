The problem requires finding the largest integer $N \le A$ such that $N$ has exactly two distinct prime factors $p, q$ and the exponents of both in the prime factorization of $N$ are even. This implies $N = p^{2k} \cdot q^{2m} = (p^k \cdot q^m)^2$, meaning $N$ must be a perfect square of a number with exactly two distinct prime factors. We can precompute all such base numbers $X = p^k \cdot q^m$ up to $\sqrt{10^{12}} = 10^6$ and store their squares in a sorted list. For each query $A$, we perform a binary search on this list to find the largest square $\le A$.
