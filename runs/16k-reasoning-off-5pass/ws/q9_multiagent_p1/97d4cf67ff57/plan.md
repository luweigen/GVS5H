The problem asks for the maximum size of a subgraph of a given tree that satisfies the "alkane" property: every vertex has degree 1 or 4, and at least one vertex has degree 4. This structure implies the subgraph consists of a central core of degree-4 vertices connected by paths of degree-2 vertices (which must be removed) or directly connected to degree-1 leaves. Effectively, we are looking for a connected subgraph where internal nodes have degree 4 and leaves have degree 1. We can model this by rooting the tree arbitrarily and using dynamic programming. For each node, we calculate the maximum size of a valid alkane component within its subtree, considering whether the node acts as a leaf (degree 1 in subgraph) or an internal node (degree 4 in subgraph). Since a valid alkane must have at least one degree-4 node, we will track the best solution found globally that satisfies this condition.
