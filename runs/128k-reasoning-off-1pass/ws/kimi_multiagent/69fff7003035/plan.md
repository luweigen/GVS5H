For each value v (1..N), compute its contribution to the total sum: v appears in every position of the permutation equally often, and its decimal string is shifted right by the total digit-length of all elements placed after it. By symmetry, the expected total length of elements after v is ((N-1)/2) * (totalDigits - len(v)), so v's total contribution is (N-1)! * v * 10^{that exponent}. Sum over all v modulo 998244353. Precompute digit-length groups (1-9, 10-99, ...) to aggregate v * 10^{-c*len(v)} per group using geometric series of 10^{-c} over each group's range, with modular inverses. All arithmetic is O(log N) per group, giving O(log N) total.
