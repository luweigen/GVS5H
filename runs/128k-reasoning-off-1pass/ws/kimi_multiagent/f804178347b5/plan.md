Model the computation as a ternary tree: each internal node takes the majority of its 3 children, leaves are the input bits. For each node compute two DP values: the minimum leaf flips needed to force that node's value to 0 and to 1. For a leaf, cost is 0 to keep its own bit and 1 to flip it. For an internal node, forcing value v requires at least 2 of its 3 children to be v, so take the two smallest child costs for v and sum them. The answer at the root is the cost to force the opposite of its current value. N ≤ 13 means up to ~1.6M leaves, so use an iterative bottom-up pass with O(1) extra memory beyond the array.
