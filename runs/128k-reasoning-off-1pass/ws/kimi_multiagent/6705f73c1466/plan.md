Adjacent swaps that gather all 1s into one block are equivalent to moving each 1 to a distinct consecutive target position; the minimum cost is the sum of distances between the sorted original indices of the 1s and the sorted target indices. The optimal target block is anchored at the median of the 1-positions (any median works for even counts). So: collect positions of 1s, subtract their rank to "de-gap" them, take the median, and sum absolute deviations. This is O(N) time and O(K) memory where K is the number of 1s.
