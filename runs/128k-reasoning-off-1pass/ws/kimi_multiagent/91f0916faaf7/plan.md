Each constraint f(S_i/S_{i+1}) = A_i means: writing A_i = p_i·q_i in coprime terms, S_i/S_{i+1} reduces to p_i/q_i, so S_i : S_{i+1} = p_i : q_i. Chaining these ratios fixes S up to a single scale factor t: S_i = t·c_i where c_i are determined minimal integers from the ratios. The gcd condition forces t = 1 / gcd(c_1..c_N)... actually t must make all entries integers with overall gcd 1, giving finitely many t (divisors related to gcd of c_i). Then sum of scores = sum over valid t of t^N · ∏c_i, computable via divisor enumeration and prime factorization of the gcd structure. Since A_i ≤ 1000, factorization is cheap; the scale factor t ranges over divisors of a bounded quantity.
