Model the valid parenthesis sequence as an ordered rooted tree (forest of top-level components; each pair of matched parens is a node whose children are the components inside it). The flip-reverse operation on a valid substring corresponds to reversing the order of children of a node (or the order of the top-level forest), so any permutation of sibling subtrees is achievable. Two strings are reachable from each other iff their trees are isomorphic as unordered rooted trees, so the answer is the number of distinct ordered trees obtainable by permuting siblings — i.e., the product over all nodes of (number of distinct permutations of its children multiset). Compute canonical hashes of subtrees to group identical children, then multiply factorials of group sizes modulo 998244353.
