- **Model.** For boundary `k` (between positions `k` and `k+1`), let `c_k = #{j > k : P_j ≤ k} = #{j ≤ k : P_j > k}`. Any sorting sequence needs at least `c_k` swaps across boundary `k` (each swap moves exactly one element left across it), giving the lower bound `base = Σ k·c_k`. This bound is achievable per boundary by routing each element directly to its final position (paths are contiguous, so element `v` at position `j > v` must cross every boundary in `[v, j-1]`).
- **Parity constraint.** The total number of swaps always has the same parity as `inv(P)`. Since `Σ x_k` (x_k = swaps at boundary k) must satisfy `Σ x_k ≡ inv (mod 2)`, if `Σ c_k ≢ inv (mod 2)` we need an odd number of extra crossings. One extra crossing at boundary `k` (`x_k = c_k + 1`) is feasible iff `c_k ≥ 1`: a value `> k` sitting left of `k` does a round trip (crosses right extra, left extra), with its extra leftward crossing paired against a mandatory rightward crossing of another `>k` value, and its extra rightward crossing paired against a mandatory leftward crossing of a `≤k` value.
