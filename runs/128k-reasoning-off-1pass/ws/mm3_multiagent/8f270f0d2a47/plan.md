A number is "beautiful" if the product of its digits is divisible by the sum of its digits. A key observation is that for any multi-digit number, the product of its digits is divisible by the sum of its digits **if and only if at least one digit is 0**. This is because: (1) any digit 0 makes the product 0, which is divisible by any non-zero sum, and (2) for numbers without any zero digit, the product is less than the sum (since each digit contributes at most 1 to the product but contributes its full value to the sum, with equality only for single digits). Single-digit numbers (1-9) are all beautiful since product equals the number equals the sum.

So the solution is: count numbers from l to r that either (a) are single-digit (1-9), or (b) contain at least one zero digit.
