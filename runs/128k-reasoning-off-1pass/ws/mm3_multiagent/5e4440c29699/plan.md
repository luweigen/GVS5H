We need to count all monotone paths on a rectangular grid with a rectangular hole (L ≤ x ≤ R and D ≤ y ≤ U) removed. The number of allowed lattice points is (W+1)(H+1) minus the hole area; paths are any non-empty sequence of steps (right or up) staying within allowed points. This equals total number of monotone walks on the full rectangle minus walks that enter or pass through the hole. Using inclusion–exclusion, the answer is total walks − walks that visit the hole. Walks that visit the hole equal walks to (L,D) times walks from (R,U) to (W,H), summed over the hole’s corner. Modulo 998244353, factorials and inverse factorials up to 2·10⁶ give O(1) combination queries.
