The game ends exactly when total sum of A becomes 0 after a move (since each move reduces sum by 1, and the set S automatically becomes full at that moment). The total number of moves is `total = sum(A)`. Since players alternate starting with Fennec, the winner is determined by parity: Fennec wins iff `total` is odd, else Snuke wins. This is independent of any strategy because moves are forced to exist until sum hits 0.
