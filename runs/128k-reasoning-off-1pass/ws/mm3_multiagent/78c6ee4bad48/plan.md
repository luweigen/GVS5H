The operation is a "fold" over the midpoint of pieces i and i+3: pieces i+1 and i+2 reflect over M. This is equivalent to choosing any index j (1 ≤ j ≤ N-3) and replacing the pair (X_{j+1}, X_{j+2}) with their reflection across the midpoint of X_j and X_{j+3}, i.e., new positions become (X_j + X_{j+3} - X_{j+2}, X_j + X_{j+3} - X_{j+1}) (in sorted order). Crucially, the sum of the four involved pieces remains unchanged: X_j + X_{j+1} + X_{j+2} + X_{j+3} stays the same. Therefore the total sum over all N pieces is invariant under all operations. So the answer is simply the initial sum of all coordinates.
