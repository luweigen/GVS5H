The constraint $\binom{N}{K} \le 10^6$ is the key, implying that even though $N$ can be up to $2 \times 10^5$, the number of ways to choose $K$ elements is small enough to iterate through all combinations. We will generate all possible combinations of $K$ indices from the array $A$, calculate the XOR sum for each combination, and track the maximum value found. Given the constraints and the nature of XOR, a brute-force approach over the combinations is efficient enough.
