The problem requires finding the largest integer $\le A$ that has exactly two distinct prime factors, both appearing with an even exponent. This structure implies the number is of the form $p^{2k} \cdot q^{2m} = (p^k \cdot q^m)^2$, meaning the answer must be a perfect square of a number with exactly two distinct prime factors. We can precompute all such base numbers (integers with exactly two distinct prime factors) up to $\sqrt{10^{12}} = 10^6$. Since $Q$ is large, we will store these valid squares in a sorted list and use binary search (`bisect_right`) to efficiently find the largest valid square $\le A$ for each query.
