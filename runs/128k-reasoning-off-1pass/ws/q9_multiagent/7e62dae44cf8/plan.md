This problem can be modeled as finding the maximum weight reachable in a state space where the state is defined by Takahashi's current strength and his current position. Since Takahashi can only absorb slimes with strength strictly less than `current_strength / X`, the condition becomes easier to satisfy as his strength increases. We can use a priority queue (max-heap) to always attempt to absorb the strongest available slime that satisfies the condition, starting from the initial position. To avoid revisiting cells unnecessarily, we track the maximum strength achieved at each cell; if we reach a cell with a strength lower than or equal to a previous visit, we skip it. The process continues until no more moves are possible.
