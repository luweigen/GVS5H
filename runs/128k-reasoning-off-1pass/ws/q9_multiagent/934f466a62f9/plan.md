The problem asks us to select $K$ disjoint pairs of cakes to maximize the sum of their prices, where the price of a pair is the maximum of the sum of their beauty, tastiness, or popularity values. Since the price of a pair is determined by exactly one of the three attributes (beauty, tastiness, or popularity), we can iterate through each attribute as the "dominant" attribute for the pairs. For a fixed dominant attribute (say, beauty), the problem reduces to finding $K$ disjoint pairs such that the sum of the sums of their beauty values is maximized, which is equivalent to selecting the $2K$ cakes with the highest beauty values and pairing them arbitrarily. We calculate the maximum total price for each of the three attributes independently and take the maximum of these three results.
